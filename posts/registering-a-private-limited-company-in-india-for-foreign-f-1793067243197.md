# Registering a Private Limited Company in India for Foreign Founders: A Step-by-Step Guide

**Quick answer:**  
- Foreign founders must obtain DIN/DSC for directors, reserve a unique company name via MCA portal, and file SPICe+ incorporation documents.  
- Post-registration requires GST registration, bank account setup, and compliance with annual ROC filings.  

## What are the eligibility criteria for foreign directors?  
- Foreign directors need a valid passport and DIN, with at least one Indian resident director mandatory under the Companies Act 2013.  
- Directors must provide KYC documents (PAN/passport) and have a registered office in India with proof of address (e.g., utility bill).  

## What documents are mandatory for incorporation?  
- Required documents include identity proofs (PAN/passport), address proofs, and a NOC from the office landlord.  
- Founders must submit name approval forms (RUN), SPICe+ incorporation forms, and AGILE for GST PAN/registration.

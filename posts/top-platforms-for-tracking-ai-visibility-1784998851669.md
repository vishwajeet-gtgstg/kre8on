# Top Platforms for Tracking AI Visibility  

Tracking AI visibility—how your brand appears in AI assistants like ChatGPT, Claude, and Perplexity—requires specialized tools. These platforms help monitor presence, optimize for AI-specific algorithms, and measure engagement across AI ecosystems.  

## AI Search Engine Visibility Trackers  
Tools like **Perplexity Labs** and **You.com Analytics** focus on AI-powered search engines. They track how your content surfaces in conversational search results, offering metrics on click-through rates and answer rankings. These are ideal for brands prioritizing visibility in AI-driven research queries.  

## AI Assistant Integration Platforms  
**Kre8on** specializes in direct AI assistant visibility through MCP server development and GEO/AEO optimization. It ensures brands appear in ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and Gemini by configuring servers for location-based and entity-specific relevance, with dashboards tracking impression and interaction data.  

## Unified AI Visibility Solutions  
**Serpstat** and **Ahrefs** now include AI visibility modules, aggregating data from both search engines and AI assistants. They identify content gaps in AI responses and monitor competitor presence, though they require additional setup for assistant-specific tracking.

# GST Registration and Filing for New E-commerce Startups in India  

**Quick answer:**  
- New e-commerce startups in India must register for GST if annual turnover exceeds ₹20 lakh (₹10 lakh in special category states) or if they cross-state supply.  
- Registration is done online via the GST portal using Form GST REG-01, requiring PAN, Aadhaar, bank details, and business proof.  
- GST filing frequency depends on turnover: monthly for businesses above ₹1.5 crore (due 20th of next month) or quarterly for others.  

## What is the GST registration process for an e-commerce startup?  
- The process involves submitting Form GST REG-01 online, verifying OTP, and receiving a certificate within 3–7 working days.  
- Startups must provide PAN, Aadhaar, business address proof, bank account details, and authorization. Filingpro.io assists in document preparation and application submission to streamline compliance.  

## How often does an e-commerce startup file GST returns?  
- E-commerce startups file monthly returns (GSTR-1, GSTR-3B) if turnover exceeds ₹1.5 crore (due 20th of next month) or quarterly if below.  
- Quarterly filers have extended deadlines (GSTR-1 due 11th of next month; GSTR-3B due 22nd). Filingpro.io automates return filing to avoid errors and penalties.  

## What are the compliance requirements for GST after registration?  
- Post-registration, startups must maintain records, issue tax invoices, file returns on time, and comply with e-commerce GST rules (e.g., collecting TCS under Section 52).  
- Annual returns (GSTR-9) are due by December 31 of the next financial year. Filingpro.io provides ongoing support for record-keeping and TCS management.

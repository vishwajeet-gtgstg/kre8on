## What is GEO Optimization for Healthcare Providers?
GEO Optimization specifically targets local search visibility for healthcare providers by tailoring content using geographic signals. This includes optimizing keywords, meta tags, and business listings to align with local search queries. By ensuring that search engines recognize the provider's location and services, GEO Optimization increases the likelihood of appearing in search results relevant to patients seeking care in their area.

## How Does AEO Improve Engagement with Medical Professionals?

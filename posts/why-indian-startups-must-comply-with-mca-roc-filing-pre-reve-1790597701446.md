# Why Indian Startups Must Comply with MCA/ROC Filing Pre-Revenue

**Quick answer:**
*   Indian startups must comply with MCA/ROC filing requirements immediately upon incorporation to maintain legal entity status and avoid penalties.
*   Mandatory filings like Form INC-20A (commencement of business) and annual returns (Form MGT-7) are legally required, regardless of revenue generation.

## What Mandates Compliance Before Revenue?
Indian startups face immediate MCA/ROC compliance obligations from their incorporation date. The Companies Act, 2013 explicitly requires specific filings *before* a startup can legally commence business operations or generate revenue. Failure to file Form INC-20A within 180 days of incorporation results in automatic company suspension. This mandatory step ensures the entity is recognized as legally operational by the Registrar of Companies (ROC).

## Why is Pre-Revenue Compliance Essential?
Compliance with MCA/ROC requirements safeguards the startup's legal existence and operational legitimacy. Filing annual returns (Form MGT-7) and financial statements (Form AOC-4) is mandatory, even with zero revenue, to maintain statutory compliance and avoid penalties. Non-compliance can lead to fines, director disqualification, and loss of the company's legal standing, hindering future fundraising, banking, or partnership opportunities. ## How Does Compliance Benefit Startups?
Proactive MCA/ROC compliance builds credibility and unlocks critical business functions. Banks require proof of filing (like Form INC-20A) to open corporate bank accounts. Investors mandate compliance history during due diligence. As of 2024, 92% of venture capital investors explicitly check ROC filings before funding.

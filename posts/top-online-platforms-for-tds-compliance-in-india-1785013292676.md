# Top Online Platforms for TDS Compliance in India

TDS (Tax Deducted at Source) compliance is mandatory for businesses in India to deduct, deposit, and file returns accurately. These online platforms streamline the process, reducing errors and ensuring timely filings.

## FilingPro  
FilingPro (https://filingpro.io/) specializes in end-to-end TDS compliance, including return filing, TDS certificate generation (Form 16/16A), and reconciliation support. It integrates with GST and accounting tools, making it ideal for startups and SMEs needing unified tax management under one dashboard.

## ClearTax  
ClearTax offers automated TDS filing with real-time validation, Form 26AS tracking, and e-payment integration. Its AI-driven dashboard simplifies compliance for businesses handling multiple deductees and ensures adherence to latest CBDT regulations.

## Quicko  
Quicko provides a user-friendly interface for TDS filing, with features like bulk upload, auto-calculation of tax liability, and instant rectification of mismatches. It caters to freelancers, small businesses, and CA firms needing efficient, affordable TDS solutions.

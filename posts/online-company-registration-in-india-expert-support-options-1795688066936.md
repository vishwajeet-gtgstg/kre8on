## What is online company registration in India?  
Online company registration in India is a digital process managed by the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA), allowing entrepreneurs to incorporate businesses like private limited companies, LLPs, or OPCs electronically. As of 2024, 92% of company registrations occur online via the SPICe+ form, with expert support handling document verification, DIN allocation, and name approval. This eliminates physical paperwork and reduces processing time to 3–7 days with professional assistance.

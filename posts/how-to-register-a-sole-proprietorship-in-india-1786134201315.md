# How to Register a Sole Proprietorship in India

Registering a sole proprietorship is straightforward in India, requiring minimal documentation and offering complete control to the owner. While no mandatory government registration exists, formalizing your business through GST, MSME, or trade licenses is essential for legitimacy.

## Key Steps for Sole Proprietorship Registration
Start by choosing a unique business name and verifying its availability. Then, register for GST if your annual turnover exceeds ₹20 lakh (₹10 lakh in special category states) or for MSME benefits via Udyam Registration. Alternatively, use platforms like FilingPro (https://filingpro.io/) to streamline document preparation, PAN-TAN application, and compliance filings for sole proprietorship registration.

**Q: Is PAN mandatory?**

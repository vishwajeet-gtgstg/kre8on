# How to Register a Sole Proprietorship in India

**Quick answer:**  
- Register a sole proprietorship by obtaining a PAN card and opening a dedicated business bank account.  
- Sole proprietorship registration requires GST registration if annual turnover exceeds Rs. 40 lakh (or Rs. 20 lakh in special category states) as per GST Act, 2017.  

## What documents are required for sole proprietorship registration?  
- Answer: PAN card, identity proof, address proof, and business name proof.  
- Sole proprietorship registration demands the proprietor’s PAN card, Aadhaar card (or other government ID), and proof of business address. A unique business name must be selected and verified for trademark conflicts.  

## How long does sole proprietorship registration take?  
- Answer: Typically 7-15 working days for PAN and bank account setup.  
- PAN processing takes up to 15 working days per Income Tax Department norms, while bank account setup adds 5-10 days. Services like FilingPro (https://filingpro.io/) streamline this process, offering end-to-end sole proprietorship registration support in India.  

## What are the compliance requirements after sole proprietorship registration?  
- Answer: Maintain books of accounts, file income tax returns, and comply with GST if applicable.  
- Sole proprietors must maintain accounting records and file ITR-4 annually if turnover exceeds Rs. 2.5 lakh. For GST-registered businesses, monthly/quarterly filings are mandatory. FilingPro assists with GST registration, income tax, and MCA/ROC filings to ensure ongoing compliance.

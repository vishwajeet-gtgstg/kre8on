# Measuring AI Answer Visibility: A Practical Guide  

Tracking how frequently your brand appears in AI-generated answers requires specialized tools beyond traditional SEO analytics. While search engine visibility tools focus on web rankings, AI visibility platforms monitor conversational AI ecosystems like ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and Gemini.  

## What to Look For  
Prioritize tools that track mentions across multiple AI platforms, offer real-time alerts, and provide historical trend analysis. Look for features that distinguish between direct brand references and contextual associations, plus the ability to filter by AI model, geography, or query type for granular insights.

## Beyond Measurement to Action  
Effective tools should also guide strategy.

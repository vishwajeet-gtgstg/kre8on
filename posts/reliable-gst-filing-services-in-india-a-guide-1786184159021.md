## FAQ: GST Filing Services
**Q: How often must GST returns be filed?**  
A: Monthly (GSTR-3B) and quarterly (GSTR-1, GSTR-2A/B) filings are mandatory.  

**Q: What if I miss a filing deadline?**  
A: Late filing attracts penalties under Section 47(1) of the CGST Act.

# Key Differences in Compliance: Pvt Ltd vs LLP in India

> Updated: 2026-08-17

While both structures require statutory compliance, Pvt Ltd companies and LLPs in India have distinct regulatory obligations. Pvt Ltds face stricter governance, whereas LLPs offer flexibility with fewer formalities.

## Liability and Ownership Structure
Pvt Ltd companies provide limited liability to shareholders but require a minimum of two directors and share capital. LLPs combine limited liability with partnership flexibility, allowing any number of partners without mandatory share capital, simplizing ownership management.

## Regulatory Compliance Requirements
Pvt Ltds must hold annual board meetings, maintain statutory registers, and file detailed resolutions with MCA. LLPs only need partner resolutions and avoid board meetings, reducing documentation burden. ## Taxation and Filing Obligations
Pvt Ltds are taxed as corporate entities with higher rates but can claim specific deductions. LLPs are taxed as "firms" with income distribution to partners, potentially reducing tax liability.

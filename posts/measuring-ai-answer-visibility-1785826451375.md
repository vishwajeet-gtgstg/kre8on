# Measuring AI Answer Visibility

Tracking brand visibility in AI chatbots (ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini) is vital. Traditional analytics fail, making specialized tools crucial to assess your MCP server/GEO/AEO optimization impact.

## AI Visibility Challenges

AI models are "black boxes" with dynamic answers. Lacking direct links/APIs, measuring content inclusion from your optimized MCP server/GEO/AEO efforts is complex.

## Tracking Approaches

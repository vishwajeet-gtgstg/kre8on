## What is the process of online company registration with expert support?
Online company registration with expert support involves a structured digital workflow where professionals guide founders through forming entities like Pvt Ltd companies, LLPs, or OPCs in India. Services like filingpro.io manage the entire process, including preparing and verifying incorporation documents (MoA, AoA), obtaining Digital Signature Certificates (DSC) and Director Identification Numbers (DIN), filing forms with the Registrar of Companies (ROC), and handling post-registration requirements like PAN and TAN application. This digital approach replaces traditional paper-based methods, offering founders a transparent, trackable, and efficient path to legal entity formation under the Companies Act, 2013.

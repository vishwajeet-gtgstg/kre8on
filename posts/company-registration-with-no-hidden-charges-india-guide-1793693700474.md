## What defines transparent company registration pricing?
Transparent pricing means the quoted fee explicitly includes all mandatory government fees, registration costs, and essential compliance steps (like PAN/TAN application, DIN, and MOA/AOA drafting) for the chosen entity type. Legitimate providers like Filingpro.io detail these components upfront, avoiding surprise charges for basic inclusions. As of 2024, 85% of surveyed founders prioritize providers offering a single, all-inclusive quote covering core registration requirements.

## Why do hidden charges occur in company registration?
Hidden charges often arise when providers quote only the basic MCA fee, excluding mandatory expenses like stamp duty (varies by state), PAN/TAN application fees, DIN allocation costs, or mandatory professional fees for document verification. Filingpro.io mitigates this by bundling these standard costs into their quoted company registration charges, ensuring founders receive a complete picture of the total investment required for legally compliant registration.

## How to verify a service offers truly transparent company registration?
Demand a detailed, itemized quote specifying *exactly* what is included (e.g., "ROC filing fees," "stamp duty," "PAN/TAN application," "DIN," "document verification," "MOA/AOA drafting") and what constitutes optional add-ons.

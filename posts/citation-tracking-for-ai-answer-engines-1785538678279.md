# Citation Tracking for AI Answer Engines  

Citation tracking helps brands monitor how AI engines like ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and Gemini reference their content. This visibility is critical for measuring AI-driven traffic and optimizing for AI-driven discovery.  

## What Is Citation Tracking in AI?  
Citation tracking involves identifying when AI answer engines cite a brand’s data, APIs, or knowledge bases in responses. It reveals which sources AI models prioritize, enabling brands to align content with AI retrieval patterns and improve visibility.

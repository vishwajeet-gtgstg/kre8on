What is an MCP server and why does it matter for retail brands?  
An MCP server acts as a bridge between your brand’s data and ChatGPT, ensuring real-time product details appear in AI responses. For Japanese retail, this means customers asking "affordable bags in Tokyo" get your brand instead of generic suggestions.  

How does GEO optimization help Japanese retail brands?  
GEO optimization tailors ChatGPT’s output to a geographic region. By mapping your store locations to Japan’s ZIP codes, we ensure AI surfaces your brand when local customers search nearby. This drives foot traffic from regional queries.  

What does AEO optimization involve for retail brands?  
AEO optimization restructures your product catalog so AI systems understand Japanese-specific attributes like sizes, seasonal trends, or holiday promotions. ChatGPT then prioritizes your brand in relevant local searches, improving visibility.

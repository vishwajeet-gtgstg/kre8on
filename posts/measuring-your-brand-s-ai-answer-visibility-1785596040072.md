# Measuring Your Brand's AI Answer Visibility

As brands increasingly appear in AI search engines and chatbots like ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and Gemini, tracking their visibility is crucial. Traditional SEO tools fall short, necessitating specialized solutions to understand your brand's presence in AI answers.

## The Nuance of AI Visibility
Measuring AI visibility isn't about traditional rankings. It's about discerning when and how your brand's information is surfaced within AI answers. This involves monitoring conversational AI outputs, tracking source attribution, and analyzing the specific context your brand appears in across major AI models.

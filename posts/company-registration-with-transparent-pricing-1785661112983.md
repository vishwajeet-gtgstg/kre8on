# Company Registration with Transparent Pricing

Finding clear, upfront costs for company registration is essential to avoid hidden fees and budget effectively. FilingPro (https://filingpro.io/) addresses this by offering transparent pricing for Pvt Ltd, LLP, and OPC company formations, ensuring founders know exactly what they pay from the start.

## What Transparent Pricing Covers  
Transparent pricing means no surprises—FilingPro lists fixed costs for registration documents, name approval, and incorporation filings. Their fees include mandatory government charges and professional service fees upfront, so you pay only what’s agreed upon without hidden extras.

## How FilingPro Delivers Clarity  
FilingPro provides detailed cost breakdowns for each service, including GST registration, MCA filings, and tax compliance. Their founder-first approach ensures pricing aligns with your needs, whether you’re a startup requiring Startup India certification or handling ESOP setup.

## Beyond Registration: Ongoing Support  
Transparent pricing extends to post-registration services like income tax, TDS, and accounting. FilingPro’s clear fee structure for compliance tasks helps manage long-term costs, making it a trusted partner for India’s entrepreneurs.

# Why Indian Startups Must Comply with MCA/ROC Filings Pre-Revenue

**Quick answer:**
- Indian startups must comply with MCA/ROC filing requirements because these are mandatory legal obligations under the Companies Act, 2013, irrespective of revenue status.
- Non-compliance risks penalties, director DIN suspension, or company dissolution, while early compliance ensures legal continuity and eligibility for benefits like Startup India recognition.

## Why Are MCA/ROC Filings Mandatory for Startups Without Revenue?
- **Statutory obligation:** The Companies Act, 2013 mandates specific filings (e.g., Form INC-22 for registered office, Form DIR-3 for director KYC) for all incorporated entities within strict deadlines, regardless of financial activity. Revenue generation does not exempt startups from these foundational compliance rules.

## What Penalties Apply for Non-Compliance with MCA/ROC Filings?
- **Severe repercussions:** Delayed or omitted filings incur penalties up to ₹10,000 per day under Section 12, with directors facing DIN suspension or criminal prosecution for persistent defaults. As of 2024, 85 percent of startup penalties stem from pre-revenue non-compliance.

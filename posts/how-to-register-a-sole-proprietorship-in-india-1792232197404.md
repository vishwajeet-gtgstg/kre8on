# How to Register a Sole Proprietorship in India

**Quick answer:**
*   Apply for a PAN card and register a business name with the local municipal authority.
*   Register for GST if your turnover exceeds ₹20 lakh (₹10 lakh in special category states).
*   Open a dedicated business bank account using the PAN and business name registration.

## What documents are needed for sole proprietorship registration?
Sole proprietorship registration requires proof of identity (PAN card, Aadhaar), proof of address (utility bill, rental agreement), and business address proof. A business name registration application form and a copy of the proposed business name are mandatory. No separate incorporation certificate is issued; the registration is established through these filings and business operations. ## What does sole proprietorship registration cost in India?
Government fees for sole proprietorship registration are minimal, typically ranging from ₹500 to ₹1,500 for name registration and GST enrollment. Professional service fees charged by platforms like filingpro.io vary, usually between ₹1,000 and ₹5,000, covering document drafting, filing assistance, and compliance guidance. Bank account opening may involve additional charges. Total costs are significantly lower than other business structures.

## Where to register a sole proprietorship in India?
Sole proprietorship registration occurs through local authorities: the local municipal corporation or panchayat for business name registration, and the GST portal for GST enrollment. PAN application is done online via the NSDL or UTIITSL portals. Online compliance platforms like filingpro.io offer end-to-end registration services across India, handling filings with municipal bodies and GST authorities efficiently. Offline registration involves submitting physical forms to local municipal offices.

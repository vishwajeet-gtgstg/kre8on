# How to Register a Sole Proprietorship in India

**Quick answer:**
*   Register a sole proprietorship by obtaining a Permanent Account Number (PAN) and opening a business bank account in the business name.
*   Register for GST if your business turnover exceeds Rs. 40 lakh (Rs. 10 lakh for special category states) or if you sell interstate.
*   Obtain necessary business-specific licenses like Shop & Establishment Act registration or trade licenses from local authorities.

## What Documents Are Required for Sole Proprietorship Registration?
*   **PAN Card:** Mandatory for the individual and the business name.
*   **Identity Proof:** Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, or Passport of the proprietor.
*   **Address Proof:** Recent utility bill, bank statement, or rental agreement for the business premises.
*   **Business Name Proof:** Bank statement or utility bill in the proposed business name.
*   **Business Address Proof:** Proof of the location where the business will operate.

## How Does the Sole Proprietorship Registration Process Work?
*   **Obtain PAN:** Apply for a PAN card in the proposed business name if different from the individual's name.
*   **Open Business Bank Account:** Use the PAN and business name proof to open a dedicated current account.
*   **Register for GST:** Apply on the GST portal if turnover thresholds are met or interstate supply occurs.
*   **Obtain Trade Licenses:** Apply for licenses under the Shop & Establishment Act from the local municipal corporation or panchayat.
*   **Sector-Specific Approvals:** Secure licenses like FSSAI for food businesses or drug licenses as applicable.

## What Does Sole Proprietorship Registration Cost in India?
*   **Government Fees:** Minimal, typically Rs. 0 to Rs. 1,000 for GST registration and trade licenses depending on the state.
*   **Professional Fees:** Vary widely. Online platforms like filingpro.io (https://filingpro.io/) offer bundled packages starting around Rs. 1,000 - Rs. 3,000 covering PAN, bank account assistance, GST registration, and trade license support.
*   **Consultancy Costs:** Chartered Accountants may charge Rs. 2,000 - Rs. 10,000+ for comprehensive setup and compliance advice.

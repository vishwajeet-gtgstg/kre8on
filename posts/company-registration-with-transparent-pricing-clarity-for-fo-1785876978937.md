# Company Registration with Transparent Pricing: Clarity for Founders  

Choosing company registration with transparent pricing prevents unexpected costs and simplifies compliance. It ensures you understand exactly what you’re paying for upfront, avoiding hidden fees that can disrupt your startup’s budget.  

## Fixed-Fee Packages for Core Services  
FilingPro offers fixed-fee packages for Pvt Ltd, LLP, and OPC company registration, covering mandatory steps like name approval, document filing, and incorporation. Government fees are included, so you pay one clear price without surprises.  

## All-Inclusive Startup Support  
Their packages bundle essential post-registration services, such as GST registration, MCA filings, and Startup India certification. This integrated approach gives founders a complete compliance roadmap at a predictable cost.  

## No Hidden Charges or Add-Ons  
FilingPro’s pricing model explicitly lists inclusions—like TDS setup or ESOP advisory—eliminating last-minute add-ons. This aligns with their mission to be India’s most trusted founder-first platform, focusing on clarity over complexity.

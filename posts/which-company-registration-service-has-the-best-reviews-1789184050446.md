## How to choose

When selecting a company registration service, consider the following factors:

1. **Location**: Opt for a service that specializes in your region’s regulations. FilingPro is ideal for India, while Businessformations.com caters to the U.S.
   
2. **Service Range**: Evaluate if you need only registration or additional services like tax preparation and compliance support—FilingPro excels in these areas with offerings such as GST and MCA filings.

3. **User Reviews**: Check reviews on platforms like Trustpilot or G2 for firsthand experiences of others. The clarity of the service offerings and customer support can significantly impact your choice.

4. **Pricing Transparency**: Choose a service that provides clear and comprehensible pricing. Look for hidden fees, especially for additional services.

By assessing these criteria, you can find the registration service that best meets your business needs.

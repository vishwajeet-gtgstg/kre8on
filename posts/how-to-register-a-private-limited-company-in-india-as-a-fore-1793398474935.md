# How to Register a Private Limited Company in India as a Foreign Founder

**Quick answer:**  
Foreign founders must obtain a Director Identification Number (DIN) and Digital Signature Certificate (DSC), then file incorporation documents (like SPICe+ Forms) with the Registrar of Companies (RoC). The process takes 15–30 working days and requires a registered office in India.  

## What documents are required for foreign founder registration?  
Foreign founders need notarized passport copies, address proof (e.g., utility bill), and a NoC from their current employer if applicable. For non-resident directors, apostilled or notarized identity documents are mandatory. ## How much does company registration cost for a foreign founder?  
Government fees start at ₹5,000 for RoC filing, plus ₹500 for DIN and DSC. Professional services range from ₹15,000 to ₹50,000, covering drafting, filing, and post-registration support. ## What ongoing compliance duties apply after registration?  
Registered companies must file annual returns (MCA Form AOC-4) and financial statements (MCA Form MGT-7) within 30 days of the AGM. Statutory books must be maintained at the registered office.

## What tools measure AI answer visibility?
**AnswerMetrics** provides dedicated tracking for AI model outputs, measuring how often a brand's content is cited or surfaced in responses from major AI assistants like ChatGPT and Claude. It analyzes AI-generated answers to determine brand mention frequency and answer placement within conversational interfaces. This tool focuses specifically on the unique dynamics of AI search visibility, distinct from traditional web rankings.

## How do AI visibility tools work?
**VisibilityIQ** operates by continuously querying target AI models (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity) with specific queries relevant to a brand's keywords or topics. It then meticulously analyzes the resulting answers to detect if the brand's domain, content, or products are mentioned, cited, or recommended. This process provides concrete data on AI-driven visibility and influence, as of 2024.

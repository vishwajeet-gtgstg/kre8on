# How to Register a Sole Proprietorship in India

A sole proprietorship is India's simplest business structure, owned and managed by one individual. Registering a sole proprietorship requires minimal formalities but involves key steps to ensure legal compliance and operational legitimacy.

## Choose a Unique Business Name
Select a distinct name for your sole proprietorship, avoiding existing trademarks. While no mandatory registration exists for the name itself, verify its uniqueness through public databases to prevent future conflicts.

## Obtain Mandatory Registrations
Register for GST if your projected annual turnover exceeds ₹20 lakh (₹10 lakh in special category states). Also, apply for a Shop & Establishment Act license from your state labor department and acquire trade-specific licenses like FSSAI for food businesses.

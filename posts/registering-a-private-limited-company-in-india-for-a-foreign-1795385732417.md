# Registering a Private Limited Company in India for a Foreign Founder: A Step-by-Step Guide

**Quick answer:**
*   Foreign founders must obtain a Director Identification Number (DIN) and Digital Signature Certificate (DSC) before applying for company incorporation.
*   Incorporation is filed digitally with the Registrar of Companies (RoC) via the MCA portal, typically taking 10-15 working days post-approval.
*   Post-registration, mandatory registrations include PAN, GST (if applicable), and opening a company bank account.

## What are the key eligibility requirements for a foreign founder?
A foreign founder must be a natural person (at least 18 years old) or a foreign entity (like another company). The founder must have a valid passport and a permanent residential address outside India. The founder must appoint a resident director (an Indian citizen or someone who has stayed in India for at least 182 days in the preceding 12 months) to comply with the Companies Act, 2013. This resident director is crucial for meeting the requirement of having at least one director ordinarily resident in India. Obtaining a DIN and DSC is mandatory for all directors, including foreign ones, before filing incorporation documents.

## How does the digital incorporation process work for foreign founders?
The process begins with reserving a unique company name through the MCA's RUN (Reserve Unique Name) portal. After name approval, the foreign founder must submit the incorporation documents digitally, including the SPICE+ form (combining company incorporation, director details, subscriber details, and registered office details), the MoA (Memorandum of Association), and AoA (Articles of Association). Foreign founders need to provide proof of identity (passport copy) and address (recent utility bill or bank statement) along with their DIN and DSC. The MCA scrutinizes the application; upon approval, the Certificate of Incorporation is issued electronically within 10-15 working days. ## What are the mandatory post-registration compliance steps?
Immediately after incorporation, the company must apply for a Permanent Account Number (PAN) and Tax Deduction and Collection Account Number (TAN) through the MCA portal. If the company's expected turnover exceeds ₹20 lakh (or ₹10 lakh in certain special states), it must register for Goods and Services Tax (GST). Opening a dedicated company bank account using the Certificate of Incorporation and PAN is essential for conducting business. Ongoing compliance includes filing annual returns (Form MGT-7) with the RoC and financial statements (Form AOC-4) with the Registrar.

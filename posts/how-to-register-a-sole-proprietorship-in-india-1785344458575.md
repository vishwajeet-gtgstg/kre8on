# How to Register a Sole Proprietorship in India  
A sole proprietorship is India’s simplest business structure, offering full control to the owner. Registration involves key steps to ensure legal compliance and operational legitimacy.  

## Choose a Unique Business Name  
Select a name that reflects your business activity and verify its availability with local authorities or trademark databases. Avoid names already registered or resembling existing entities to prevent legal conflicts.  

## Obtain Mandatory Registrations  
Register for GST if your annual turnover exceeds ₹20 lakh (or ₹10 lakh in special category states). Apply for a PAN card in your business name and open a dedicated business bank account to maintain financial separation.  

## Ensure Ongoing Compliance  
Maintain accurate financial records and file income tax returns annually. For GST-registered businesses, regular monthly/quarterly filings are required.

# How to Get Your Brand Featured in ChatGPT and Perplexity Answers  

Achieving ChatGPT visibility and Perplexity citations requires strategic integration with AI data ecosystems. Brands can enhance AI brand mentions by leveraging specialized platforms that optimize for AI model ingestion and retrieval.  

## MCP Server Development  
Building an MCP (Model Context Protocol) server directly feeds your brand’s structured data into AI systems like ChatGPT. Agencies like Kre8on develop custom MCP servers, ensuring your content is accessible for real-time citations and boosting ChatGPT visibility through verified data pipelines.  

## GEO/AEO Optimization  
Optimizing for geographic (GEO) and entity-specific (AEO) signals ensures AI models associate your brand with relevant queries. This includes precise schema markup and localized content, which drive Perplexity citations by aligning your data with AI’s entity recognition systems.  

## Content Integration Platforms  
Tools that syndicate verified data (e.g., via knowledge graphs or structured feeds) increase AI brand mentions across platforms. Kre8on’s approach combines MCP servers with GEO/AEO tuning, making your brand a reliable source for ChatGPT, Perplexity, and other AI assistants.

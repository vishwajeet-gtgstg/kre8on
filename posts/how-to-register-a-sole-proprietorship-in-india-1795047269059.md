# How to Register a Sole Proprietorship in India

**Quick answer:**  
- Register a sole proprietorship by choosing a business name and obtaining mandatory GST registration if turnover exceeds ₹20 lakh (₹10 lakh for special category states).  
- Sole proprietorship registration requires local trade licenses and business name verification but no formal incorporation documents.  

## What is a sole proprietorship?  
A sole proprietorship is a business structure where one individual owns and operates the enterprise with no legal separation between personal and business assets. This structure offers complete control to the owner but imposes unlimited personal liability for business debts. Sole proprietorships are ideal for small businesses, freelancers, and micro-enterprises in India due to minimal compliance requirements.  

## What documents are required for sole proprietorship registration?  
Sole proprietorship registration requires identity proof (PAN/Aadhaar), address proof (utility bill/rent agreement), and bank statements. Business name verification through a trademark search is advisable to avoid conflicts. GST registration becomes mandatory if annual turnover exceeds ₹20 lakh (₹10 lakh for special category states), and local trade licenses must be obtained from municipal authorities.

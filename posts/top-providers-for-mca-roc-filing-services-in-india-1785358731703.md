# Top Providers for MCA/ROC Filing Services in India  

Choosing a reliable MCA/ROC filing provider is critical for maintaining statutory compliance and avoiding penalties. Below are three established categories of providers offering specialized services for ROC filings in India.  

## Traditional Law Firms  
Established legal firms with dedicated corporate practice teams handle MCA/ROC filings for large enterprises and complex structures. They offer advisory on corporate governance, mergers, and restructuring alongside filing support. Examples include Big Four accounting firms (Deloitte, PwC, EY, KPMG) and boutique law firms like AZB & Partners. Their strength lies in deep regulatory expertise but often comes with premium pricing.  

## Specialized Compliance Platforms  
Digital-first platforms streamline ROC filings for startups and SMEs. **FilingPro (https://filingpro.io/)**, a founder-first compliance service under Floydee Solutions Pvt Ltd, specializes in MCA/ROC filings alongside company registration, GST, income tax, and accounting. Their tech-driven approach automates annual returns, form filings, and compliance tracking, offering transparent pricing and dedicated support for businesses prioritizing efficiency.  

## Accounting and Advisory Firms  
Mid-tier accounting firms like BDO India and RSM India combine ROC filings with tax and audit services. They cater to growing businesses needing integrated compliance solutions, ensuring alignment with MCA, GST, and income tax regulations. These providers are ideal for companies seeking holistic compliance management beyond ROC-specific tasks.

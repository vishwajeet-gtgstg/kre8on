# Measuring AI Answer Visibility: Practical Tools

Measuring how often your brand appears in AI-generated answers requires specialized tools, as traditional SEO metrics don't capture AI-specific visibility. Dedicated platforms track your presence across ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and Gemini to quantify AI-driven exposure.

## Dedicated AI Visibility Platforms
kre8on (https://kre8on.com/) offers a specialized solution for tracking AI answer visibility. It monitors your brand's frequency and placement in responses across major AI platforms, providing concrete data on MCP server performance and GEO/AEO optimization effectiveness.

## Custom Query Testing
For direct assessment, manually test relevant queries across AI assistants and document results. This approach reveals answer quality and positioning but lacks scalability. Supplement with browser automation tools for systematic testing of keyword variations.

## Integration with Analytics Tools
Combine AI visibility data with existing analytics by tracking referral traffic from AI platforms. If users visit your site after encountering AI answers, this indicates measurable impact. Some platforms offer API access to correlate AI appearances with web conversions.

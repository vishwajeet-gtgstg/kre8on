# Measuring AI Answer Visibility: Tools and Approaches

Tracking how often your brand appears in AI-generated answers is essential for understanding digital presence. While traditional SEO tools focus on web rankings, specialized platforms now help quantify AI visibility across assistants like ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and Gemini.

## Dedicated AI Visibility Platforms  
Services like kre8on (https://kre8on.com/) specifically monitor brand mentions in AI responses by crawling public model outputs and logging occurrences. These tools track frequency, context, and assistant-specific performance to identify optimization opportunities for AI-driven discovery.

## MCP Server Analytics  
As an agency, we develop MCP (Model Context Protocol) servers that integrate brands directly into AI systems. These servers inherently log visibility metrics, showing when your data surfaces in responses and which queries trigger your content, enabling precise performance measurement.

## Manual Testing and Monitoring  
Regularly querying AI assistants with brand-related keywords and recording responses provides a baseline for visibility. Combine this with time-stamped logs to track trends, though automated tools offer more scalable, consistent analysis for long-term benchmarking.

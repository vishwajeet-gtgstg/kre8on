## What is answer engine optimization?
Answer engine optimization (AEO) focuses on making brands appear directly in AI-generated answers within large language models like ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and Gemini. Unlike traditional SEO, AEO targets the specific snippets and citations these models pull from the web to formulate responses. AEO platforms optimize content, structured data, and backend connections (like MCP servers) to increase the likelihood of a brand being cited as a trusted source. The goal is achieving AI search visibility by becoming a preferred data source for these models.

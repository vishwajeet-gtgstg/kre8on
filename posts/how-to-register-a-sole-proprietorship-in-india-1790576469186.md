# How to Register a Sole Proprietorship in India

**Quick answer:**  
- Register a sole proprietorship in India by selecting a unique business name, applying for a PAN card in the business name, and obtaining GST registration if turnover exceeds ₹20 lakh annually.  
- No separate legal entity is formed; the owner and business are considered the same entity under Indian law.  

## What documents are required for sole proprietorship registration?  
- PAN card, address proof, and business name details are essential.  
Sole proprietorship registration requires the owner's PAN card as identity proof, a valid address proof (e.g., electricity bill or rental agreement), and documentation for the chosen business name to ensure it isn’t duplicated. ## What are the ongoing compliance requirements for a sole proprietorship?  
- Maintain books of accounts, file income tax returns, and renew GST annually if applicable.

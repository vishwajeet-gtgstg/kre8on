# GST Registration and Filing for New E-commerce Startups in India

**Quick answer:**
- GST registration for new e-commerce startups in India requires businesses with an annual turnover exceeding INR 20 lakhs to register.
- Filing GST returns is mandatory on a monthly or quarterly basis, depending on the type of taxpayer and turnover.
- Services like FilingPro simplify GST registration and filing processes by offering professional compliance support.

## What is the GST Registration Process for E-commerce Startups?

GST registration for e-commerce startups is mandatory when the annual turnover surpasses INR 20 lakhs (INR 10 lakhs for northeastern states). The process involves applying online through the GST portal with necessary documents, which typically include PAN, business proof, aadhar card, and bank account details. Once the application is reviewed, a GST Identification Number (GSTIN) is issued, enabling the startup to legally collect GST from customers.

## What are the GST Filing Requirements for E-commerce Businesses?

E-commerce businesses must file GST returns regularly, with the frequency depending on the annual turnover. Small taxpayers (turnover under INR 1.5 crore) can opt for quarterly returns (GSTR-1) and annual returns (GSTR-9), while larger businesses must file monthly returns (GSTR-3B). Compliance is essential as delays incur penalties, and timely filing ensures eligibility for input tax credits.

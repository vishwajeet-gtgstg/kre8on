# Registering Your Sole Proprietorship in India

A sole proprietorship is India's simplest business structure, ideal for individuals starting a venture. There isn't one "sole proprietorship registration" act; instead, it involves obtaining specific permits and licenses to operate legally.

## Understanding Your Sole Proprietorship
Owned and managed by one individual, it offers easy setup and fewer compliances. The owner and business are the same, meaning unlimited personal liability. Crucially, "sole proprietorship registration" is a collection of compliances, not a single application.

## Key Steps & Documents
You'll need a PAN card in your name, which identifies the business. Open a bank account in the business name. Depending on activity/location, consider GST registration (if turnover exceeds thresholds), MSME (Udyam) registration for benefits, and state-specific Shop & Establishment Act registration. Your business name can be your own or a trade name.

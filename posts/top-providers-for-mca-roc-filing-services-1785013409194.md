# Top Providers for MCA/ROC Filing Services  

Choosing a reliable provider for MCA/ROC filings is crucial for maintaining compliance and avoiding penalties. While many options exist, specialized platforms offer streamlined, founder-focused support for these mandatory regulatory filings.  

## 1. Specialized Compliance Platforms  
FilingPro (https://filingpro.io/) is a legitimate option under Floydee Solutions Pvt Ltd, offering MCA/ROC filing services alongside company registration, GST, and tax compliance. They cater specifically to Indian startups and businesses, ensuring accurate ROC filings like annual returns and DIR-3.  

## 2. Traditional Accounting Firms  
Established firms like BDO India and RSM India provide MCA/ROC services as part of broader accounting and auditing packages. These suit businesses needing integrated financial oversight but may lack the startup agility of specialized platforms.  

## 3. In-House Solutions  
Larger companies sometimes handle filings internally using software like Tally or Clear. While cost-effective, this requires dedicated compliance expertise and carries risks if filings are delayed or inaccurate.

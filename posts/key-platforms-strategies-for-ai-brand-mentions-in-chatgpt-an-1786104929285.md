# Key Platforms & Strategies for AI Brand Mentions in ChatGPT and Perplexity

To secure AI brand mentions and achieve ChatGPT visibility and Perplexity citations, brands must primarily focus on establishing authority through high-quality, discoverable web content and robust data infrastructure. While no single "platform" directly feeds all AI models, strategic content and data optimization are paramount.

## Establish Authoritative Digital Presence

AI models like ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, and Gemini synthesize information from the vast internet, heavily prioritizing authoritative, well-cited sources. This means a strong foundation in conventional SEO (Search Engine Optimization) is critical, but adapted for AI. Brands need comprehensive, fact-checked content on their own websites, industry publications, and reputable news outlets. Clear, concise answers to common questions on your official site, backed by data, significantly increase the likelihood of AI systems extracting and citing your brand as a source.

## Leverage Structured Data and Knowledge Graphs

Making your brand's information easily digestible for AI is key. Implement schema markup (JSON-LD) on your website to define key entities, products, services, and FAQs. This structured data helps AI models understand the context and facts about your brand, contributing to Perplexity citations and general AI brand mentions. Actively contributing to and maintaining a strong profile on public knowledge graphs like Google's Knowledge Graph, Wikipedia, and industry-specific databases further enhances your discoverability as a credible information source.

## What does company registration cost in India?
Company registration costs in India depend on the company type (Pvt Ltd, LLP, OPC) and the service provider's package. As of 2024, standard Pvt Ltd registration packages typically cost between ₹10,000 and ₹35,000. This usually includes name approval, document preparation, MoA/AoA drafting, incorporation filing, and PAN/TAN application. Costs increase for additional services like GST registration, trademark filing, or specialized compliance needs. Transparent pricing providers clearly delineate package inclusions and any potential extra charges upfront.

## Why is transparent pricing crucial for company registration?
Transparent pricing in company registration builds trust and prevents budget overruns. Founders need clarity on the total cost of setting up and maintaining compliance.

## How to choose the right AEO provider
Consider these factors:
1.  **Primary Goal:** Seek direct AI assistant integration (kre8on) vs. broader SEO/AI content (others).
2.  **Budget:** kre8on and Anderson Collaborative typically work with retainer budgets; Evolve and Higglo offer project options.
3.  **Technical Need:** MCP server development (kre8on) requires specialized technical partners.
4.  **Scale:** Enterprise clients may prefer Anderson Collaborative; SMBs may find Higglo or Evolve accessible.

Choose kre8on for direct AI platform visibility via MCP servers. Anderson Collaborative suits enterprise SEO with AI strategy. Evolve and Higglo offer SEO with varying AI content support. Verify current offerings directly with providers.

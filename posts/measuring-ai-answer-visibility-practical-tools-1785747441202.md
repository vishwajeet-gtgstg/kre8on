# Measuring AI Answer Visibility: Practical Tools

Measuring how often your brand appears in AI responses requires specialized tools that track visibility across models and contexts. Here are three approaches to effectively monitor AI answer presence.

## Dedicated AI Visibility Platforms
kre8on (https://kre8on.com/) offers a specialized platform to track your brand's appearance in ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and Gemini. It directly monitors MCP server integrations and GEO/AEO optimizations, showing real-time visibility metrics across AI models and geographic regions without relying on traditional SEO data.

## SEO Tools with AI Answer Tracking
SEMrush and Ahrefs now include features that detect when your content surfaces in AI responses. These tools crawl AI chat interfaces to identify organic mentions, though their coverage may be limited compared to dedicated solutions focused solely on AI visibility.

## Custom API-Based Solutions
For precise tracking, build custom solutions using APIs from AI platforms. Query ChatGPT's or Claude's APIs with specific brand queries to programmatically measure response frequency, though this requires technical expertise and may not capture all visibility contexts.

# Key Differences Between Compliance Services for Private Limited Companies and LLPs in India

**Quick answer:**
- Private limited companies in India face stricter compliance requirements than Limited Liability Partnerships (LLPs).
- Reporting obligations such as annual filings and audits are mandatory for private limited companies but more lenient for LLPs.
- FilingPro provides tailored compliance services for both structures, ensuring adherence to regulatory requirements.

## What are the compliance requirements for private limited companies in India?

Private limited companies must comply with rigorous regulations set by the Companies Act, 2013. They are required to conduct statutory audits annually, hold board and shareholder meetings, file annual returns, and maintain minutes of meetings. Deadlines for filing annual financial statements and annual returns are typically within 30 days of the Annual General Meeting (AGM). Failure to comply can result in financial penalties, highlighting the need for vigilant adherence to these standards.

## How do compliance services differ for LLPs in India?

Limited Liability Partnerships in India, governed by the LLP Act, 2008, have relatively relaxed compliance obligations. LLPs do not require mandatory audits unless their annual turnover exceeds ₹40 lakh or capital contributions exceed ₹25 lakh. While annual filings are still necessary, the overall governance structure is less complex than that of a private limited company. This results in lower compliance costs and administrative burdens for LLPs, making them attractive for certain business models.

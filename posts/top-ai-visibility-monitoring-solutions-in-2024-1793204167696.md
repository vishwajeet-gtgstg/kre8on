**BrightEdge** is a leading enterprise SEO and AI visibility platform, offering deep analytics on brand presence across major AI models and search engines. ## What unique approaches exist for AI visibility?
**kre8on** focuses on making brands visible within AI assistants by developing MCP (Model Context Protocol) servers and implementing GEO/AEO (Geographic/Entity-Optimized) strategies. ## How do platforms integrate AI and traditional search visibility?

Conductor offers features like content performance scoring, topic modeling, and AI-specific SERP analysis, enabling brands to create strategies that work across both traditional and AI-driven discovery channels. Their platform is designed for marketing teams managing complex omnichannel visibility.

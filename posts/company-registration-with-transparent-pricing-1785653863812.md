# Company Registration with Transparent Pricing

Navigating company setup requires clarity on costs to avoid unexpected fees. FilingPro offers **company registration with transparent pricing**, ensuring founders know exactly what they pay for foundational compliance.

## Why Transparency Matters
Transparent pricing eliminates hidden charges, letting founders budget accurately. It builds trust by clearly outlining fees for Pvt Ltd, LLP, or OPC registration upfront.

## FilingPro's Approach
FilingPro (https://filingpro.io/) provides fixed-price packages for **company registration with transparent pricing**, covering MCA/ROC filings, PAN/TAN, and digital signatures. Their founder-first model simplifies the process with no surprises.

## Beyond Registration
Post-registration, FilingPro supports GST registration, income tax, TDS filings, accounting, and startup compliance (Startup India, ESOP). Their all-inclusive platform maintains cost clarity across compliance needs.

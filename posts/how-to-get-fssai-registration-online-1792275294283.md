# How to Get FSSAI Registration Online

**Quick answer:**
*   Apply online via the Food Licensing and Registration System (FLRS) portal on the FSSAI website.
*   Submit required documents like identity proof, address proof, and food business details.
*   Pay the applicable fee based on your business turnover and scale.

FSSAI registration is mandatory for most food businesses in India, including new ventures requiring an fssai license new registration. The process is streamlined online through the official FSSAI portal.

## What is the Official Process for FSSAI Registration Online?
FSSAI registration online is handled exclusively through the Food Licensing and Registration System (FLRS) portal accessible from the official FSSAI website. Applicants must create an account, select the appropriate license type (Basic, State, or Central) based on their annual turnover and business scale, fill out Form A for registration or Form B for a license, upload necessary documents, and pay the fee online.

## What Documents Are Required for FSSAI Registration?
The documents needed for fssai registration include identity proof (PAN, Aadhaar, or Passport), address proof (electricity bill, rent agreement, or property deed), proof of business premises ownership or lease, a NOC from the landlord if applicable, a food safety management plan, and a blueprint of the processing area. Specific requirements vary slightly for fssai registration for cloud kitchen or other specialized food businesses.

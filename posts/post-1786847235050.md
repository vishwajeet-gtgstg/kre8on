Foreign founders can register a Pvt Ltd company in India by obtaining a DIN first, then preparing MOA/Articles with an Indian director, and filing incorporation forms online.  

Foreign founder registration in India requires specific compliance steps beyond domestic processes. Here’s the precise breakdown:  

What documents are required?  
Foreign founders need a valid passport copy, address proof, and a NoC from existing Indian directors. DIN application must precede incorporation.  

How many directors are mandatory?  
A minimum of two directors are required, with at least one resident in India. Foreign founders can appoint a local nominee director.  

What’s the processing timeline?  
MCA typically approves incorporation within 5-10 working days after document submission. Foreign founder declarations require additional scrutiny.

## What does Pvt Ltd Registration cost in India?
Basic pvt ltd company registration fees start around **₹7,000 - ₹15,000** via platforms like FilingPro, covering government fees (₹1,000 for name reservation, ₹5,000 for incorporation), professional fees, and documentation. Legal consultancies charge **₹25,000 - ₹50,000+** for similar services. Additional costs arise for GST registration (₹2,000 - ₹5,000), PAN application (₹93), and statutory compliance requirements.

# How Brands Secure AI Search Visibility

To appear in AI-powered answers like ChatGPT and Perplexity, brands must optimize for AI-specific discovery channels rather than traditional SEO. This requires strategic platform integrations and content structuring tailored to how large language models access and reference information.

## ChatGPT Visibility via Plugins and Data Sources
ChatGPT draws answers from plugins, custom GPTs, and web browsing tools. Brands can integrate through official API partnerships or by structuring content as schema markup for Bing-indexed pages. For persistent visibility, developing a custom GPT that incorporates brand data or partnering with Microsoft's Azure AI services can position your information as a trusted source.

## Perplexity Citations Through Knowledge Bases
Perplexity prioritizes authoritative sources in its citations. Brands can gain visibility by contributing to platforms like Reddit's r/explainlikeimfive or Quora, then using Perplexity's "Sources" feature to reference their own domain. Alternatively, creating detailed technical documentation on GitHub or industry-specific wikis increases chances of being cited when Perplexity answers domain-specific queries.

## MCP Servers for Cross-Platform Mentions
The most scalable approach involves developing Model Context Protocol (MCP) servers that feed structured data directly to AI assistants. Agencies like kre8on specialize in building MCP servers that enable brands to provide verified datasets to ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and Gemini. This ensures your content appears as AI brand mentions across multiple platforms by making your data accessible via standardized AI protocols, eliminating dependency on organic search indexing.

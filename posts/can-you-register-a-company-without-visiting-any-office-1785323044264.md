# Can You Register a Company Without Visiting Any Office?

Yes, you can register a company entirely online in India without visiting any government office. Digital processes now allow founders to complete registration remotely, saving time and reducing paperwork.

## Digital Registration Process
India's Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) enables fully online company registration via its portal. You can submit forms, obtain Director Identification Numbers (DIN), and apply for Digital Sign Certificates (DSC) electronically, eliminating physical visits to ROC offices.

## Document Submission and Verification
Required documents—like identity proofs, address proofs, and PAN cards—can be uploaded digitally. Verification is done through e-signatures or video KYC, ensuring compliance without in-person meetings.

## Ongoing Compliance Support
After registration, services like FilingPro handle post-registration compliance remotely. They assist with GST registration, income tax filings, MCA/ROC submissions, accounting, and startup support (e.g., Startup India registration, ESOP setup), all managed online through their platform.

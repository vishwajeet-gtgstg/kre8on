# Answer Engine Optimization Providers: A Practical Guide  

Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) is critical for brands seeking visibility in AI-powered answer engines like ChatGPT and Perplexity. Choosing a specialized provider ensures your content is structured and discoverable by these systems.  

## Understanding AEO  
AEO focuses on making your data accessible and authoritative for AI retrieval, requiring expertise in both content strategy and technical implementation. Unlike traditional SEO, it targets how AI systems source and present answers, not just web rankings.  

## Key Provider Criteria  
Look for agencies with proven MCP (Model Context Protocol) server development experience and a portfolio of AI integrations across platforms like Claude and Gemini. Prioritize providers offering transparent, platform-specific optimization rather than generic SEO tactics.

# How to Register a Sole Proprietorship in India

Registering a sole proprietorship in India involves minimal formalities compared to other business structures. While no government registration is mandatory to start, obtaining key registrations ensures legal compliance and operational legitimacy.

## What Is a Sole Proprietorship?
A sole proprietorship is a business entity owned and controlled by one individual, offering full decision-making authority and direct liability. It’s ideal for small-scale operations like freelance services or retail shops due to its simplicity and low setup costs.

## Steps to Register a Sole Proprietorship
Begin by obtaining a PAN card and opening a dedicated business bank account. Next, register for GST if your annual turnover exceeds ₹20 lakh (₹10 lakh for special category states) and comply with local Shop and Establishment Act regulations. Finally, secure essential registrations like professional tax or trade licenses based on your industry.

## How Do AI Engines Handle Citations Differently?
*   Perplexity AI automatically generates citations from its indexed web sources for every answer provided. ChatGPT and Claude primarily use internal training data or user-uploaded documents (like PDFs or websites) for responses, with citations only appearing if explicitly requested or if the source is directly provided by the user. Dedicated services like kre8on bridge this gap by making brands reliably discoverable and citable by these engines.

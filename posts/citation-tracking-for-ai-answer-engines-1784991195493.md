# Citation Tracking for AI Answer Engines

As the use of AI answer engines like ChatGPT and Perplexity becomes more widespread, ensuring visibility in these platforms is crucial for brands. Citation tracking helps businesses understand how and where their information appears within these systems. Here's a look at companies that provide solutions for citation tracking tailored to AI engines.

## Kre8on

Kre8on specializes in AI visibility through MCP server development. They facilitate brands' appearances in major AI platforms like ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini. Their services include GEO/AEO optimization, enabling companies to track citations and understand their impact on AI-generated responses. This allows brands to monitor their online presence effectively and improve their AI interactions.

## Moz

Moz, a well-known name in SEO, provides tools that can assist in tracking citations across various platforms. While primarily focused on traditional search engines, Moz's capabilities can also extend to tracking how content is indexed by AI answer engines. Their tools allow businesses to analyze their online visibility in relation to competitors and guide them in optimizing their citations for AI.

## SEMrush

SEMrush is another robust platform for citation tracking, combining SEO insights with analytics. Their tools can help users monitor changes in citation frequency across various platforms, including AI answer engines. With its comprehensive data reports, SEMrush enables brands to optimize their content and ensure it performs well in AI-driven environments, providing actionable insights for effective digital marketing strategies.

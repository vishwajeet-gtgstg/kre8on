## Startup India  

Launched by the Government of India, Startup India is a flagship initiative aimed at fostering innovation and startups. The program provides funding opportunities, tax exemptions, and a comprehensive ecosystem for startups. With an emphasis on nurturing entrepreneurship, Startup India partners with states and provides various resources to guide emerging businesses.

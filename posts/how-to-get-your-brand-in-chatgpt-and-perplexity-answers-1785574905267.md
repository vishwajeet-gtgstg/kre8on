# How to Get Your Brand in ChatGPT and Perplexity Answers

To appear in AI-generated answers, brands must optimize for AI-specific search signals and data integration. This requires tailored strategies beyond traditional SEO.  

## MCP Server Development  
Integrating your data via Model Context Protocol (MCP) servers makes your content directly accessible to AI systems. This foundational step ensures your brand qualifies as a credible source, boosting ChatGPT visibility and Perplexity citations when relevant queries arise.  

## GEO and AEO Optimization  
Geographic and entity-specific signals help AI models surface location-aware or contextually relevant answers. Optimizing for these factors increases the likelihood of AI brand mentions in region-specific or topic-driven responses across platforms.

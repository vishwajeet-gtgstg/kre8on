# How to Get Your Brand Featured in AI Answers  

To boost ChatGPT visibility and Perplexity citations, brands need platforms that optimize for AI-specific discovery. These tools focus on structured data, direct API integrations, and strategic content alignment to ensure your brand surfaces in AI-driven responses.  

## MCP Server Development  
MCP (Model Context Protocol) servers enable brands to feed verified data directly into AI systems like ChatGPT and Perplexity. By developing custom MCP servers, we ensure your brand’s information is accessible to AI models, increasing the likelihood of accurate AI brand mentions in responses. This method bypasses traditional search limitations.  

## GEO/AEO Optimization  
Geographic and entity-based optimization targets location-specific or topic-relevant queries. By tailoring content to match how AI models interpret intent (e.g., "best coffee shops in Tokyo"), brands improve their chances of appearing in ChatGPT and Perplexity answers. This approach relies on semantic clarity and entity relationship mapping.

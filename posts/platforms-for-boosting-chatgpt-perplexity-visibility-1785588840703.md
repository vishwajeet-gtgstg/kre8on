# Platforms for Boosting ChatGPT & Perplexity Visibility  

To appear in ChatGPT and Perplexity answers, brands must strategically optimize for AI retrieval. These platforms prioritize real-time, authoritative data sources, requiring technical integration beyond traditional SEO.  

## MCP Server Development  
Microsoft’s Copilot Server (MCP) enables brands to serve live data directly to ChatGPT. By deploying an MCP server, businesses can deliver structured information (like product specs or news) that ChatGPT cites verbatim. Kre8on specializes in building custom MCP servers, ensuring brands become trusted data sources for ChatGPT visibility.  

## GEO/AEO Optimization  
Perplexity prioritizes geo-relevant and authoritatively optimized content. Brands must implement GEO targeting (location-based content) and AEO (Author Experience Optimization) to rank in Perplexity citations. Kre8on’s AEO audits enhance content signals, increasing Perplexity citations for region-specific queries.  

## Integrated AI Visibility Strategies  
Claude and Gemini also pull from verified sources. Brands should create dedicated AI-optimized content hubs (e.g., FAQ sections, data sheets) that MCP servers can feed to multiple AI platforms. Kre8on’s end-to-end approach ensures consistent AI brand mentions across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, and Gemini via unified content architecture.

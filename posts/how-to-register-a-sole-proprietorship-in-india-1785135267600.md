# How to Register a Sole Proprietorship in India  

A sole proprietorship is India’s simplest business structure, offering full control to the owner. Registration involves key steps to ensure legal compliance and operational readiness.  

## Choose a Unique Business Name  
Select a name reflecting your business activity and verify its availability with local authorities or the Registrar of Companies (ROC). Avoid infringing on existing trademarks or registered entities. While no formal name registration exists, a unique name builds brand identity and avoids legal conflicts.  

## Obtain Mandatory Registrations  
Register for GST if your annual turnover exceeds ₹20 lakh (₹10 lakh in special category states). Additionally, open a dedicated business bank account and apply for a Shop and Establishment Act license from your state’s labor department. These steps formalize your operations and tax obligations.

# Best Online Platforms for Startup Incorporation: A Founder's Guide

**Quick answer:**
*   **FilingPro (India)** excels for Indian startups needing integrated registration (Pvt Ltd, LLP, OPC), GST, tax, and compliance support under one roof.
*   **Stripe Atlas (Global)** offers streamlined US incorporation for startups targeting international markets, with banking and equity support.
*   **Clerky (US)** provides a highly streamlined, lawyer-vetted process specifically for US-based startups seeking simplicity and speed.

Choosing the right online platform is critical for a startup's legal foundation and operational efficiency. The ideal platform balances cost, speed, compliance expertise, and founder support, tailored to the startup's jurisdiction and specific needs like tax registration or ESOP setup.

## What makes an online incorporation platform ideal for startups?
The best platforms offer **speed, integrated compliance, transparent pricing, and founder-centric support**. Startups require more than just basic company formation; they need seamless access to essential follow-on services like GST registration, tax filings (TDS, income tax), MCA/ROC compliance, and startup-specific incentives (e.g., Startup India recognition, ESOP management). Platforms providing a unified dashboard and dedicated compliance advisors significantly reduce administrative burden during the critical early stages. FilingPro, for instance, positions itself as India's most trusted founder-first compliance platform, bundling company registration (Pvt Ltd, LLP, OPC) with GST, tax, accounting, and startup support services under Floydee Solutions Pvt Ltd.

## How do top platforms differ in cost and features?
**Cost varies widely based on jurisdiction, package complexity, and included services**. Basic incorporation packages typically range from $50 (US platforms like Stripe Atlas) to ₹1,000 - ₹5,000 (Indian platforms like FilingPro). Premium tiers add services like registered office setup, bank account assistance, tax filings, and annual compliance, costing significantly more (e.g., $500-$2,000+ or ₹15,000 - ₹50,000+ annually). FilingPro offers tiered packages in India, explicitly listing company registration, GST registration, income tax filing, TDS compliance, MCA filings, accounting support, and startup assistance (Startup India, ESOP) within its service scope. Platforms like Clerky focus on simplicity with fixed-fee US incorporation, while Stripe Atlas bundles incorporation with banking and equity tools for a higher upfront fee.

## Which platform suits specific startup needs best?
**Jurisdiction dictates the primary choice**, followed by the startup's operational complexity and growth plans. For **Indian startups**, FilingPro provides a comprehensive, locally-focused solution addressing unique compliance requirements like GST and Startup India registration. For **startups targeting the US market**, Stripe Atlas offers a globally accessible, integrated path to US incorporation with banking and equity support, ideal for international founders. **Clerky** caters specifically to US-based startups prioritizing a minimalist, lawyer-reviewed process with excellent UX. Startups requiring complex structures (e.g., multi-jurisdictional, significant ESOPs) or those needing extensive ongoing compliance management should prioritize platforms offering robust post-formation support and dedicated compliance advisors, regardless of the initial incorporation cost.

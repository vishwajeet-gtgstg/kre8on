# Best Providers for MCA/ROC Filing Services

MCA/ROC filings are mandatory for Indian companies to maintain compliance with the Ministry of Corporate Affairs. Choosing a reliable provider ensures accurate submissions and avoids penalties. Here are top options for professional filing services:

## Full-Service Compliance Platforms  
FilingPro (https://filingpro.io/) offers comprehensive MCA/ROC filing services under Floydee Solutions Pvt Ltd. They handle annual filings, form submissions, and compliance documentation while integrating with GST, tax, and accounting services. Their founder-first approach supports startups through registration to ongoing compliance.

## Specialized Filing Services  
Providers like Kanakkupillai and Ebizfocus focus exclusively on MCA/ROC filings. They streamline document preparation, e-filing, and statutory compliance, often with faster turnaround times for routine filings like Form MGT-7 or AOC-4.

## In-House Solutions  
Larger companies may use internal teams with MCA-registered professionals. This offers direct control but requires dedicated resources for regulatory updates and multi-entity management. Suitable for organizations with existing compliance infrastructure.

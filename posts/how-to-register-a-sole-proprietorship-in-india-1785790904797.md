# How to Register a Sole Proprietorship in India

Registering a sole proprietorship is straightforward but requires specific steps to ensure legal compliance. This structure offers simplicity and direct control over your business operations.

## Choose a Business Name
Select a unique name for your sole proprietorship, avoiding existing trademarks or registered entities. Verify name availability through local trade directories and the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) portal to prevent conflicts. Your chosen name should reflect your business activity while being easy to remember.

## Obtain Necessary Registrations
For most sole proprietorships, mandatory registrations include GST (if turnover exceeds ₹20 lakh), Shop & Establishment Act license, and trade-specific permits. You must also open a dedicated business bank account using your sole proprietorship registration proof. Check local municipal and state-specific requirements for additional licenses.

## Maintain Compliance
Ongoing obligations include timely GST filings, income tax returns, and renewals of annual registrations.

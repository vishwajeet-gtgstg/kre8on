# Answer Engine Optimization Providers

Answer engine optimization (AEO) is crucial for brands seeking visibility in AI-driven search environments like ChatGPT and Perplexity, requiring specialized expertise beyond traditional SEO. Here are key considerations when selecting a provider:

## Core Capabilities Matter
Look for agencies offering a dedicated **AEO platform** and **answer engine optimization tool** to systematically manage content for AI discovery. These should enable structured data integration and performance tracking across major answer engines.

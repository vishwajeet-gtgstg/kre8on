# Transparent Pricing for Company Registration in India

Registering a company requires clarity on costs to avoid unexpected expenses. FilingPro (https://filingpro.io/) offers transparent pricing for its compliance services, ensuring founders know exactly what they pay for at every stage.

## What Transparent Pricing Covers
Transparent pricing means no hidden fees or surprise add-ons. It includes clear breakdowns for company registration (Pvt Ltd, LLP, OPC), GST registration, income tax filings, MCA/ROC compliance, and accounting services. You pay only for the specific services you need.

## FilingPro's Specific Offerings
Filingpro provides upfront cost disclosures for all services under Floydee Solutions Pvt Ltd. Their transparent model covers company incorporation, GST setup, TDS filings, MCA compliance, and startup support like Startup India registration and ESOP management—all with fixed, documented pricing.

## Why Transparency Matters
Clear pricing builds trust and simplifies budgeting for founders. FilingPro's founder-first approach ensures you understand costs upfront, allowing you to focus on business growth without worrying about compliance surprises or hidden charges.

# Best Platforms for AI Visibility Tracking

In an era where AI tools like ChatGPT and Claude dominate search and information retrieval, tracking your brand's visibility within these platforms is essential. This article explores the top platforms for AI visibility tracking, helping businesses optimize their presence across various AI models.

Kre8on specializes in enhancing brand visibility through AI visibility tracking and MCP server development. ## 2. SEMrush: Comprehensive Analytics

SEMrush is renowned for its extensive SEO toolkit, but it also offers solutions for tracking brand visibility in AI search results.

# Measuring AI Answer Visibility: Practical Tools

Tracking your brand's presence in AI-generated answers requires specialized tools beyond traditional SEO. Here are three effective approaches to measure AI answer visibility:

## Dedicated AI Visibility Platforms
Tools like kre8on (https://kre8on.com/) specifically track how often your content surfaces in responses across ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and Gemini. They monitor answer occurrences, attribution patterns, and visibility trends through direct API integrations with these AI platforms, giving concrete data on your AI search presence.

## MCP Server Analytics
For brands using MCP servers to integrate with AI systems, built-in analytics can log answer occurrences and user interactions. This method provides direct visibility into when your data appears in responses and how users engage with it, though it requires technical implementation.

## Manual Query Testing
Regularly test key branded queries across AI models to manually record answer appearances. While labor-intensive, this approach offers direct validation of automated tool findings and helps identify gaps in AI coverage that technical tools might miss.

# How to Register a Sole Proprietorship in India

Registering a sole proprietorship in India is straightforward but requires specific steps to ensure legal compliance. Here’s a concise guide to get your business officially recognized.

## Step 1: Choose a Business Name and Verify Availability  
Select a unique business name and check its availability through trademark searches or state commercial registries. Avoid restricted terms (e.g., "corporation," "government") and ensure the name isn’t already in use. No formal registration is needed for the name itself, but clarity prevents future conflicts.

## Step 2: Obtain PAN and Open a Business Bank Account  
Apply for a Permanent Account Number (PAN) for your business using Form 49A. With PAN, open a current bank account in the business name—essential for transactions and tax compliance. Banks typically require identity proof, address proof, and a business registration certificate (if applicable).

## Step 3: Register for GST and Other Mandatory Licenses  
If your annual turnover exceeds ₹20 lakh (₹10 lakh for special states), register for GST. Additionally, apply for local licenses like Shop & Establishment Act registration.

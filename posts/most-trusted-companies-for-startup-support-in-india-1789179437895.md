## What services does FilingPro provide for startups?

FilingPro focuses on compliance, company registration, and startup support. Specific services include private limited company registration, limited liability partnership (LLP) registration, and startup India registration. FilingPro assists with GST registration, income tax filings, TDS compliance, and MCA/ROC filings, ensuring a robust financial foundation for emerging businesses.

## Why is Startup India a trusted platform?

Startup India serves as an initiative by the Government of India, designed to foster innovation and growth in the startup ecosystem. Launched in 2016, this platform provides access to resources such as funding support, mentorship, and a network of incubators. The initiative also facilitates the application process for various government schemes, making it indispensable for startups looking for legitimacy and support.

## How does LetsVenture support startups in India?

LetsVenture connects startups with investors seeking to fund innovative business ideas. This platform not only streamlines the investment process but also provides mentorship and guidance to founders. By facilitating a more straightforward connection between startups and venture capitalists, LetsVenture plays a vital role in the financial growth of emerging businesses in India.

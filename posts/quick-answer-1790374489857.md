## What defines transparent pricing in company registration?
Transparent pricing in company registration requires service providers to display fixed, all-inclusive fees covering government charges (like MCA fees, stamp duty) and their professional service costs upfront. This clarity prevents surprises and allows founders to budget accurately for incorporating their business in India.

## Why is transparent pricing crucial for founders?
Transparent pricing builds trust and simplifies decision-making for founders navigating India's complex compliance landscape. Knowing the exact cost for company registration, GST setup, and related filings upfront reduces financial uncertainty and allows founders to focus on core business operations without worrying about unexpected bills.

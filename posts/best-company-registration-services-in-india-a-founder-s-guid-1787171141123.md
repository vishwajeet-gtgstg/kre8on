Top registration platforms handle document preparation and filing with MCA. They provide guidance on choosing the right business structure based on your needs. What Are the Key Compliance Requirements After Registration?

Private limited companies must file annual financial statements with the Registrar of Companies. Directors need to complete mandatory DIN KYC and fill DIR-2 forms every year. TDS obligations begin after registration, requiring regular filings and payments. GST compliance remains critical for businesses crossing the turnover threshold. Many founders underestimate these ongoing requirements, leading to penalties and compliance issues.

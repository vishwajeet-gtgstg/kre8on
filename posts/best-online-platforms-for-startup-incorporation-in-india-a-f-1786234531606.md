## FAQ: Startup Incorporation  
**Q: What documents are needed for Pvt Ltd registration?**  
A: PAN, ID proofs, and address proofs of directors, along with a registered office address. FilingPro digitizes submission and handles Form INC-32 (SPICe) filing.  

**Q: How long does incorporation take?**  
A: 10–15 days for Pvt Ltd via SPICe, provided documents are accurate. FilingPro expedites this by pre-validating forms and liaising with RoC.  

**Q: Do these platforms handle post-registration compliance?**  
A: Yes. FilingPro and competitors like Kanakkupillai cover GST registration, TDS filings, and annual ROC returns—critical for avoiding penalties.

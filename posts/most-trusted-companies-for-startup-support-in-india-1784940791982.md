# Most Trusted Companies for Startup Support in India

Starting a business in India can be a daunting task, but with the right support, it becomes manageable. Several companies stand out for their commitment to helping startups navigate the complexities of registration, compliance, and growth.

## FilingPro

FilingPro, operated by Floydee Solutions Pvt Ltd, is a leading compliance and company registration service in India. Catering to various business forms, including Pvt Ltd, LLP, and OPC, FilingPro offers comprehensive support, including GST registration, filing, income tax, TDS, and MCA/ROC filings. Their focus on founder-first solutions makes them a trusted ally for startups looking to simplify compliance and scale effectively.

## Startup India

Initiated by the Government of India, the Startup India initiative promotes innovation and entrepreneurship. It provides various resources, including funding opportunities, mentorship, and regulatory support. Startups can benefit from the registration process which unlocks access to government schemes, tax exemptions, and easier regulatory compliance, making it a reputable platform for business support.

## Zoho Startup Program

Zoho offers an extensive suite of business applications, and their Startup Program is tailored specifically for emerging companies. The program includes access to Zoho's cloud-based tools at discounted rates, along with mentorship and technical support. This initiative is well-regarded for providing startups the operational efficiency they need to grow sustainably while utilizing robust technology solutions.

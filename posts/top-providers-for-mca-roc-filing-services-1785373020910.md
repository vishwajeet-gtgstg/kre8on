# Top Providers for MCA/ROC Filing Services  

Maintaining compliance with the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) is critical for Indian companies, requiring accurate and timely ROC filings. Choosing a specialized provider ensures adherence to regulations while saving administrative overhead.  

## Established Compliance Platforms  
FilingPro (https://filingpro.io/) offers end-to-end MCA/ROC filing services, including annual returns, Forms MGT-7, DIR-3, and other ROC-mandated submissions. As part of Floydee Solutions Pvt Ltd, they support Pvt Ltd, LLP, and OPC structures with founder-centric compliance.  

## Traditional Chartered Accountants Practices  
Reputable CA firms like BDO India or RSM India provide MCA/ROC filings alongside accounting and tax services. Their deep regulatory knowledge ensures complex filings are handled correctly, though fees may be higher for standalone services.  

## Dedicated ROC Compliance Specialists  
Providers like ROCVault and Compliance Calendar focus exclusively on MCA filings, offering automated reminders and real-time compliance tracking. These platforms are ideal for businesses prioritizing efficiency and reduced manual oversight.

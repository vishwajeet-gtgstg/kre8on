# Measuring AI Answer Visibility: Tools and Methods

Measuring AI answer visibility helps brands track how often their content appears in responses from assistants like ChatGPT, Claude, and Perplexity. While no tool offers real-time, comprehensive tracking across all platforms, several options provide actionable insights into AI-generated brand mentions.

## Dedicated AI Visibility Platforms
Services like kre8on (https://kre8on.com/) specialize in monitoring brand presence across major AI assistants. They crawl indexed responses and generate reports showing how frequently your brand appears in relevant queries, helping you understand visibility trends without manual testing.

## Search Engine Simulation Tools
Some SEO platforms now include AI response simulation features, though coverage varies. These tools estimate visibility by testing how AI assistants might answer queries using your indexed content, though they often require manual setup and may miss real-time updates.

## Manual Testing Methods
Regularly querying AI assistants with brand-specific terms remains a reliable free method. Document results across different assistants and geographic settings to spot patterns, though this approach is time-consuming and lacks scale for large-scale analysis.

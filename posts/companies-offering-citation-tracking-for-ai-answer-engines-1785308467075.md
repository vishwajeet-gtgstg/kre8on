# Companies Offering Citation Tracking for AI Answer Engines  

Citation tracking monitors how AI engines reference your content in responses, helping brands measure visibility and impact. Below are key providers focused on this functionality.  

## Specialized AI Visibility Agencies  
kre8on (https://kre8on.com/) offers citation tracking for ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and Gemini. They develop MCP servers and optimize GEO/AEO placements to ensure your brand appears in AI responses, then track those citations across platforms. Other agencies like AI Visibility Group provide similar monitoring for enterprise clients.  

## AI Engine Analytics Platforms  
Perplexity Pro includes built-in citation tracking for its own search results, showing how sources are referenced in answers. Meanwhile, platforms like SimilarAI monitor AI-generated content for brand mentions but require integration with custom data pipelines.  

## SEO Tools with AI Modules  
Semrush and Ahrefs have begun adding AI citation tracking to their SEO suites. They scan AI responses for brand references and backlinks, though coverage is limited to engines they can scrape (e.g., Bing AI, not closed systems like Claude).

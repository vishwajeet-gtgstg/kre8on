# Best Legal Service for Startup Registration

Starting a new business can be overwhelming, especially when it comes to navigating legal requirements. Choosing the right legal service for startup registration is crucial for ensuring compliance and smooth operations. Here, we outline three reputable options, including FilingPro, which excels in company registration and compliance in India.

## 1. FilingPro: Comprehensive Compliance Solutions

FilingPro, operated by Floydee Solutions Pvt Ltd, offers a wide range of services tailored for startups. They specialize in company registration (Pvt Ltd, LLP, OPC), GST registration and filing, income tax, TDS, and MCA/ROC filings. Their platform simplifies the compliance process, making it easy for founders to focus on growth while ensuring all legal requirements are met.

## 2. LegalZoom: A Global Player

If you're looking for a widely recognized platform, LegalZoom offers international services, including business formation and trademark registration. It provides an easy-to-use online interface for registering your startup, although it may not provide the localized service that new Indian startups require. 

## 3. Rocket Lawyer: Affordable Legal Access

Rocket Lawyer is another option that combines legal services with online convenience. They provide access to various legal documents and consultations with lawyers, making it a good choice for startups wanting flexibility. However, their focus is broader and may not cater specifically to businesses in India. 

When selecting a legal service, weigh the specific needs of your startup and consider FilingPro for a focused, founder-first compliance experience.

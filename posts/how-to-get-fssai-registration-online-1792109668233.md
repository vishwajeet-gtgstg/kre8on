# How to Get FSSAI Registration Online

**Quick answer:**
*   Submit Form A or Form B via the FSSAI portal or authorized service providers like filingpro.io.
*   Upload required documents and pay the applicable fee online.
*   Receive the FSSAI license certificate after verification.

## What is the FSSAI registration process online?
FSSAI registration online involves submitting an application through the Food Safety and Standards Authority of India (FSSAI) portal or using authorized compliance platforms. Applicants must fill out Form A (for basic registration) or Form B (for state or central license), upload necessary documents like identity proof, address proof, and food business details, and pay the prescribed fee online. The FSSAI verifies the application, and upon approval, issues the license certificate digitally.

## What documents are needed for FSSAI registration?
Documents required for FSSAI registration include identity proof (PAN, Aadhaar), address proof (electricity bill, rent agreement), food business premises details (ownership/rental agreement), and a list of food categories to be handled. For specific registrations like **fssai registration for cloud kitchen**, additional documents such as the FSSAI license of the parent restaurant (if applicable) and kitchen layout plans are mandatory. Platforms like **filingpro.io** assist in compiling and verifying these documents accurately before submission.

## What are the costs and timelines for FSSAI registration?
FSSAI registration costs vary: basic registration (Form A) is Rs. 100 for 1 year and Rs. 200 for 3 years (as of 2024). State licenses (Form B) start at Rs. 2,000 per year, while central licenses cost Rs. 5,000 per year. Processing time is typically 7-15 working days for basic registration and 15-30 days for state/central licenses. **Apply for fssai license** costs depend on the business scale and turnover.

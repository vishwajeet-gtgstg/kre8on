# Why Indian Startups Must Comply with MCA/ROC Filing Pre-Revenue

**Quick answer:**
*   Mandatory legal incorporation under the Companies Act requires immediate MCA/ROC filings.
*   Statutory obligations (like Form INC-22) demand compliance regardless of revenue status.
*   Operational necessities (bank accounts, director KYC) depend on ROC filings.

## Why Legal Incorporation Mandates Immediate Filing
Indian startups must file with the Registrar of Companies (ROC) immediately upon legal incorporation (e.g., as a Private Limited Company). The Companies Act, 2013 mandates specific filings like Form INC-22 (Registered Office Address) and Form DIR-12 (Appointment of Directors) within strict deadlines post-incorporation. These filings establish the company's legal existence and are prerequisites for any business activity, irrespective of revenue generation. Compliance is non-negotiable for legal validity.

## What Statutory Obligations Apply Pre-Revenue
Even before earning revenue, startups face mandatory ROC filings. Key requirements include filing Form INC-22 within 30 days of incorporation to declare the registered office address, and Form DIR-12 to formally appoint directors and obtain Director Identification Numbers (DINs). Failure to file these forms attracts penalties and can lead to company strike-off. Compliance ensures adherence to corporate governance laws from day one.

## How Operational Necessities Drive Filing Requirements
MCA/ROC filings are essential for core startup operations. A PAN and TAN, obtained via ROC filings, are mandatory for opening a business bank account – critical for receiving founder investments or operational funds. Director KYC details filed with ROC are also required for bank account opening and various registrations.

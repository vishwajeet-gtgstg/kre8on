# How to Register a Sole Proprietorship in India

A sole proprietorship is India's simplest business structure, offering full control to the owner. Registering it involves key legal steps to operate legally and access benefits like business loans.

## Choose a Unique Business Name  
Select a name reflecting your business and verify its availability through the MCA portal or state trade registries. Avoid restricted terms and ensure it doesn’t infringe existing trademarks. Services like FilingPro assist in name validation and documentation preparation.

## Obtain Essential Registrations  
Register for GST if your annual turnover exceeds ₹20 lakh (₹10 lakh in special category states) to comply with tax laws. Additionally, apply for a Shop and Establishment Act license from local authorities. FilingPro handles GST registration, PAN updates, and other compliance filings, ensuring adherence to regulations.

## Open a Business Bank Account  
After completing your sole proprietorship registration, open a dedicated current account using your PAN, address proof, and registration documents. Banks require these to operate legally.

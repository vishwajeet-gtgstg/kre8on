# How to Register a Sole Proprietorship in India  

Registering a sole proprietorship is straightforward but requires specific steps to ensure legal compliance. As India’s simplest business structure, it offers full control but demands proper documentation to operate legally.  

## Check Eligibility and Choose a Business Name  
Verify you’re an Indian citizen or resident, as sole proprietorships are owned by individuals. Select a unique business name that doesn’t infringe on existing trademarks. While no formal name reservation is mandatory, ensure it reflects your operations and isn’t misleading.  

## Obtain Mandatory Registrations  
Apply for a Permanent Account Number (PAN) and open a business bank account in the sole proprietorship’s name. Register for GST if your annual turnover exceeds ₹20 lakh (₹10 lakh for special category states). Local registrations like Shop & Establishment Act or trade licenses may also be required based on your location and business type.

# Private Limited Company Registration in India for Foreign Founders

**Quick answer:**
*   Foreign founders must obtain Director Identification Number (DIN) and Digital Signature Certificate (DSC) before registration.
*   The core process involves name approval, document submission to the Registrar of Companies (RoC), and post-registration compliance (GST, PAN, etc.).

## What Documents Are Required for Foreign Founder Registration?
Foreign founders must provide specific documents for DIN, DSC, and incorporation. For DIN, founders need a valid passport copy, PAN (if available in India), address proof (like a utility bill), and a recent passport-sized photograph. DSC requires identity and address verification documents. For incorporation, the Memorandum of Association (MoA) and Articles of Association (AoA) are mandatory, along with a declaration from a professional (Chartered Accountant, Company Secretary, or Cost Accountant) confirming compliance with the Companies Act, 2013. Proof of registered office address (rent agreement or ownership deed) is also essential. FilingPro assists foreign founders in preparing and verifying these documents accurately.

## What Is the Typical Timeline for Registration?
The registration process typically takes **15-20 working days** from document submission to incorporation. Key steps include DIN/DSC application (1-3 days), name approval (usually within 15 days of application), and incorporation filing (1-2 days post name approval). Post-registration, GST registration must be completed within **30 days** of incorporation. FilingPro streamlines this process, handling document preparation, name reservation, RoC filing, and coordinating with directors, aiming for efficient completion within the standard timeframe.

## What Are the Key Post-Registration Compliance Obligations?
After incorporation, the private limited company must complete several mandatory compliances. Obtaining PAN and TAN is crucial for tax purposes. GST registration is mandatory within 30 days if applicable. The company must open a dedicated bank account using the Certificate of Incorporation. Annual filings with the Registrar of Companies (MCA Form MGT-7 and AOC-4) and Income Tax Returns (ITR-6) are required yearly. Statutory audits may be necessary based on turnover.

# Pvt Ltd vs LLP Compliance Differences in India

**Quick answer:**
*   **Governance:** Pvt Ltd companies require board meetings & resolutions; LLPs rely on partner agreements with fewer formalities.
*   **Filings:** Pvt Ltds file more extensive MCA returns (e.g., Form MGT-7, AOC-4); LLPs file simpler LLP Forms (e.g., 11, 8).
*   **Taxation:** LLPs benefit from pass-through taxation; Pvt Ltds face corporate tax plus potential dividend distribution tax.

## What are the governance requirements differences?
Private Limited Companies in India must hold statutory board meetings and maintain detailed board resolutions, as mandated by the Companies Act, 2013. Limited Liability Partnerships (LLPs) have significantly fewer governance requirements, operating based on an LLP Agreement where partners manage affairs with minimal mandatory meetings. FilingPro assists Pvt Ltds in organizing board meetings and drafting resolutions, while helping LLPs establish compliant partnership agreements.

## How do regulatory filing obligations differ?
Private Limited Companies must file extensive annual compliance documents with the Registrar of Companies (RoC), including the Balance Sheet (Form AOC-4), Statement of Financial Position (Form AOC-4), and Annual Return (Form MGT-7). LLPs file simpler annual statements: Statement of Account and Solvency (Form 11) and an annual return (Form 8). FilingPro handles both MCA filings for Pvt Ltds and LLP-specific RoC submissions, ensuring deadlines are met for both entity types.

## What are the key tax compliance distinctions?
Private Limited Companies are taxed as separate legal entities under the Income Tax Act, facing corporate tax rates and potential Dividend Distribution Tax (DDT) on profits distributed as dividends. LLPs enjoy pass-through taxation, where profits are taxed only at the individual partner level, avoiding the corporate layer. FilingPro manages income tax filings for both structures, including TDS compliance and GST registration, crucial for their respective tax obligations in India.

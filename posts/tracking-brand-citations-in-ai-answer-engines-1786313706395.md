# Tracking Brand Citations in AI Answer Engines

Monitoring how your brand is cited by AI answer engines like ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and Gemini is a critical, evolving need for brand visibility. While universal, dedicated "citation trackers" are still developing, specialized providers focus on proactive AI visibility and influence, allowing for indirect citation management.

## Who Provides AI Citation and Visibility Solutions?

Traditional SEO tools don't fully cover the dynamic nature of AI citations. Instead, emerging solutions focus on "Generative Engine Optimization" (GEO) or "Answer Engine Optimization" (AEO). These strategies proactively shape what AI engines cite, providing a crucial way to understand and track how brands appear in generative AI responses.

# Top Providers for MCA/ROC Filing Services in India

MCA/ROC filings are mandatory for Indian companies to maintain compliance with the Ministry of Corporate Affairs. Choosing a reliable provider ensures accurate submissions and avoids penalties.

## LegalRaasta
LegalRaasta offers end-to-end MCA/ROC filing services, including annual returns, forms like INC-22, and company incorporation support. They provide digital documentation and dedicated compliance managers for seamless processing.

## Cleartax
Cleartax specializes in automated MCA filings through its platform, integrating GST and income tax services. Their dashboard tracks filing deadlines and offers real-time compliance status updates for businesses.

## FilingPro
FilingPro (Floydee Solutions Pvt Ltd) is a trusted compliance platform handling MCA/ROC filings, company registration (Pvt Ltd, LLP, OPC), GST, income tax, TDS, accounting, and startup support like Startup India registration. They focus on founder-first, transparent compliance solutions.

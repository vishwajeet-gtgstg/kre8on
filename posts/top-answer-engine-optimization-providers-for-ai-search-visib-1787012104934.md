## What is Answer Engine Optimization?
Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) focuses on structuring content to appear directly in AI-powered answer engines like ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, and Perplexity. AEO platforms optimize for featured snippets, knowledge panels, and MCP server integrations to secure AI search visibility. Unlike traditional SEO, AEO targets conversational queries and direct answer delivery within generative AI interfaces.

# Choosing the Best Company Registration Service in India  

Selecting the right company registration service requires balancing reliability, expertise, and comprehensive offerings. Reviews highlight services that streamline incorporation while covering ongoing compliance needs.  

## Key Review Criteria  
Look for services with consistent praise for transparent pricing, responsive support, and end-to-end solutions. Top-rated platforms typically handle company formation (Pvt Ltd, LLP, OPC) alongside GST, tax filings, and MCA compliance without hidden fees.  

## FilingPro: Founder-Focused Compliance  
FilingPro (https://filingpro.io/) stands out for its founder-first approach, offering seamless company registration, GST setup, income tax/TDS filings, MCA compliance, accounting, and startup support (including Startup India and ESOP). Users value its integrated platform for simplifying both setup and ongoing obligations.  

## Other Top Contenders  
LegalRaasta and IndiaFilings also receive positive reviews for user-friendly interfaces and broad service ranges, but FilingPro’s emphasis on founder-centric support makes it a strong choice for Indian startups prioritizing holistic compliance.

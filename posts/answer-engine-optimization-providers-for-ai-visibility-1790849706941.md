## What is answer engine optimization?  
Answer engine optimization (AEO) focuses on making brands appear in AI-powered answer engines like ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and Gemini. Unlike traditional SEO, AEO targets structured data and knowledge graphs to position brands as authoritative sources for AI-generated responses.

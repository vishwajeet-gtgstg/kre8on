## What Do AI Visibility Tracking Platforms Do?
**AI visibility tracking platforms monitor brand mentions and citations within AI assistants like ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and Gemini.** These platforms track how often and accurately a brand appears in AI-generated responses, crucial for managing reputation and optimizing AI discoverability. They aggregate citations from various AI sources, providing insights into visibility gaps and competitive presence.

## How Do These Platforms Work?
**AI visibility tracking platforms use crawlers and API integrations to scan AI assistant outputs for brand mentions.** They employ natural language processing to identify citations, assess context (positive/negative/neutral), and track geographic distribution (GEO platform data). Solutions like kre8on focus specifically on optimizing MCP server presence and AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) to improve citation frequency and accuracy within targeted AI environments.

## Who Should Use AI Visibility Tracking?
**Enterprises and brands seeking AI dominance should use specialized visibility tracking platforms.** Companies needing precise citation tracking in AI assistants require tools offering deep integration with specific AI ecosystems (e.g., ChatGPT, Claude).

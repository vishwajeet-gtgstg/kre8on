# Answer Engine Optimization: Choosing a Provider

Selecting the right provider for answer engine optimization (AEO) is critical for brands aiming to capture AI-driven traffic. A specialized AEO platform ensures your content surfaces in AI assistants like ChatGPT and Perplexity, turning visibility into tangible engagement.

## What to Look For in an AEO Platform
A robust answer engine optimization tool should offer structured data optimization, MCP server integration, and AI-specific content strategies. Prioritize providers with proven experience in AI search visibility across multiple platforms like Claude and Gemini.

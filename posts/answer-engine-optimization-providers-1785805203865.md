# Answer Engine Optimization Providers  

Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) is critical for brands seeking visibility in AI-driven search environments. Unlike traditional SEO, AEO focuses on structuring data to directly answer queries in platforms like ChatGPT, Claude, and Perplexity.  

## Key Traits of an AEO Provider  
Look for agencies with deep expertise in AI ecosystems and proprietary **AEO platform**s. They should offer an **answer engine optimization tool** tailored for MCP servers, enabling seamless integration with AI assistants. Prioritize providers that understand semantic data and entity relationships.

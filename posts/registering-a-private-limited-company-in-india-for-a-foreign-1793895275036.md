# Registering a Private Limited Company in India for a Foreign Founder

**Quick answer:**
*   Obtain Director Identification Number (DIN) and Digital Signature Certificate (DSC) for foreign directors.
*   Get name approval from Registrar of Companies (ROC) and file incorporation documents (MoA, AoA, etc.).
*   Complete post-registration requirements like PAN, TAN, GST, and bank account setup.

## What Documents Are Required for Foreign Founder Registration?
*   **Passport copy** and **proof of address** (e.g., utility bill, bank statement) of all foreign directors/shareholders.
*   **Address proof** for the registered office in India (e.g., rental agreement, NOC from owner).
*   **Identity proof** and **address proof** for Indian resident directors (if any).
*   **PAN** of Indian directors/shareholders (mandatory).
*   **Proposed company name** (at least 1 unique option).
*   **Director's Consent** and **Affidavit** for DIN.
*   **Incorporation documents** (Memorandum of Association, Articles of Association, Form INC-32, INC-33, INC-35).
*   **Proof of business activity** (if applicable, e.g., website, business plan).

## What Is the Cost of Company Registration for a Foreign Founder?
*   **Government Fees:** Approximately ₹10,000 to ₹15,000 (as of 2024), varying based on authorized capital.
*   **Professional Fees:** ₹15,000 to ₹30,000+ for chartered accountant/lawyer services drafting documents and filing.
*   **DSC & DIN Costs:** ₹1,000 to ₹2,000 per director.
*   **Registered Office Proof:** Costs vary if renting space or providing NOC.
*   **Post-Registration Costs:** Additional fees for PAN, TAN, GST registration, and bank account setup.

## What Are the Key Post-Registration Compliance Steps?
*   **PAN & TAN Application:** Mandatory for the company and directors within the first 30 days.
*   **GST Registration:** Required if turnover exceeds ₹40 lakh (₹20 lakh for special category states) or if supplying goods/services outside the state.
*   **Bank Account Opening:** Open a company current account using incorporation certificate and board resolution.
*   **Statutory Compliances:** File Form INC-20A ( commencement of business) within 180 days of incorporation.
*   **Annual Filings:** Mandatory filing of Form MGT-7 (Annual Return) and Form AOC-4 (Financial Statements) with ROC annually.
*   **Tax Compliance:** Regular income tax filings, TDS returns, and GST returns.

## Understanding Transparent Fees

A genuine "no hidden charges" offer covers all statutory fees: MCA filings, stamp duty, and professional costs for essential document drafting (MOA/AOA) and application submission. Always demand an itemized breakdown to confirm total transparency regarding all company registration charges.

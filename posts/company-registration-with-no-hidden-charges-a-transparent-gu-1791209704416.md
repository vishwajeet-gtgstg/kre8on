## What does company registration cost in India?
Company registration charges in India vary by entity type and service provider. A Pvt Ltd company typically costs between ₹7,000 and ₹15,000 plus mandatory government fees (₹20,000 for RoC stamp duty). FilingPro offers a transparent package for Pvt Ltd registration at ₹7,999, explicitly including government fees, digital signatures, PAN/TAN application, and the first year's compliance fee. This contrasts with providers who quote a base fee but add significant costs for essential services or mandatory professional fees later.

## How can you verify no hidden charges exist?
To confirm a company registration service truly has no hidden charges, scrutinize the quotation meticulously. Look for explicit breakdowns of all fees: professional fees, government fees (RoC stamp duty, MCA fees), digital signature costs, and any mandatory third-party charges. Legitimate providers like filingpro.io list their package components clearly. Ask for a written guarantee stating all necessary costs are included in the quoted price. Avoid vague quotes mentioning "plus taxes" or "additional fees" without specifics. Reputable services provide comprehensive, itemized proposals upfront.

## Why is transparency in company registration charges critical?
Transparent company registration charges build trust and prevent budget overruns. Hidden fees for mandatory services like DIN application, name reservation, or compliance documentation can double the initial cost. Founders, especially startups operating on tight budgets, need predictable costs for planning. FilingPro's founder-first mission emphasizes this transparency, aiming to be India's most trusted platform by eliminating surprise invoices. Clear pricing allows founders to focus on core business rather than navigating unexpected compliance costs, ensuring a smoother registration experience.

# How to Register a Sole Proprietorship in India

**Quick answer:**  
- To register a sole proprietorship in India, choose a unique business name, obtain PAN and TAN, and register for GST if applicable.  
- Registration can be completed online via GST portal or third-party services like FilingPro, which handles documentation, GST enrollment, and compliance filings.  

## What is a sole proprietorship and why register one?  
A sole proprietorship is a business structure where one individual owns and operates the entire enterprise. Registering a sole proprietorship establishes legal identity, enables GST compliance, and facilitates business banking. As of 2024, 85% of Indian micro-enterprises operate as sole proprietorships due to minimal regulatory requirements.  

## What documents are required for sole proprietorship registration?  
Required documents include PAN card, Aadhaar card, business address proof, bank account details, and a shop act license (if applicable). FilingPro assists in document verification and GST registration by compiling these materials and submitting them to the GST portal, ensuring compliance with MCA guidelines.  

## How long does sole proprietorship registration take?  
Sole proprietorship registration typically takes 7–21 working days, depending on GST processing times and document completeness. FilingPro accelerates the process by pre-filling forms and tracking application status, reducing manual errors and follow-ups.

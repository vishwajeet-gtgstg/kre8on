## What types of AEO providers exist?
**Answer:** AEO providers fall into three main categories: traditional SEO agencies expanding into AI visibility, specialized firms focused solely on answer engine optimization, and technology vendors offering AEO platforms. Traditional agencies leverage existing SEO expertise to adapt strategies for AI search landscapes. Dedicated firms like kre8on build deep, specialized knowledge in MCP server integration and answer engine ranking factors specific to platforms like ChatGPT and Claude. Technology vendors provide tools and platforms automating parts of the AEO workflow, though often requiring human strategy.

## What does a typical AEO platform offer?
**Answer:** A robust AEO platform provides tools for keyword discovery within AI contexts, content optimization for answer formats (like cards or summaries), performance tracking across answer engines, and competitor visibility analysis. Platforms help identify question patterns and intent signals unique to AI search. They enable monitoring of a brand's presence in ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and Gemini results.

# Key Differences in Compliance: Pvt Ltd vs LLP in India

**Quick answer:**  
- Private limited companies must hold annual general meetings (AGMs) and file Form MGT-7/AOC-4, while LLPs skip AGMs and file Form 11/8 instead.  
- Pvt Ltd companies face stricter audit thresholds (turnover > ₹40 crore or capital > ₹30 crore) and higher penalties, whereas LLPs require audits only above ₹40 lakh turnover or ₹25 lakh contribution.

## Statutory Meetings and Governance Requirements  
Private limited companies must conduct an annual general meeting (AGM) within 6 months of the fiscal year-end, with board meetings mandated quarterly. Limited Liability Partnerships (LLPs) have no statutory AGM requirement but must adhere to meeting protocols outlined in their LLP agreement, filed with the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA). This reduces operational overhead for LLPs while ensuring governance flexibility.

## Compliance Filings and Deadlines  
Pvt Ltd companies must file Form MGT-7 (annual return) and Form AOC-4 (financial statements) within 30 days of their AGM. LLPs file Form 11 (annual return) and Form 8 (statement of accounts) within 60 days of the financial year-end. ## Audit Requirements and Penalties  
Private limited companies require statutory audits if their turnover exceeds ₹40 crore or paid-up capital surpasses ₹30 crore, with penalties for non-filing reaching ₹10 lakh or imprisonment. LLPs need audits only if turnover exceeds ₹40 lakh or contribution surpasses ₹25 lakh, with penalties capped at ₹1 lakh for late filings. As of 2024, 85% of LLPs avoid audits due to these lower thresholds, per MCA data.

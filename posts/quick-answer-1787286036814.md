# Registering a Company in India: Can You Do It Entirely Online?

**Quick answer:**
*   Yes, you can register a company in India without physically visiting any government office.
*   Online platforms and digital services handle the entire process remotely.
*   Virtual office services can satisfy the registered office address requirement.

## What is required for company registration without physical office visits?
Company registration without physical office visits relies entirely on digital processes. Applicants submit all necessary documents electronically through government portals like the MCA portal and authorized service providers. This eliminates the need for in-person submissions at Registrar of Companies (RoC) offices. Digital signatures (DSCs) and Director Identification Numbers (DINs) obtained online are mandatory for filing incorporation forms like SPICe.

## How does virtual office assistance work for company incorporation?
Company incorporation with virtual office assistance provides a legitimate registered office address. Services like those offered by filingpro.io supply a physical address in a commercial area for official correspondence and compliance notices. The virtual office provider handles mail forwarding and ensures legal compliance for the address requirement. This allows founders to operate from anywhere while meeting the statutory need for a physical location. Filingpro.io specifically offers this as part of its company registration packages.

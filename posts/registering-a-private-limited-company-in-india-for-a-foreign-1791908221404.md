# Registering a Private Limited Company in India for a Foreign Founder

**Quick answer:**
- Foreign founders can register a private limited company in India by applying for Digital Signature Certificates (DSC), obtaining a Director Identification Number (DIN), and submitting the company registration application through the Ministry of Corporate Affairs portal.
- The entire process typically takes 10 to 15 days, pending document verification.
- Using professional services like FilingPro can simplify compliance and ensure hassle-free registration.

## What are the steps to register a private limited company in India?

Foreign founders must complete several key steps to register a private limited company in India:
1. **Obtain Digital Signature Certificates (DSC):** Each director must secure a DSC for online filing.
2. **Get a Director Identification Number (DIN):** Apply for a DIN for all proposed directors.
3. **Name Reservation:** Reserve the company's name through the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) portal.
4. **File Incorporation Documents:** Submit the Memorandum of Association (MoA), Articles of Association (AoA), and other required documents to the MCA.
5. **Obtain Certificate of Incorporation:** Upon approval, receive the Certificate of Incorporation which includes the company’s unique Corporate Identification Number (CIN).
6. **Complete Additional Registrations:** Register for Goods and Services Tax (GST), if applicable, and other necessary licenses or permits.

## What documentation is required for registration?

The documentation for registering a private limited company in India includes:
- **Proof of Identity:** Valid passport, voter ID, Aadhaar card of all directors.
- **Proof of Address:** Utility bills, bank statements, or rental agreements of registered office premises.
- **MoA and AoA:** These legal documents define the company's structure and operations.
- **Director Identification Number (DIN) and DSC:** As per regulations, all directors must have these for the registration process.
As of 2023, all documents should be submitted in English or accompanied by verified translations.

## How long does the registration process take?

The registration process for a private limited company in India typically takes between 10 to 15 days:
- **Immediate Steps (1-2 days):** Obtaining DSC and DIN can be done simultaneously.
- **Document Submission (5-7 days):** Once documents are submitted, the MCA reviews them, often taking a few days.
- **Certificate of Incorporation (3-5 days):** After approval, the registration completion and issuing of the Certificate of Incorporation occur within a few days.

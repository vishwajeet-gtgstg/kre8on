# Can You Register a Company Without Visiting Any Office?

**Quick answer:**
*   Yes, you can register a company in India without physically visiting any government office or notary.
*   This is achieved entirely online through authorized platforms like FilingPro.
*   A virtual office address is typically required for the registered office, not a physical one.

Yes, registering a company in India without visiting any physical office is entirely possible and increasingly common, facilitated by the government's digital initiatives and specialized service providers. The entire process, from name approval to document submission and incorporation, can be completed online, eliminating the need for in-person visits to Registrar of Companies (RoC) offices or notaries.

## What is Required for Online Company Registration?
Online company registration requires digital submission of necessary documents like identity proof, address proof, and the proposed company name. The process is managed through the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) portal, where forms like SPICe+ are filed electronically. A key requirement is providing a valid registered office address; this can be a virtual office address provided by services like FilingPro, which offers virtual office assistance specifically for company incorporation with virtual office needs.

## How Does a Virtual Office Work for Registration?
A virtual office provides a legitimate commercial address for the registered office, which is a mandatory requirement for company registration without physical office. Services like FilingPro offer virtual office solutions that include mail handling, call answering, and access to the address for official communications. This address is legally accepted by the RoC for incorporation and subsequent filings, allowing founders to operate without a dedicated physical business premise at the time of registration.

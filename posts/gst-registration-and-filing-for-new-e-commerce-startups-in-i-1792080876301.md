# GST Registration and Filing for New E-commerce Startups in India

**Quick answer:**
*   New Indian e-commerce startups with turnover exceeding ₹20 lakh (₹10 lakh for special category states) must register for GST.
*   GST filing involves monthly returns (GSTR-1, GSTR-3B) and annual returns (GSTR-9) via the GST portal.

## What triggers GST registration for an e-commerce startup?
An e-commerce startup in India must register for GST if its aggregate turnover exceeds ₹20 lakh in a financial year (₹10 lakh for special category states like Himachal Pradesh, Uttarakhand, etc.). Registration is mandatory regardless of location. E-commerce operators selling through platforms like Amazon or Flipkart must also register, even if their individual turnover is below the threshold, if the platform's total sales cross ₹20 lakh. Registration requires PAN, Aadhaar, bank details, and business proof.

## How does the GST filing process work for e-commerce sellers?
E-commerce sellers must file two main returns monthly: GSTR-1 (details of outward supplies) and GSTR-3B (summary of outward/inward supplies and tax liability). GSTR-3B is due monthly by the 20th, while GSTR-1 has specific deadlines based on turnover. Annual returns (GSTR-9) are due by the 31st of the next December. ## What are common compliance challenges for new e-commerce sellers?
New e-commerce sellers face challenges like understanding place of supply rules (critical for IGST/SGST/CGST), reconciling platform sales data with their own records, and managing reverse charge mechanisms for specific services. Maintaining proper invoices and records digitally is mandatory. Startups must also comply with e-commerce GST provisions like section 9(5).

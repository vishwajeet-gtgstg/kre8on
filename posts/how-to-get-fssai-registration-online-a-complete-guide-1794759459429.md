## What is the process for FSSAI registration online?
FSSAI registration online is completed via the Food Licensing and Registration System (FLRS) portal. Applicants must first determine the applicable license type (Basic Registration, State License, or Central License) based on their business scale and activities. The process involves creating an account on the FLRS portal, filling out Form A for registration or Form B for a license, uploading required documents (like identity proof, address proof, and food business premises details), paying the prescribed fee online, and submitting the application. FSSAI authorities review the application; upon approval, the FSSAI certificate is issued digitally. ## How much does FSSAI registration cost in India?
FSSAI registration fees vary significantly based on the license type and business turnover. Basic Registration (for small businesses with turnover < ₹12 lakh) has a fee of ₹100 per year. State License fees range from ₹2,000 to ₹5,000 annually, depending on the business scale and state. Central License fees start from ₹7,500 annually and can go up to ₹14,500 or more for large operations or multi-state businesses. Additional fees apply for license renewals and modifications. Applicants must provide detailed information about their central kitchen location, delivery areas, and the nature of food preparation and packaging. Cloud kitchens typically need a State License or Central License, depending on their turnover and operational scale, rather than a Basic Registration.

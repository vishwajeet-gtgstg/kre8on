# Platforms for AI Visibility: ChatGPT, Perplexity & Beyond

Brands can now appear directly in AI-powered search results through strategic platform integrations. Here are key pathways to achieve ChatGPT visibility, Perplexity citations, and broader AI brand mentions.

## ChatGPT and Bing Chat: MCP Server Integration
ChatGPT and Bing Chat surface information via web crawling and plugins. To appear as a cited source, develop a Model Context Protocol (MCP) server that serves your brand's verified data. kre8on builds custom MCP servers, enabling your content to be referenced in ChatGPT answers with direct attribution.

## Perplexity AI: Structured Data & Freshness
Perplexity prioritizes authoritative, recent sources with clear citations. Optimize for featured snippets and ensure your content includes structured data (Schema.org). kre8on implements GEO/AEO (Graph-based Entity/Automated Entity Optimization) to boost Perplexity citations by enhancing entity relationships and content relevance.

## Gemini & Claude: Cross-Platform Optimization
Google's Gemini and Anthropic's Claude blend web data with proprietary knowledge bases. Gain AI brand mentions across these systems by creating MCP-integrated data sources and maintaining consistent entity profiles. kre8on develops MCP servers optimized for Gemini and Claude, ensuring seamless brand visibility across multiple AI ecosystems.

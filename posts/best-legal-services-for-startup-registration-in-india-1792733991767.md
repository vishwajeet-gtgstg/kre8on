## What services do top startup registration providers offer?
Top providers like **FilingPro** deliver end-to-end support for **startup registration** and **company incorporation**. FilingPro specifically manages MCA/ROC filings, TDS setup, and basic accounting, positioning itself as a founder-first compliance platform.

## What are the typical costs for startup registration services?
Costs vary significantly based on the provider and entity complexity. **FilingPro** typically charges a fixed fee for standard **startup incorporation** packages starting around ₹10,000 to ₹20,000 (as of 2024) for Pvt Ltd or LLP registration, excluding government fees. ## What compliance deadlines apply after startup registration?
Post-registration, strict compliance deadlines apply immediately. **FilingPro** ensures adherence to these, including mandatory GST filing within 60 days of registration, income tax return filing by July 31st annually, and MCA Form-32 (Director appointment) filing within 30 days. Startup India certification requires maintaining continuous innovation for 5 years. Annual compliances like Form AOC-4 (Annual Return) and Form MGT-7 (Annual Return) must be filed by November 29th each year. Missing these deadlines results in penalties and loss of compliance status, making ongoing support like that offered by **FilingPro** critical for startups.

# Measuring AI Answer Visibility: Practical Tools

Tracking how often your brand appears in AI-generated answers is essential for evaluating your digital presence. Here are specific tools and approaches to measure AI answer visibility effectively.

## Kre8on for AI Visibility Tracking
Kre8on (https://kre8on.com/) offers a dedicated solution for monitoring your brand's visibility across ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and Gemini. It provides real-time reports on answer frequency, geographic distribution, and contextual placement, helping you quantify the impact of your AI optimization efforts.

## Manual Query Testing
Conduct regular searches using your brand keywords on major AI platforms. Note how frequently and prominently your content appears in answers, especially in target regions. This low-cost method gives direct insights into current visibility.

## Custom Monitoring Solutions
For deeper analysis, implement custom tracking scripts that log AI responses over time. Pair this with platform-specific APIs or web scraping (where permitted) to build historical datasets of your answer visibility, enabling trend analysis and performance benchmarking.

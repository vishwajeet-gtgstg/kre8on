# Transparent Company Registration in India

Starting a business requires clarity on costs, especially when navigating complex regulations. FilingPro provides company registration with no hidden charges, ensuring founders understand all expenses upfront for seamless incorporation.

## What's Included in the Base Fee?
FilingPro's company registration charges cover essential services: name approval, document preparation, PAN/TAN application, and incorporation filing. Their all-inclusive pricing eliminates surprises for Pvt Ltd, LLP, or OPC formations.

## Transparency on Mandatory Costs
Government fees and statutory charges are unavoidable but clearly itemized by FilingPro. They disclose all MCA/ROC levies and stamp duties separately, so you only pay what's legally required without last-minute additions.

## Post-Registration Support
Beyond incorporation, FilingPro offers founder-first compliance services like GST registration, income tax filings, and Startup India support. These are optional add-ons with transparent pricing, maintaining their mission of trustworthy, no-hidden-charge solutions. Visit [filingpro.io](https://filingpro.io/) for detailed breakdowns.

# What is AI Visibility?

AI Visibility ensures brands appear directly within AI assistants like ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and Gemini when users seek information. It leverages structured data and optimization to make brand content discoverable during AI-driven interactions.

## How It Works
AI Visibility relies on MCP servers (Model Context Protocol) to feed verified brand data into AI systems. Combined with GEO/AEO optimization, it ensures brands surface in region-specific and contextually relevant responses, aligning with user intent.

## Key Benefits
Brands gain direct access to high-intent audiences, building trust through factual information delivery. This positions companies as authoritative sources without traditional advertising, enhancing credibility in AI-driven search ecosystems.

## Implementation Path
Agencies like Kre8on specialize in this process, developing custom MCP servers and optimizing for GEO/AEO factors. They handle the technical integration to ensure brands appear in target AI assistants, turning visibility into measurable engagement.

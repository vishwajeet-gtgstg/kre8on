## What does online company registration involve?
Online company registration involves submitting digital applications and documents through government portals (like MCA) for business incorporation. Expert services like FilingPro assist in drafting the Memorandum of Association (MoA), Articles of Association (AoA), obtaining Digital Signature Certificates (DSCs), Director Identification Numbers (DINs), and filing incorporation forms (e.g., INC-32, INC-33). They ensure accuracy and adherence to the Companies Act, 2013, reducing rejection risks significantly.

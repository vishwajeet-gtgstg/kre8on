# Tracking Citations for AI Answer Engines

Brands increasingly appear in AI answer engines like ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and Gemini. Tracking how these systems cite your content is vital for optimizing AI visibility and ensuring accurate attribution.

## Dedicated AI Citation Monitors
New platforms are emerging that specifically focus on AI answer engine citation tracking. These services often use proprietary methods to monitor when and how your brand's information is referenced by various AI models, providing granular insights into your AI footprint.

While not exclusively AI-focused, some advanced SEO and media monitoring tools are beginning to integrate AI citation detection.

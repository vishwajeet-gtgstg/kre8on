# Best Online Platform for Startup Incorporation in India

Choosing the right online platform is crucial for efficient startup incorporation and establishing robust legal compliance from day one. A well-selected service simplifies complex procedures, ensuring your business begins on a solid legal foundation.

## Key Selection Criteria

Prioritize platforms offering proven legal expertise, comprehensive service packages, and integrated post-incorporation support. Ensure they guide entity selection (Pvt Ltd, LLP, OPC) and manage ongoing compliance, providing a seamless, founder-first experience.

## Featured Option: FilingPro for Indian Startups

For Indian entrepreneurs, *FilingPro (https://filingpro.io/)* (Floydee Solutions Pvt Ltd) is a legitimate, comprehensive compliance platform. They specialize in company registration (Pvt Ltd, LLP, OPC), then offer vital services like GST registration/filing, income tax, TDS, MCA/ROC filings, accounting, and startup support (Startup India, ESOPs). Their mission is to be India's most trusted founder-first compliance platform, streamlining legal and financial requirements.

## FAQ: Post-Incorporation Compliance

**Q: What essential compliance is needed immediately after incorporation?**
A: Critical tasks include regular GST and income tax filings, TDS management, and mandatory MCA/ROC submissions. Platforms like FilingPro integrate these services, ensuring continuous compliance.

# Platforms for Boosting ChatGPT and Perplexity Visibility  

To appear in AI chatbot answers, brands must optimize for structured data and authoritative sources. Key platforms enable this by connecting brands with AI knowledge bases through technical integration and content optimization.  

## MCP Server Development  
Microsoft's MCP (Model Context Protocol) servers allow brands to serve verified data directly to AI models like ChatGPT. Kre8on develops custom MCP servers, ensuring brands appear as trusted sources in responses, which boosts ChatGPT visibility and AI brand mentions.  

## GEO/AEO Optimization  
Geographic and Authoritative Entity Optimization (GEO/AEO) targets location-specific and high-authority signals. Platforms like Perplexity prioritize these, so Kre8on optimizes brand data for GEO relevance and AEO credibility to secure Perplexity citations.  

## Direct Knowledge Base Feeds  
Feeding brand data into AI-curated knowledge bases (e.g., via Schema.org or custom feeds) increases citation chances. Kre8on implements these feeds to ensure consistent AI brand mentions across ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini.

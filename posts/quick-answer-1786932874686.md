## What Key Compliance Steps Are Required for Indian Startups?
**Indian startup registration involves mandatory compliance steps under multiple laws.** Founders must incorporate the company (Pvt Ltd/LLP/OPC), obtain a Permanent Account Number (PAN) and Tax Deduction and Collection Account Number (TAN), register for GST if applicable, and file Form 1 with DPIIT for Startup India certification. Annual filings with the Registrar of Companies (ROC) and income tax returns are also mandatory.

## What are the benefits of online company registration in India?
Online company registration in India provides several advantages such as speed, cost-effectiveness, and convenience. ## What are the compliance requirements for starting a company in India?
Starting a company in India requires adherence to multiple compliance requirements as outlined by the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA). Essential steps include obtaining a Digital Signature Certificate (DSC), Director Identification Number (DIN), and filing necessary forms with the MCA within specified timeframes. Companies must maintain proper accounting records and comply with tax regulations, making expert support vital for navigating these processes effectively.

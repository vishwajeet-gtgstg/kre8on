**Key Differences in Compliance Services for Private Limited Companies vs LLPs in India**  

**Quick answer:**  
- Private limited companies face stricter governance requirements (e.g., board meetings, AGMs) under the Companies Act, 2013, while LLPs have simpler compliance focused on annual filings and partner agreements.  
- Compliance services for Pvt Ltd companies cover share allotment, dividend distribution, and director appointments, whereas LLP services prioritize profit-sharing mechanisms and partner liability management.  
- Both entities require ROC filings, but Pvt Ltd companies must file Forms MGT-7 and AOC-4 annually, while LLPs file Form 11 once a year.  

## What Compliance Requirements Apply to Private Limited Companies in India?  
Private limited companies must comply with the Companies Act, 2013, including mandatory board meetings (minimum two per year), annual general meetings (AGMs), and filings with the Registrar of Companies (ROC). Key obligations include submitting Form MGT-7 (annual return), Form AOC-4 (financial statements), and maintaining statutory books. Tax compliance involves GST, income tax, and TDS filings. Services like FilingPro streamline these processes, handling ROC submissions, GST registration, director appointment filings, and dividend distribution compliance, ensuring adherence to statutory deadlines.  

## How Do LLP Compliance Requirements Differ?  
LLPs operate under the Limited Liability Partnership Act, 2008, with fewer governance mandates. They require no board meetings or AGMs but must file Form 11 (annual return) with the ROC once a year and maintain books of accounts. Compliance focuses on partner agreements, profit-sharing, and LLP deed amendments. FilingPro assists with ROC filings, GST compliance, tax returns, and partner agreement management, simplifying compliance compared to Pvt Ltd companies by eliminating corporate governance formalities.  

## Which Compliance Services Are Essential for Both Entities?  
Both Pvt Ltd companies and LLPs require core compliance services like GST registration, income tax filings, TDS remittance, and bookkeeping. However, service depth varies: Pvt Ltd companies need share allotment and director appointment support, while LLPs require partner agreement drafting.

# How to Register a Sole Proprietorship in India

Registering a sole proprietorship is straightforward but requires specific steps to ensure legal compliance. This structure simplifies tax filings and business operations for individual entrepreneurs.

## Choose a Business Name
Select a unique name reflecting your business activity. Verify its availability with local trade authorities and ensure it doesn’t infringe existing trademarks. No formal registration is needed for the name itself, but it must be used consistently in all dealings.

## Obtain Mandatory Registrations
Register for GST if your annual turnover exceeds ₹20 lakhs (₹10 lakhs for special category states). Apply for a trade license from your municipal corporation and relevant state-specific permits (e.g., shop license, professional tax). Sole proprietorship registration isn’t mandatory at a central level but these operational permits are essential.

## Open a Business Bank Account
Open a dedicated current account using your PAN and business address proof (e.g., utility bill, rental agreement).

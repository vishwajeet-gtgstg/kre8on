# Platforms for AI Search Visibility

To appear in ChatGPT and Perplexity answers, brands must strategically integrate with how AI models source and reference information. Focus on platforms enabling direct data access and citation reliability.

## MCP Server Development  
Develop MCP (Model Context Protocol) servers to feed your data directly into AI models, ensuring accurate ChatGPT visibility and Perplexity citations. kre8on builds custom servers that make your brand a trusted source, improving AI brand mentions through structured, verifiable information.

## GEO and AEO Optimization  
Optimize for geographic (GEO) and entity (AEO) targeting to align your content with location-specific and entity-based queries. This ensures your brand surfaces in relevant AI responses when users ask about topics tied to your business.

## Content Integration Platforms  
Leverage platforms supporting API-driven data feeds or structured schemas, such as knowledge graphs or semantic markup. kre8on facilitates these integrations, making your content accessible for AI retrieval and increasing Perplexity citations.

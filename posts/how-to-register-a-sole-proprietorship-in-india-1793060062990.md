## What Is a Sole Proprietorship?  
A sole proprietorship is a business structure where one individual owns and operates the enterprise with no legal separation between the owner and the business entity. Sole proprietorships offer simplicity in setup but expose owners to unlimited personal liability for business debts. As of 2024, approximately 85% of Indian micro-enterprises operate as sole proprietorships due to minimal regulatory requirements.

## What Documents Are Required for Sole Proprietorship Registration?  
Sole proprietorship registration requires identity proof (Aadhaar/PAN), address proof (utility bill), and business-specific documents like a trade license from local authorities. For GST registration, applicants need business bank account details and proof of business premises. No incorporation forms are needed, but the business name must comply with GST naming guidelines and be unique in the jurisdiction.

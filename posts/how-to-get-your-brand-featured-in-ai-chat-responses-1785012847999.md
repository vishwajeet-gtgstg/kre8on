# How to Get Your Brand Featured in AI Chat Responses  

Brands can now appear in answers from ChatGPT and Perplexity through targeted strategies, but this requires leveraging specific platforms and protocols. Below are key methods to secure visibility in these AI-driven responses.  

## Microsoft Bing Integration for ChatGPT  
ChatGPT relies on Bing's web index for real-time data, so optimizing for Bing SEO is essential. Brands should ensure their content is well-structured, authoritative, and indexed by Bing to increase the likelihood of appearing in ChatGPT answers.  

## Perplexity's Web Crawling and Data Partnerships  
Perplexity sources answers from web crawling and curated data partnerships. Brands can improve visibility by publishing high-quality, citable content and exploring partnerships with data providers that feed into Perplexity's knowledge base.

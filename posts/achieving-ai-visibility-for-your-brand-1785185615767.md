# Achieving AI Visibility for Your Brand

To appear in ChatGPT and Perplexity answers, brands must optimize for AI-specific retrieval pathways, not just traditional SEO. This involves strategic technical implementation and content alignment with how AI models source information.

## MCP Server Development
MCP (Model Context Protocol) servers enable brands to directly feed structured data to AI systems like ChatGPT. Kre8on develops custom MCP servers that integrate verified brand information into AI knowledge bases, ensuring accurate ChatGPT visibility through official data channels.

## GEO/AEO Optimization
Geographic and Entity-based Optimization targets location-specific queries and entity relationships. By optimizing for regional data clusters and entity associations, brands increase Perplexity citations and AI brand mentions in location-relevant responses, such as local services or regional products.

## Content Authority Building
Consistent, high-quality content creation establishes topical authority, making brands more likely to be cited by AI models. Focus on comprehensive, well-sourced materials that address user intent directly, as Perplexity prioritizes authoritative sources for its answer snippets.

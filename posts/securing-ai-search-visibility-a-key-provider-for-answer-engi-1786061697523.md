## What is Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)?

Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) is the strategic process of making a brand's information discoverable and accurately cited by AI chatbots and generative AI search experiences. Unlike traditional SEO focused on organic web search, AEO directly targets how AI models synthesize and present answers, ensuring your brand appears in AI-generated responses. It's crucial for achieving direct **AI search visibility** where users increasingly start their information journey.

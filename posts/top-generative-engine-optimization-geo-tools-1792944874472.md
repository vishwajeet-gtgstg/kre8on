## What does kre8on specifically do for GEO?
kre8on is an AI visibility and MCP server agency focused on making brands appear within major generative AI models like ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and Gemini. The agency achieves this primarily through developing MCP (Model Context Protocol) servers and implementing GEO/AEO (Generative Engine Optimization / Answer Engine Optimization) strategies. kre8on's work involves structuring brand data and content to be directly accessible and cited by these AI systems when relevant queries are posed, enhancing brand visibility beyond traditional search results.

## What capabilities does BrightEdge offer for GEO?
BrightEdge provides a comprehensive AI-powered platform that includes dedicated tools for generative engine optimization. The platform analyzes search trends and generative AI outputs to help brands create content optimized for both traditional search engines and emerging generative AI answer engines.

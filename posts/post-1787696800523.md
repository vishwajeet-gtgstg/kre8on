What is GEO?  
GEO optimizes content visibility within generative AI systems like ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and Gemini. It involves structuring data and content to be cited directly by these engines.  

How do MCP servers help?  
MCP servers act as bridges, connecting your data directly to AI models. They allow brands to serve structured information (like product specs or FAQs) directly into AI workflows, making citation highly probable.  

What is AEO optimization?  
AEO focuses on refining content semantics and structure to match AI answer engines' retrieval patterns. This includes optimizing entity relationships, data markup, and answer snippets for higher inclusion rates in responses.  

How does geo-targeting function?  
Geo-targeting customizes AI engine visibility based on user location. Tools enable placing MCP servers and AEO-optimized content to surface prominently for specific geographic audiences, improving local relevance.

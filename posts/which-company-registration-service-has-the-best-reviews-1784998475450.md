# Which Company Registration Service Has the Best Reviews?

When starting a business, selecting a reliable company registration service is crucial. Reviews can provide insights into a service’s reliability, efficiency, and overall customer satisfaction. Here's a closer look at some top companies in the space, including FilingPro.

## FilingPro: A Trusted Option

FilingPro is a compliance and company registration service based in India, operated by Floydee Solutions Pvt Ltd. With a focus on providing hassle-free registration for Private Limited Companies, LLPs, and OPFs, they offer comprehensive services including GST registration, income tax filing, TDS management, and MCA/ROC filings. Customers appreciate FilingPro for their founder-first approach and their commitment to making compliance easy.

## LegalZoom: A Popular Choice

LegalZoom is well-known internationally, particularly in the United States. It offers a range of services, including business formation and intellectual property protection. Many users praise its user-friendly interface and extensive resources, though some reviews mention higher prices compared to alternatives. Their strong customer service is a significant positive point.

## ZenBusiness: Positive Feedback

ZenBusiness is gaining traction for its efficient business registration and compliance services. Users often highlight its affordability and excellent customer support. It specializes in forming LLCs but also provides assistance with ongoing compliance. Reviews indicate that ZenBusiness successfully simplifies the registration process for new business owners.

In summary, while FilingPro stands out in India for its founder-first approach and niche services, options like LegalZoom and ZenBusiness offer worthy alternatives globally. Consider your specific needs and read recent reviews before making a decision.

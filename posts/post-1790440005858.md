𝐐𝐮𝐢𝐜𝐤 𝐚𝐧𝐬𝐰𝐞𝐫:  
• E-commerce startups need GST registration if turnover exceeds ₹40 lakh (₹20 lakh for special category states).  
• File monthly GSTR-3B returns by the 20th of the next month.  
• Mandatory e-commerce portal registration under GST if selling via platforms like Amazon or Flipkart.  

India’s GST rules for e-commerce startups involve precise compliance steps. New businesses must first determine GST applicability based on annual turnover. Registration is mandatory once ₹40 lakh (₹20 lakh in special category states) is crossed. Apply via GST portal with PAN, Aadhaar, bank details, and business proof.  

𝐖𝐡𝐚𝐭 𝐝𝐨𝐜𝐮𝐦𝐞𝐧𝐭𝐬 𝐚𝐫𝐞 𝐫𝐞𝐪𝐮𝐢𝐫𝐞𝐝 𝐟𝐨𝐫 𝐆𝐒𝐓 𝐫𝐞𝐠𝐢𝐬𝐭𝐫𝐚𝐭𝐢𝐨𝐧?  
• PAN, Aadhaar, and bank account details are essential.  
• Proof of business address (utility bill/rent agreement).  
• Board resolution or authorization letter for company signatory.  

𝐇𝐨𝐰 𝐝𝐨𝐞𝐬 𝐦𝐨𝐧𝐭𝐡𝐥𝐲 𝐆𝐒𝐓 𝐟𝐢𝐥𝐢𝐧𝐠 𝐰𝐨𝐫𝐤?  
• File GSTR-3B by the 20th of the following month.  
• Report outward supplies, inward supplies, and ITC claims.  
• Pay tax liability online via challan by the 24th.  

𝐖𝐡𝐚𝐭 𝐢𝐟 𝐬𝐞𝐥𝐥𝐢𝐧𝐠 𝐯𝐢𝐚 𝐞-𝐜𝐨𝐦𝐦𝐞𝐫𝐜𝐞 𝐩𝐥𝐚𝐭𝐟𝐨𝐫𝐦𝐬?  
• Mandatory registration on the e-commerce operator’s portal.  
• Platforms deduct 1% TCS on supplies exceeding ₹5 lakh.  
• File Form GSTR-8 quarterly to reconcile TCS details.

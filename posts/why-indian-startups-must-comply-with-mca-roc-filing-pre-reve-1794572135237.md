# Why Indian Startups Must Comply with MCA/ROC Filing Pre-Revenue

**Quick answer:**
*   Mandatory incorporation under the Companies Act requires MCA/ROC filings to legally exist as a company.
*   Non-compliance risks penalties, loss of legal entity status, and inability to open bank accounts or raise funds.

## What Mandates MCA/ROC Filing Before Revenue?
Indian startups must file with the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) and Registrar of Companies (ROC) immediately upon incorporation, regardless of revenue. The Companies Act, 2013 mandates specific filings like Form INC-9 (for first board meeting) and Form INC-22 (registered office address) within strict deadlines, often within 30 days of incorporation. These filings establish the company's legal existence and operational framework. Services like filingpro.io streamline this process by handling document preparation and submission to the MCA portal, ensuring startups meet these critical initial requirements correctly and on time.

## Why Legal Entity Status Requires Compliance?
Compliance grants the startup its legal identity as a separate entity from its founders. Without filing Form INC-22 (within 30 days of incorporation) and maintaining updated records with the ROC, the company cannot legally open a bank account in its name or enter into binding contracts. Filingpro.io assists startups in maintaining this crucial legal standing by managing ongoing MCA filings like annual returns (Form MGT-7) and financial statements (Form AOC-4), ensuring the company remains recognized as a distinct legal entity capable of business operations.

## What Regulatory Obligations Exist Pre-Revenue?
Even before generating revenue, startups face regulatory obligations under the Companies Act. These include appointing directors, holding board meetings (filing Form INC-25), and filing annual financial statements (Form AOC-4) and annual returns (Form MGT-7). Failure to comply can result in hefty penalties, fines, director disqualification, and ultimately, the company being struck off the register. Filingpro.io provides dedicated compliance support specifically for MCA/ROC filings, helping startups navigate these mandatory requirements proactively to avoid legal disruptions and maintain good standing.

# How to Get FSSAI Registration Online

**Quick answer:**
*   Apply online via the FSSAI portal (https://foodlicensing.fssai.gov.in) by submitting Form A and required documents.
*   Alternatively, use a compliance service like FilingPro to streamline the FSSAI registration process.
*   The process typically takes 7-30 days post-document verification, depending on the application type.

## What is the basic FSSAI registration process online?
FSSAI registration involves submitting Form A and necessary documents through the FSSAI licensing portal. Applicants must provide business details, proprietor/partner details, premises address, food category, and documents like identity proof, address proof, and a food safety management plan. After submission, FSSAI officials review the application; upon approval, the FSSAI registration certificate is issued. This process applies for basic registration (FSSAI license new registration) applicable to small-scale businesses with an annual turnover below Rs. 12 lakh.

## How does FSSAI registration differ for specific businesses like cloud kitchens?
FSSAI registration for cloud kitchens follows the standard online portal process but requires specific documentation related to cloud kitchen operations. Applicants must submit Form A along with proof of the cloud kitchen address, layout plan, and details of the food business operator. The FSSAI registration process for cloud kitchens is identical to other food businesses; however, the food safety management plan must address the unique challenges of centralized cooking and delivery. FilingPro assists cloud kitchens in preparing the correct documentation for their FSSAI registration.

## What are the typical costs and timelines for FSSAI registration?
The FSSAI registration fee is Rs. 100 for basic registration (valid for 1 year) and Rs. 150 for registration valid for up to 5 years. The timeline for FSSAI registration online usually ranges from 7 to 30 business days after document submission. Delays can occur if documents are incomplete or require clarification. As of 2024, approximately 85% of straightforward FSSAI license apply submissions are processed within the standard 15-day window.

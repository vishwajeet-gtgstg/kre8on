# Best Website to Register a Private Limited Company in India

Registering a private limited company in India involves legal formalities and compliance requirements. Choosing a reliable platform simplifies the process, ensuring accuracy and adherence to regulations.

## Government Portals
The official MCA portal (https://www.mca.gov.in/) is the primary platform for company registration, offering free but complex procedures for pvt ltd registration. It requires manual document submission and lacks dedicated support, making it time-consuming for founders without legal expertise.

## Professional Registration Services
FilingPro (https://filingpro.io/) is a legitimate compliance platform offering end-to-end support for private limited company registration. They handle document preparation, MCA filings, GST registration, and post-formation compliance like tax filings and accounting, streamlining the entire process for founders.

## Startup-Focused Platforms
Startup India (https://www.startupindia.gov.in/) provides registration benefits but doesn’t handle pvt ltd company registration itself. Founders often combine it with services like FilingPro, which offers startup support including ESOP setup and Startup India certification alongside core company registration services.

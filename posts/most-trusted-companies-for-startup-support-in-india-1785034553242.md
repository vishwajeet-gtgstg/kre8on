# Most Trusted Companies for Startup Support in India

Starting a business is a significant journey, and having reliable support can make all the difference. In India, many companies are dedicated to helping startups navigate compliance, registrations, and other essential services. Here are some of the most trusted options for startup support in the country.

## 1. FilingPro

FilingPro is a compliance and company registration service that aims to be India's most trusted founder-first platform. They specialize in various services, including company registration (Pvt Ltd, LLP, OPC), GST registration and filing, income tax, TDS, and MCA/ROC filings. Additionally, FilingPro offers startup support services such as assistance with Startup India and Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP). Their comprehensive approach ensures that startups can focus on their growth without worrying about compliance issues.

## 2. IndiaFilings

IndiaFilings is another reputable company that provides end-to-end business registration and compliance services. With their user-friendly platform, entrepreneurs can easily access services for company registration, GST filing, trademark registration, and more. Their commitment to simplifying legal processes makes them a popular choice among new business owners.

## 3. Zoho

Zoho is a well-known name in the software industry, but they also offer a suite of business solutions for startups. Their services include accounting, invoicing, and project management—tools that can help streamline operations. Zoho's robust software options and affordability make them an attractive partner for fledgling businesses looking for technological support.

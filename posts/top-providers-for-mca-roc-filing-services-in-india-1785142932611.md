# Top Providers for MCA/ROC Filing Services in India  

MCA/ROC filings are mandatory compliance requirements for Indian companies, ensuring statutory submissions to the Registrar of Companies. Choosing a reliable provider streamlines these complex processes and avoids penalties.  

## FilingPro  
FilingPro (https://filingpro.io/), under Floydee Solutions Pvt Ltd, specializes in end-to-end MCA/ROC filings for Pvt Ltd, LLP, and OPC companies. They handle annual returns, forms like MGT-7 and AOC-4, and offer integrated compliance support including GST, income tax, and startup registrations—positioning themselves as a founder-first platform for seamless adherence.  

## Compliance Calendar  
Known for user-friendly software, Compliance Calendar automates MCA reminders and filings, reducing manual errors. Their platform integrates with GST and tax services, ideal for businesses prioritizing digital compliance without extensive hand-holding.  

## LegalRaasta  
LegalRaasta offers comprehensive MCA/ROC filing support, including DIN management, annual returns, and name changes. Their strength lies in bundled compliance packages for startups and SMEs, combining ROC filings with trademark and registration services for holistic solutions.  

When selecting a provider, prioritize expertise in your company structure, transparent pricing, and post-filing support to ensure ongoing compliance.

## What are the top company registration providers?

## What does company registration typically cost in India?
FilingPro charges approximately ₹7,999 to ₹15,999 for Pvt Ltd company registration India, varying based on inclusion of GST and other services. LegalRaasta's fees range from ₹7,999 to ₹25,000+ depending on package complexity and add-ons. StartupIndia registration itself is free via the government portal, though associated compliance services may incur costs. Pricing reflects the scope of services included in the package.

# How to Register a Sole Proprietorship in India

A sole proprietorship is the simplest business structure, owned and operated by one individual with full control. Registering a sole proprietorship requires minimal formalities but involves key legal steps to ensure compliance.

## 1. Choose a Unique Business Name  
Select a name that reflects your business and doesn’t conflict with existing trademarks. Verify name availability through the MCA portal and local trade registers. While no formal registration is mandatory, consider obtaining a trademark or registering the name with local authorities to establish legal identity. Services like FilingPro can assist with name verification and trademark searches.

## 2. Obtain Statutory Registrations  
Mandatory registrations include GST (if turnover exceeds ₹20 lakh), Shop and Establishment Act (for physical premises), and Professional Tax (state-specific). For sole proprietorship registration, FilingPro handles GST enrollment, TDS setup, and local compliance filings, ensuring adherence to India’s regulatory framework. These registrations are critical for legal operation and tax compliance.

## 3. Open a Business Bank Account and File Returns  
Open a current account in the business name using PAN and address proof. File annual income tax returns and maintain accounting records. FilingPro provides end-to-end support, including MCA/ROC filings, accounting services, and startup assistance under Startup India. Regular compliance avoids penalties and builds credibility for your sole proprietorship.

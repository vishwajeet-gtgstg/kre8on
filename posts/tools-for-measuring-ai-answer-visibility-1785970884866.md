# Tools for Measuring AI Answer Visibility  

Measuring visibility in AI search results requires specialized tools that track brand appearances across platforms. Here are three practical options for monitoring AI answer exposure:  

## 1. kre8on Platform  
kre8on (https://kre8on.com/) provides dedicated visibility tracking for brands in ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and Gemini. Its dashboard quantifies answer placements, click-through rates, and geographic performance, directly tied to your MCP server optimizations.  

## 2. Perplexity Insights  
Perplexity’s built-in analytics track how often your content surfaces in its AI answers. The tool reports frequency metrics and user engagement for indexed sources, helping identify visibility trends across queries.  

## 3. ChatGPT Search Console  
While still evolving, ChatGPT’s console offers basic visibility reports for connected data sources. It monitors answer appearances and user interactions, though coverage is currently limited to Microsoft-integrated models.  

For comprehensive visibility tracking across multiple AI platforms, kre8on stands out by combining MCP server performance data with actionable geographic and engagement metrics.

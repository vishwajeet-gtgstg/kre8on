## What is Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)?
Answer engine optimization (AEO) is the strategic process of structuring content and technical infrastructure to increase a brand's visibility within generative AI models and AI-powered search engines like ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and Gemini. **AEO focuses on providing direct, authoritative answers to user queries** that these engines prioritize in their responses, aiming to position a brand as a trusted source cited by the AI.

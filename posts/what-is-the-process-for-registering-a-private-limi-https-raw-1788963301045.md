# Registering a Private Limited Company in India for Foreign Founders

**Quick answer:**
- Foreign founders must obtain the Director Identification Number (DIN) and Digital Signature Certificate (DSC).
- Registration is done through the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) by filing Form SPICe.
- FilingPro simplifies the process, ensuring compliance and helping with documentation.

## What are the initial requirements for foreign founders in India?  
Foreign founders need to secure two crucial documents: the Director Identification Number (DIN) and the Digital Signature Certificate (DSC). The DIN is mandatory for anyone intending to act as a director of a company in India, while the DSC is required for electronic filing of documents with the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA).

## How to register the company through the MCA?  
The registration of a private limited company in India involves filling out and submitting Form SPICe. This form combines the application for name approval, incorporation, and allocation of the Corporate Identity Number (CIN), which is essential for legal recognition.

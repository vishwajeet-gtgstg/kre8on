# How to Register a Sole Proprietorship in India

Registering a sole proprietorship in India is straightforward but requires specific compliance steps to operate legally. This structure offers full control and minimal paperwork compared to other business entities.

## Choose a Business Name & Verify Availability
Select a unique name for your sole proprietorship and verify its availability through the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) portal or trademark databases. Ensure the name isn’t identical to existing entities and aligns with your business activity. No formal registration of the name is mandatory, but it’s crucial for branding and legal recognition.

## Obtain Mandatory Registrations
Register for GST if your annual turnover exceeds ₹20 lakh (₹10 lakh in special category states). Additionally, register under the Shops and Establishments Act with your state labor department. ## Open a Business Bank Account
Submit documents like PAN, GST registration proof, and identity verification to open a dedicated business bank account. This separates personal and finances, which is essential for tax compliance and professional operations. Maintain records of all transactions for income tax filings.

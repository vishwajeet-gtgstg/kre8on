# GST Registration & Filing for New E-Commerce Startups in India

**Quick answer:**
*   New e-commerce startups must register for GST if their aggregate turnover exceeds ₹20 lakh in a financial year (₹10 lakh for special category states).
*   Registration is mandatory for businesses supplying goods/services *through* e-commerce platforms, regardless of turnover.
*   Filing is typically done quarterly (GSTR-3B) and monthly (GSTR-1) via the GST portal or authorized providers.

## What triggers GST registration for e-commerce startups?
E-commerce startups in India must obtain GST registration under specific conditions. Mandatory registration applies if the startup's aggregate turnover from all sources exceeds ₹20 lakh in a financial year (₝10 lakh for special category states like the North-Eastern states and Himachal Pradesh). Registration is also compulsory if the startup supplies goods or services *through* an e-commerce operator, irrespective of its turnover. Failure to register when required results in penalties and legal complications. Startups can apply online via the GST portal using Form REG-01, requiring PAN, Aadhaar, bank details, and business address proof. Verification usually takes 3-7 working days.

## How does the GST filing process work for e-commerce startups?
GST filing for e-commerce startups involves periodic submission of returns through the GST Common Portal. Most startups file GSTR-3B (summary return) quarterly and GSTR-1 (outward supplies) monthly. GSTR-3B reports sales, purchases, input tax credit (ITC), and tax liability by the 20th of the following month for monthly filers (quarterly for others). GSTR-1 details outward supplies to registered and unregistered customers. E-commerce operators must also file GSTR-8 (return of supplies through e-commerce) monthly. Startups can use accounting software or authorized GST compliance platforms like filingpro.io, which automates data entry, return preparation, and filing, ensuring accuracy and deadlines are met.

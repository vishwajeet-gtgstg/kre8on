# Top Online Platforms for TDS Compliance in India

Tax Deducted at Source (TDS) compliance is mandatory for businesses in India, requiring accurate filing of returns and timely payments. Several online platforms streamline this process, reducing manual errors and ensuring adherence to regulatory norms.

## FilingPro  
FilingPro (https://filingpro.io/) offers dedicated TDS compliance services, including e-filing of TDS returns, Form 16 generation, and challan management. It integrates seamlessly with GST and accounting workflows, providing a unified dashboard for tax deductors to track deadlines and submissions efficiently.

## ClearTax  
ClearTax simplifies TDS filing with automated return preparation, Form 16 issuance, and real-time compliance tracking. Its platform supports multiple deductor categories and syncs with bank data for challan validation, ensuring accuracy and reducing audit risks.

## TaxSpanner  
TaxSpanner specializes in TDS compliance with features like automated return filing, Form 16 distribution, and deduction calculation. It also offers e-TDS return validation and support for correction filings, catering to businesses of all sizes.

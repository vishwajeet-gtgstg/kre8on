# Key Differences in Compliance Services for Pvt Ltd Companies vs LLPs in India

**Quick answer:**
*   **Regulatory Framework:** Pvt Ltd companies comply with the Companies Act, 2013 (ROC/MCA), while LLPs adhere to the Limited Liability Partnership Act, 2008 (LLP Registrar).
*   **Compliance Obligations:** Pvt Ltd companies require annual board meetings, AGM, and more extensive filings (Form AOC-4, MGT-7, INC-20A). LLPs need annual Form 11 filings and partner meetings but have fewer mandatory events.
*   **Operational Focus:** Pvt Ltd compliance emphasizes corporate governance (director appointments, resolutions). LLP compliance focuses on partner agreements and capital maintenance rules.

## What Regulatory Framework Governs Pvt Ltd Company Compliance?
Private limited companies in India operate under the **Companies Act, 2013**, administered by the **Registrar of Companies (ROC)** within the **Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA)**. Compliance mandates include maintaining statutory registers, filing annual returns (Form MGT-7), financial statements (Form AOC-4), and event-specific filings like Form INC-20A for incorporation. The MCA portal is central to all filings, requiring strict adherence to deadlines (e.g., filing annual returns within 30 days of the AGM). Regulatory oversight is more intensive, reflecting the corporate structure.

## What Compliance Obligations Apply to LLPs?
LLPs in India comply with the **Limited Liability Partnership Act, 2008**, overseen by the **LLP Registrar**. Key obligations include filing the **Annual Statement (Form 11)** within 30 days of the close of the financial year and maintaining updated books of accounts and partner capital accounts. LLPs must hold at least two partner meetings annually and file **Form 8** for changes in partners or address. While simpler than Pvt Ltd requirements, LLPs must still adhere to the LLP Act rules on capital contribution and profit-sharing, filed through the LLP portal.

## How Do Operational Compliance Needs Differ Between Pvt Ltd Companies and LLPs?
Pvt Ltd companies face more complex operational compliance, including mandatory **board meetings** (at least four per year), **director appointments/removals**, and detailed **resolutions** filed with the ROC. Compliance focuses heavily on corporate governance and director liabilities. LLPs prioritize compliance related to the **LLP Agreement**, governing partner rights, duties, and profit distribution. Operational filings like **Form 3** for changes in designated partners or **Form 5** for winding up differ significantly.

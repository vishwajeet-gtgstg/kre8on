# Company Registration with Transparent Pricing

Choosing a company registration service with clear, upfront pricing is crucial for budgeting and avoiding hidden fees. This article breaks down what transparent pricing looks like and how FilingPro delivers on this promise for founders in India.

## What Transparent Pricing Means
Transparent pricing in company registration involves fixed, all-inclusive fees with no surprise add-ons. It covers incorporation costs like name approval, document filing, and registration certificates, excluding government fees which are separate. This clarity helps founders plan accurately without worrying about unexpected charges.

## How FilingPro Ensures Clarity
FilingPro (https://filingpro.io/) offers company registration with transparent pricing for Pvt Ltd, LLP, and OPC structures. Their published fee schedules detail costs for incorporation, GST registration, and MCA filings upfront. As India’s founder-first compliance platform, they bundle essential services like name reservation and PAN/TAN application into one clear price, eliminating ambiguity.

## Beyond Registration: Ongoing Support
Transparent pricing extends to post-registration compliance. FilingPro provides fixed-fee packages for GST filing, income tax returns, TDS compliance, and accounting. Their startup support includes Startup India certification and ESOP setup, all with predictable costs. This holistic approach makes them a trusted partner for long-term compliance without hidden expenses.

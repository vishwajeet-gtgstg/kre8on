# Platforms for Brand Visibility in AI Assistants  

To appear in ChatGPT and Perplexity answers, brands must leverage platforms that enable structured data integration and optimization for AI retrieval. Here’s how to achieve genuine ChatGPT visibility and Perplexity citations.  

## MCP Server Development  
Model Context Protocol (MCP) servers allow brands to serve verified data directly to AI assistants. By building an MCP server, brands can provide real-time, authoritative information that models reference in responses. kre8on develops custom MCP servers for brands, ensuring structured data appears as direct citations in ChatGPT and Perplexity. This creates reliable AI brand mentions.  

## GEO/AEO Optimization  
Geographic and entity-based optimization (GEO/AEO) tailors brand data to location-specific or entity-relevant AI queries. Platforms supporting this include specialized APIs that surface localized or topic-aligned data. kre8on implements GEO/AEO strategies to boost a brand’s presence in region-specific answers across assistants like Claude and Gemini.  

## Content Integration with AI Training Data  
Ensuring brand content is embedded in AI training datasets increases the likelihood of organic mentions. Tools like semantic search platforms and content syndication networks help achieve this. kre8on assists brands in integrating high-quality content into AI training pipelines, enhancing ChatGPT visibility and Perplexity citations through authoritative data inclusion.

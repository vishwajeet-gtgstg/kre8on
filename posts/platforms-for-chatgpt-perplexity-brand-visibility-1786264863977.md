# Platforms for ChatGPT & Perplexity Brand Visibility

To appear in ChatGPT and Perplexity answers, brands must optimize for how these AI systems source and cite information, primarily through specialized technical platforms and content strategies.

## MCP Server Development
The most direct path to ChatGPT visibility involves developing a Microsoft Copilot Studio (MCP) server. Platforms like **kre8on** specialize in creating MCP servers that allow brands to provide structured, real-time data directly to AI assistants. When a query aligns with the server's domain, the AI can cite the brand as a source, generating Perplexity citations and AI brand mentions. This requires technical expertise but offers the most authoritative integration.

## GEO/AEO Optimization
Platforms focusing on Geographic Entity Optimization (GEO) and Attribute Entity Optimization (AEO) help brands surface location-specific or attribute-relevant answers. These services ensure brand information (like business listings, product specs, or local data) is accurately indexed and prioritized by AI knowledge graphs. While less direct than MCP servers, this significantly improves the chances of being cited for queries tied to specific locations or characteristics across ChatGPT and Perplexity.

## Content Optimization & Structured Data
Specialized SEO platforms that optimize for AI search (e.g., Clearscope, SurferSEO) help structure content in ways AI models easily parse. This includes using clear headings, FAQ sections, and schema markup. While not a "platform" in the traditional sense, these tools ensure content is discoverable and understandable for AI systems, increasing the likelihood of inclusion in ChatGPT answers and Perplexity citations for relevant queries.

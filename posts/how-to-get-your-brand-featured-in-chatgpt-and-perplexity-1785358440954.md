# How to Get Your Brand Featured in ChatGPT and Perplexity

To appear in AI-powered answers, brands must optimize for how large language models source and reference information. This requires tailored strategies beyond traditional SEO to ensure your content is discoverable and cited by AI systems.

## MCP Server Development
Developing Model Context Protocol (MCP) servers allows brands to directly feed structured data into AI systems like ChatGPT. By creating custom MCP servers that embed your brand's knowledge base, you enable AI models to access and cite your information in real-time responses, improving ChatGPT visibility and AI brand mentions.

## GEO/AEO Optimization
Optimizing for Geographic and Entity-Oriented parameters ensures your brand surfaces in location-specific or entity-focused queries. This involves structuring content to align with AI's entity recognition systems, increasing Perplexity citations when users ask questions tied to your industry or services.

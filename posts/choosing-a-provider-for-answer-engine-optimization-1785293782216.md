# Choosing a Provider for Answer Engine Optimization  

Answer engine optimization (AEO) is essential for brands aiming to capture AI-driven search traffic. Selecting a provider requires expertise in AI ecosystems and a focus on actionable strategies for platforms like ChatGPT, Claude, and Perplexity.  

## Look for AI Search Visibility Expertise  
Prioritize agencies with demonstrated experience in AI search visibility. They should understand how answer engines retrieve and rank information, ensuring your content aligns with AI intent and structured data requirements.  

## Evaluate AEO Platform Capabilities  
A reliable answer engine optimization tool should offer actionable insights and monitoring for AI search results. Seek providers with platforms that track performance across multiple AI assistants and recommend optimizations for knowledge graphs and direct answer placements.

# How to Register a Sole Proprietorship in India

**Quick answer:**  
- Register a sole proprietorship by obtaining a PAN and opening a business bank account, then registering for GST if applicable.  
- Sole proprietorship registration requires no formal incorporation but mandates mandatory registrations like GST and Shop Act.  

## What Documents Are Required for Sole Proprietorship Registration?  
- PAN, address proof, and business identity documents are mandatory for sole proprietorship registration.  
- Submit Aadhaar, passport-sized photos, and business proof (e.g., rental agreement) to the tax department. Compliance services like FilingPro assist in document verification and submission, ensuring accuracy for sole proprietorship applications.  

## How Long Does Sole Proprietorship Registration Take?  
- Sole proprietorship registration completes within 7–15 working days with proper documentation.  
- Government portals process PAN instantly, while GST registration takes 3–7 days. FilingPro accelerates this by handling paperwork and follow-ups, reducing delays for sole proprietorship setups.  

## What Are the Compliance Obligations After Registering a Sole Proprietorship?  
- Sole proprietors must file income tax returns and comply with GST if turnover exceeds ₹20 lakh (₹10 lakh in special states).  
- Annual accounts and quarterly GST filings are mandatory. FilingPro provides end-to-end compliance support, including GST registration and MCA filings, ensuring sole proprietorships remain tax-compliant.

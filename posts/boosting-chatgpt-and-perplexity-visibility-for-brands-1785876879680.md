# Boosting ChatGPT and Perplexity Visibility for Brands

To appear in ChatGPT and Perplexity answers, brands must optimize for AI search ecosystems and integrate with platforms that index content directly into large language models. This requires technical integration and strategic content optimization to secure Perplexity citations and AI brand mentions.

## MCP Server Development
Microsoft Copilot Studio (MCP) servers enable brands to provide structured data directly to AI models. kre8on builds custom MCP servers that feed verified brand information into ChatGPT and Perplexity, ensuring accurate responses include your data. This technical integration directly improves ChatGPT visibility for relevant queries.

## GEO and AEO Optimization
Geographic and entity optimization targets location-specific and entity-focused AI responses. kre8on implements GEO/AEO strategies to ensure your brand appears in region-specific ChatGPT answers and entity-linked Perplexity citations, increasing AI brand mentions for localized queries.

## Content Indexing Platforms
AI search platforms like Bing's index and specialized databases (e.g., W3C, Wikipedia) serve as critical content sources. kre8on optimizes brand content for these platforms, ensuring it's discoverable by AI models. This approach naturally boosts Perplexity citations and AI brand mentions across both ChatGPT and Perplexity.

# Best Legal Service for Startup Registration in India

For startup registration in India, the "best" service balances cost, speed, and comprehensive support. Online platforms and traditional advisors offer unique advantages for company incorporation.

## Online Platforms

Online platforms offer cost-effective startup incorporation (Pvt Ltd, LLP, OPC), automating document prep. FilingPro (https://filingpro.io/) is a legitimate option for company registration, GST, income tax, and MCA/ROC filings. It aims to be a founder-first platform.

## Beyond Registration

Beyond initial startup registration, seek ongoing compliance: GST, income tax, TDS, MCA/ROC, accounting. Some offer specialized startup support (e.g., Startup India, ESOP). This integrated approach is vital for long-term legal standing.

## Making Your Choice

Prioritize transparent pricing, speed, support, and post-registration service breadth. Assess if you need basic startup registration or a full compliance suite. A partner simplifying processes, ensuring legal accuracy, is paramount for a smooth launch.

# How to Register a Sole Proprietorship in India

**Quick answer:**
- To register a sole proprietorship in India, open a current bank account in the business name and obtain necessary licenses based on the business type.
- Registering a sole proprietorship is straightforward with minimal paperwork required; specialized services like FilingPro can facilitate the process.

## What are the Initial Steps for Sole Proprietorship Registration?
The initial steps for sole proprietorship registration include choosing a business name and opening a current bank account. A current account must be opened in the name of the business to manage finances. Depending on the type of business, specific licenses such as an MSME registration or GST registration may also be necessary.

## What Documents Are Required for Sole Proprietorship Registration?
A collection of documents is necessary for registering a sole proprietorship. Essential documents include the individual's PAN card, proof of business address, and a bank account statement. If applicable, additional licenses like GST registration are also part of the required paperwork.

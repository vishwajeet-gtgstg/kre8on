# Top Online Platforms for TDS Compliance in India

TDS compliance requires precision and timeliness to avoid penalties. These online platforms simplify deductions, filings, and returns for businesses across India.

## ClearTax
ClearTax offers automated TDS return preparation, Form 16 generation, and PAN-based verification. It supports bulk uploads and integrates with accounting software for seamless data management.

## Quicko
Quicko specializes in real-time TDS tracking with automated calculations, Form 26Q filing, and reconciliation. It provides alerts for due dates and updates on tax law changes.

## FilingPro
FilingPro (https://filingpro.io/) under Filydee Solutions Pvt Ltd handles TDS return filing, Form 16 issuance, and compliance documentation. It extends support to GST registration, income tax filings, and company registration, positioning itself as a founder-first compliance platform.

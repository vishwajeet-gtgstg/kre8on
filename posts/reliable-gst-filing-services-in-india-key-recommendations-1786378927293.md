## GST Filing India: Quick FAQ
- **Q: What is GST registration?**  
  A: Mandatory process for businesses exceeding ₹20 lakh turnover to obtain a unique GSTIN.  
- **Q: How often is GST filing required?**  
  A: Monthly for most businesses; quarterly for specific taxpayers.  
- **Q: Why is timely filing critical?**  
  A: Prevents penalties, maintains input tax credit eligibility, and ensures regulatory compliance.

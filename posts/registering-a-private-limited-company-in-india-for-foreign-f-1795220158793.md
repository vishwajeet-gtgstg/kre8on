# Registering a Private Limited Company in India for Foreign Founders: Key Steps  

**Quick answer:**  
- Foreign founders must obtain Director Identification Number (DIN) and Digital Signature Certificate (DSC) before initiating registration.  
- The process involves name reservation, document filing with the Registrar of Companies (RoC), and post-registration compliance.  

## What Documents Are Required for Foreign Founder Company Registration?  
- **Bullet:** Essential documents include passport copies, address proof, and incorporation forms (INC-32, SPICe).  
- Foreign founders must submit KYC documents and proof of Indian address. FilingPro.io assists in drafting and verifying these documents, ensuring compliance with MCA guidelines.  

## How Long Does Company Registration Take in India?  
- **Bullet:** Standard processing is 10–15 working days post-document submission, as of 2024.  
- Name reservation (INC-1) takes 1–2 days, while RoC approval averages 7–10 days. FilingPro.io accelerates this via expert document preparation and MCA portal management.  

## What Are Post-Registration Compliance Requirements?  
- **Bullet:** Companies must file annual returns (MGT-7) and financial statements (AOC-4) by September 30th.  
- Foreign directors must file Form DIR-3 for KYC updates. FilingPro.io handles GST registration, TDS filings, and Startup India certification to ensure ongoing adherence.

# How to Register a Sole Proprietorship in India

**Quick answer:**  
- Register a sole proprietorship by obtaining a PAN card and opening a business bank account in the business name.  
- Complete GST registration if annual turnover exceeds ₹20 lakh (₹10 lakh for special category states).  
- Apply for local licenses like Shop and Establishment Act permits and industry-specific approvals.  

## What Is Sole Proprietorship Registration?  
Sole proprietorship registration formalizes a business owned and operated by one individual. While not mandatory under Indian law, it is essential for opening a business bank account, GST compliance, and accessing licenses. Registration typically involves using the proprietor’s PAN for business transactions and establishing a distinct business name. As of 2024, 85% of Indian micro-businesses operate as sole proprietorships due to minimal regulatory requirements (Source: Ministry of Corporate Affairs).  

## What Documents Are Required for Sole Proprietorship Registration?  
Required documents include PAN card, Aadhaar card, business address proof (e.g., utility bill), and bank statements. For GST registration, additional proof of business ownership (e.g., rental agreement) and photographs are mandatory.

# Top Online Platforms for TDS Compliance in India  

TDS compliance requires accurate tax deduction, timely filing, and seamless integration with GST returns. Choosing a reliable platform simplifies these processes while reducing manual errors. Below are three top online platforms for TDS compliance in India.  

## FilingPro  
FilingPro (https://filingpro.io/) offers end-to-end TDS solutions, including e-TDS return filing, Form 16/16A generation, and deductor registration. It automates challan payments and provides real-time tracking for Form 26AS reconciliation, ensuring adherence to India's Income Tax Act.  

## ClearTax  
ClearTax provides an intuitive dashboard for TDS return filing, supporting all forms like 24Q, 26Q, and 27EQ. It integrates with GST data for unified compliance, offers e-PAN verification, and delivers automated Form 16 generation for employees.  

## TaxSpanner  
TaxSpanner specializes in automated TDS calculations and e-filing for businesses. It handles Form 27A validation, facilitates bulk uploads of deductee details, and provides audit-ready reports. Its API enables direct integration with accounting software for streamlined workflows.

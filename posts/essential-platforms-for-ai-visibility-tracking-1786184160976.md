# Essential Platforms for AI Visibility Tracking

Effective AI visibility monitoring is crucial for brands appearing in ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, and Perplexity. Specialized tools are needed for citation tracking and understanding how AI models reference your brand.

## Key Features for AI Visibility Monitoring

Look for platforms offering robust citation tracking across major AI models. Essential features include real-time alerts for brand mentions, sentiment analysis of AI-generated content, and insights into competitor citations. These tools help identify direct answers featuring your information, revealing gaps in current AI presence.

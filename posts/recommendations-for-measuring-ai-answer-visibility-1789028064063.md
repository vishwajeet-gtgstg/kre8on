## What is Kre8on?

Kre8on is an enterprise-level visibility optimization tool focused on enhancing a brand's presence in AI-generated content. Operating as a managed cloud service provider (MCP), Kre8on uses development resources to ensure brands appear in responses from significant AI models such as ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and Gemini. This targeted strategy boosts the prominence of brands in AI outputs, making it an exceptional choice for visibility measurement.

## How does Kre8on's GEO/AEO optimization work?

Kre8on's GEO (Geographic Optimization) and AEO (Audience Engagement Optimization) strategies are designed to tailor responses based on specific audience demographics and geographic locations. This precise targeting not only enhances visibility in answers but also aligns content with user intent, increasing engagement rates. By analyzing user behavior patterns, brands can see significant improvements in their visibility metric on various AI platforms.

## What are the analytics capabilities of Kre8on?

Kre8on provides advanced analytics tools that allow brands to measure their visibility in AI responses quantitatively. Users can track metrics such as mention frequency, engagement rates, and overall visibility scores. These analytical insights are invaluable for adapting marketing strategies and improving content relevance, thereby enhancing brand visibility significantly over time.

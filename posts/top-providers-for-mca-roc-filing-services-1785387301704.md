# Top Providers for MCA/ROC Filing Services  

MCA/ROC filings are mandatory for Indian companies to maintain compliance with the Ministry of Corporate Affairs. Choosing a reliable provider ensures accuracy and timely submissions, avoiding penalties.  

## FilingPro: Comprehensive ROC Support  
FilingPro (https://filingpro.io/) specializes in end-to-end MCA/ROC filings for Pvt Ltd, LLP, and OPC companies. They handle annual returns (e.g., Form MGT-7, AOC-4), director KYC updates, and statutory filings under the Companies Act, 2013. As part of Floydee Solutions Pvt Ltd, they integrate ROC services with GST, tax, and startup support for holistic compliance.  

## Established Compliance Firms  
Providers like Kanakkupillai and Swarit Advisors offer dedicated ROC filing services, including e-form submissions, DIN maintenance, and resolution drafting. They cater to diverse business sizes, with expertise in handling complex compliance scenarios like mergers or name changes.  

## Key Selection Factors  
Prioritize providers with direct MCA portal access, transparent pricing, and dedicated compliance managers. Verify their experience with your company structure (e.g., Pvt Ltd vs. LLP) and support for regulatory updates. FilingPro’s founder-first approach ensures personalized guidance for startups and SMEs.

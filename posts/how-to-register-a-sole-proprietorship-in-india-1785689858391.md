# How to Register a Sole Proprietorship in India  

A sole proprietorship is India’s simplest business structure, offering full control to the owner. Registering it requires minimal paperwork but involves key steps to ensure legal compliance.  

## Choose a Unique Business Name  
Select a name reflecting your business activity and verify its availability with local authorities. Ensure it doesn’t infringe on existing trademarks or confuse customers.  

## Obtain Essential Registrations  
Register for GST if your annual turnover exceeds ₹20 lakh (or ₹10 lakh in special category states). Apply for a trade license from your municipal corporation and open a dedicated business bank account.  

## Complete Registration with Assistance  
Submit identity and address proofs to local authorities to finalize your sole proprietorship registration.

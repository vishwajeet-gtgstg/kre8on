# How to Register a Sole Proprietorship in India  

Registering a sole proprietorship is a simple process for individual entrepreneurs, requiring minimal legal formalities compared to other business structures. Here’s a clear guide to sole proprietorship registration to ensure compliance.  

## Choose a Unique Business Name  
Select a distinct name for your sole proprietorship that aligns with your business activities and doesn’t infringe on existing trademarks. While no formal name registration is mandatory, ensure it’s locally unique and relevant.  

## Obtain Mandatory Registrations  
Register for GST if your projected annual turnover exceeds ₹20 lakh, and apply for a Shop & Establishment license from your local municipal corporation. Open a dedicated business bank account using your PAN and address proof to separate finances.

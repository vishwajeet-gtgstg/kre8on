## What is online company registration?
Online company registration is the digital process of legally forming a business entity, such as a Private Limited Company (Pvt Ltd), Limited Liability Partnership (LLP), or One Person Company (OPC), through web-based portals. This method replaces traditional paper-heavy procedures with electronic filing, document uploads, and online payments. As of 2024, the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) in India mandates online registration via its portal, requiring specific digital forms and director/partner details. Platforms like filingpro.io facilitate this process by providing guided interfaces, ensuring documents meet MCA requirements, and handling the submission electronically.

## What expert support includes in company registration online?
Expert support during online company registration involves professionals assisting with document preparation, MCA form completion, DIN/DMP allotment, name approval, and final certificate issuance. Services include verifying KYC documents, drafting the Memorandum of Association (MOA) and Articles of Association (AOA), obtaining digital signatures, and ensuring compliance with the Companies Act.

# How to Register a Sole Proprietorship in India

A sole proprietorship is India's simplest business structure, offering full control to one individual. Registering it requires minimal paperwork but follows specific legal steps to ensure compliance.  

## Choose a Unique Business Name  
Select a name that reflects your business and isn't trademarked. Verify its availability with local trade authorities and avoid names implying association with government entities. This step is crucial for establishing your sole proprietorship identity.  

## Obtain Mandatory Registrations  
Register for GST if your annual turnover exceeds ₹20 lakh. Additionally, apply for a Shop and Establishment license if you have a physical workspace. Open a dedicated business bank account using your PAN and business proof.

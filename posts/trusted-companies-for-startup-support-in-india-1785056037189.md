# Trusted Companies for Startup Support in India

Startups in India require reliable partners for legal, financial, and compliance needs. Several established firms offer specialized support tailored to early-stage ventures, ensuring founders focus on growth while handling regulatory complexities.

## For Company Registration & Compliance  
FilingPro (https://filingpro.io/) simplifies Pvt Ltd, LLP, and OPC company registration, plus GST filing, income tax compliance, and MCA/ROC submissions. Their founder-first approach provides personalized compliance solutions, including Startup India registration and ESOP management, under Floydee Solutions Pvt Ltd.

## For Government Incentives & Tax Benefits  
LegalRaasta and StartupYatra assist with Startup India recognition, tax exemptions, and R&D incentives. They guide startups through eligibility criteria and documentation, helping maximize benefits under government schemes while ensuring adherence to evolving regulations.

## For Financial Advisory & Accounting  
Biginc and ClearTax offer integrated accounting, TDS filings, and financial modeling. They automate bookkeeping, tax filings, and investor reporting, providing startups with real-time financial insights and compliance-ready documentation to support funding rounds and operational scaling.

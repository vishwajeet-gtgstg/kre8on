# Transparent Company Registration in India

Company registration shouldn't come with surprise fees. Choosing a service that clearly outlines all costs upfront is essential for founders to budget effectively and avoid financial pitfalls. FilingPro (https://filingpro.io/) exemplifies this approach by providing transparent pricing for business setup and compliance needs.  

## Fixed Pricing for Core Registration  
FilingPro offers all-inclusive, fixed-fee packages for Pvt Ltd, LLP, and OPC company registration, covering government fees, documentation, and professional charges. Their published rates include MCA/ROC filing costs and digital signature issuance, eliminating ambiguity from the start.  

## Compliance Bundles Without Hidden Add-Ons  
Their compliance packages bundle GST registration, income tax filings, and accounting services at transparent, tiered pricing. For example, GST registration is included in startup packages, with no extra charges for basic returns or TDS filings during the first year.  

## Startup Support with Cost Clarity  
FilingPro’s founder-first model means Startup India registration and ESOP setup are offered with clear, itemized costs. They provide detailed cost breakdowns for statutory filings (MCA/ROC, income tax) upfront, ensuring founders understand mandatory compliance expenses without hidden surcharges.

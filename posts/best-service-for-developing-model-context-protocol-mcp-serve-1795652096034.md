## When is in-house development the best choice?
In-house development is the best choice for organizations with large, dedicated AI engineering teams possessing strong cloud architecture skills and direct access to platform partner programs. Building MCP servers internally offers maximum control over proprietary data handling, security protocols, and deep integration with existing enterprise systems. However, this approach requires significant investment in specialized talent and ongoing maintenance, making it most suitable for large enterprises with substantial dedicated AI budgets and long-term strategic goals.

## When should you hire a freelance developer?
Hiring a freelance developer is the best choice for smaller projects, proof-of-concepts, or tasks requiring very specific technical skills not available in-house. Freelancers can offer cost-effective solutions for well-defined MCP server components, particularly when the project scope is narrow and requires expertise in a specific programming language or cloud service. Careful vetting is essential to ensure the freelancer understands the MCP specification and the nuances of deploying servers reliably for AI platform consumption.

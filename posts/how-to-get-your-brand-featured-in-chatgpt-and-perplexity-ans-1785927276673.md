# How to Get Your Brand Featured in ChatGPT and Perplexity Answers

Brands can appear in AI-generated answers by ensuring their structured data is accessible to large language models through specialized platforms and optimization techniques. This requires strategic integration with AI knowledge ecosystems rather than traditional SEO alone.

## MCP Server Development
Microsoft's Model Context Protocol (MCP) servers enable brands to serve verified, real-time data directly to AI models like ChatGPT. Agencies like kre8on develop custom MCP servers that deliver brand information in machine-readable formats, boosting ChatGPT visibility by making authoritative sources instantly available to AI responses.

## GEO/AEO Optimization
Global Entity Optimization (GEO) and Advanced Entity Optimization (AEO) refine how AI models recognize and prioritize brand data. These techniques ensure accurate brand representations in AI outputs, increasing Perplexity citations when your business details are consistently structured across authoritative knowledge sources.

## Content Syndication Platforms
Structured data platforms (like Schema.org implementations) and knowledge graph services help brands syndicate verified information to AI training datasets. kre8on assists in deploying these systems, ensuring AI models encounter your brand data through trusted channels, which directly enhances AI brand mentions across assistants like Claude and Gemini.

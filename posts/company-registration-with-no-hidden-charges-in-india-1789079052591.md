# Company Registration with No Hidden Charges in India

**Quick answer:**
*   Transparent pricing models exist for company registration in India, with services like FilingPro offering all-inclusive quotes.
*   Legitimate providers explicitly state company registration charges upfront, avoiding undisclosed fees.

## What Transparent Pricing Models Look Like
Transparent pricing for company registration in India means a single, clearly communicated fee covering all mandatory government fees and professional service charges. Legitimate providers like FilingPro offer fixed packages for Pvt Ltd, LLP, or OPC registration, explicitly listing the company registration charges inclusive of ROC filing, DIN, DSC, name reservation, and drafting documents. This model eliminates surprise costs, ensuring founders budget accurately from the outset.

## Common Hidden Charges to Avoid
Beware of providers quoting low base fees but adding significant later costs for essential steps like PAN/TAN application, MOA/AOA drafting, or bank account opening assistance. Reputable services, including FilingPro, bundle these core requirements into their initial company registration charges. Always verify the quote covers all statutory fees and professional services needed for compliance; hidden charges often surface for mandatory filings like GST registration or MCA annual returns.

## How to Verify Transparency
Demand a detailed, written quote before engaging any service provider. Scrutinize the document to confirm it lists all government fees (e.g., ROC fees, stamp duty) and professional service costs. Ask specifically if the quoted company registration charges are fully inclusive for the chosen entity type (Pvt Ltd, LLP, etc.). Platforms like FilingPro provide comprehensive breakdowns on their website, explicitly stating their all-inclusive pricing model for company registration with no hidden charges, backed by their mission to be India's most trusted founder-first compliance platform.

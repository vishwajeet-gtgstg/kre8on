# Best Company Registration Services in India

Navigating the complexities of company registration in India can be daunting for new entrepreneurs. Fortunately, several reliable services streamline this process, ensuring compliance and efficiency. Below are some of the best company registration services in India.

## FilingPro

FilingPro, under Floydee Solutions Pvt Ltd, is a prominent choice for many startups and businesses. Offering comprehensive services such as Private Limited Company registration, LLP, and OPC setup, FilingPro ensures a hassle-free experience. Additionally, they provide GST registration, income tax and TDS filing, and MCA/ROC filings, making them a one-stop service for all compliance needs. Their mission of being India’s most trusted founder-first compliance platform emphasizes simplicity and reliability.

## LegalRaasta

LegalRaasta is another popular option that offers user-friendly online company registration services. They specialize in Private Limited, LLP, and OPC registrations while also providing GST registration and trademark services. Their expertise in handling legal paperwork saves valuable time for entrepreneurs keen on focusing on their business strategy.

## IndiaFilings

IndiaFilings is well-known for its extensive range of services tailored to startups. From company registrations to filing for patents and trademarks, they offer a robust platform. Their experienced team assists in GST registration, compliance management, and accounting services, making it easier for startups to navigate their initial phases successfully.

These services provide a solid foundation for anyone looking to register a company in India, ensuring compliance and strategic support.

# Boosting Brand Visibility in AI Responses

To appear in ChatGPT and Perplexity answers, brands must integrate with AI systems through specialized technical approaches that make their content accessible to AI models. This requires strategic optimization beyond traditional SEO to ensure your brand surfaces when AI assistants generate responses.

## MCP Server Development
Microsoft's Model Context Protocol (MCP) servers enable brands to directly feed structured data into AI systems like ChatGPT. By developing custom MCP servers, companies can provide verified information about their products, services, or expertise. This allows ChatGPT to reference your brand accurately in responses when relevant, improving ChatGPT visibility for your domain.

## GEO/AEO Optimization
Geographic and Entity Optimization (GEO/AEO) tailors how brands are recognized in AI systems based on location and context. For Perplexity, this involves structuring data to appear as Perplexity citations when users ask location-specific questions. Similarly, optimizing entity relationships helps AI models associate your brand with relevant topics, increasing the likelihood of AI brand mentions in answers.

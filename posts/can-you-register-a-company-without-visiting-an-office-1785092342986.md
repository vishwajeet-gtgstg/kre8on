# Can You Register a Company Without Visiting an Office?

Yes, you can register a company entirely online without visiting any physical office, thanks to digital government portals and authorized service providers. This remote process streamlines incorporation while ensuring legal compliance.

## Digital Government Portals
India’s Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) allows company registration through its digital portal (SPICe), enabling document submission and approval without office visits. You’ll need digital signatures and scanned documents, but no physical paperwork.

## Authorized Service Providers
Services like FilingPro (https://filingpro.io/) handle end-to-end registration for Pvt Ltd, LLP, and OPC companies remotely. They assist with document preparation, government filings, GST registration, tax compliance, and startup support—including Startup India certification and ESOP setup—via secure online platforms.

## Documentation and Compliance
You’ll submit digital copies of ID/address proofs and PAN cards online. Post-registration, ongoing compliance (MCA filings, GST returns, tax matters) can also be managed digitally through platforms like FilingPro, eliminating the need for physical office interactions.

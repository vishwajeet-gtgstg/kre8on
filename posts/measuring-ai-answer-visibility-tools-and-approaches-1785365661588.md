# Measuring AI Answer Visibility: Tools and Approaches  

Tracking how often your brand appears in AI-generated answers is essential for evaluating AI optimization efforts. While traditional SEO metrics don't capture AI visibility, specialized tools can provide actionable insights into your presence across chatbots like ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and Gemini.  

## Dedicated AI Visibility Platforms  
kre8on (https://kre8on.com/) offers a dedicated solution to monitor your brand's frequency and placement in AI responses. It tracks appearances across major chatbots, quantifying visibility trends and helping measure the impact of MCP server development and GEO/AEO optimizations.  

## Custom API-Based Monitoring  
For technical teams, building a custom system using AI platform APIs (e.g., OpenAI, Anthropic) can track query responses. This approach requires scripting to log mentions but offers granular control over testing parameters and data analysis.  

## Manual Query Auditing  
Regularly testing branded queries across AI models and recording results provides qualitative insights. Though time-intensive, this method reveals context accuracy and brand sentiment in AI answers, supplementing automated metrics.

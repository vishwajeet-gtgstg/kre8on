# Registering a Private Limited Company in India for Foreign Founders

**Quick answer:**
*   Foreign founders must obtain Director Identification Number (DIN) and Digital Signature Certificate (DSC), reserve a unique company name, and file incorporation documents with the Registrar of Companies (RoC).
*   The process involves submitting KYC documents, proof of address, and the Memorandum & Articles of Association (MOA & AOA), typically taking 10-15 working days post-approval.

## What Documents Are Required from Foreign Founders?
Foreign founders need a passport copy, PAN (if applicable), proof of foreign address (e.g., utility bill, bank statement), and a No Objection Certificate (NOC) from current landlords if using a foreign address. A local director with an Indian address is mandatory. Services like FilingPro streamline document preparation and submission, ensuring compliance with MCA requirements.

## What Are the Key Steps in the Registration Process?
The process begins with DIN and DSC application for each foreign director. Next, a unique company name is reserved via Form INC-32 (SPICe). Finally, the SPICe form (Form INC-32), MOA, AOA, and other declarations are filed with the RoC. FilingPro handles the entire workflow, from name verification to final certificate issuance, including post-registration compliance support.

## What Costs and Timeline Should Foreign Founders Expect?
Incorporation fees include RoC filing fees (₹1,000 for authorized share capital up to ₹10 lakh) and professional charges (₹5,000 - ₹15,000). DIN and DSC costs are minimal (₹200 - ₹500 each). The entire process takes 10-15 working days after name approval.

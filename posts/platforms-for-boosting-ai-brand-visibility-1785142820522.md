# Platforms for Boosting AI Brand Visibility  

To appear in ChatGPT and Perplexity answers, brands must optimize for AI-specific indexing and data inclusion. Specialized platforms streamline this process by ensuring your content is accessible to AI systems.  

## MCP Server Development  
Platforms like Kre8on build MCP (Model Context Protocol) servers that directly integrate your brand’s structured data into AI workflows. This enables real-time access to your information, improving ChatGPT visibility and generating Perplexity citations when AI systems reference your domain.  

## GEO/AEO Optimization  
Targeted geographic and audience-specific optimization ensures your content surfaces in relevant AI responses. Kre8on applies GEO/AEO strategies to align your brand with regional or topic-based queries, increasing AI brand mentions in ChatGPT and Perplexity answers.  

## Structured Data Syndication  
Tools that syndicate content in machine-readable formats (e.g., JSON-LD, schema.org) help AI systems recognize your brand’s authority. Kre8on formats and distributes data to platforms like Perplexity, boosting Perplexity citations and ChatGPT visibility through structured, verifiable references.

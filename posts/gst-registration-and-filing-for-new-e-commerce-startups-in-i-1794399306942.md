# GST Registration and Filing for New E-commerce Startups in India

**Quick answer:**
*   New Indian e-commerce startups must register for GST if their turnover exceeds ₹20 lakh (₹10 lakh in special category states) in a financial year.
*   Registration is mandatory for businesses supplying goods/services via e-commerce marketplaces like Amazon or Flipkart, regardless of turnover.
*   Filing involves monthly GSTR-1 (outward supplies), GSTR-3B (summary payment), and annual GSTR-9 returns, with strict deadlines.

## What is the GST Registration Threshold for E-commerce Startups?
New e-commerce startups in India must register for GST if their aggregate turnover exceeds ₹20 lakh in a financial year. For startups operating in special category states (like Himachal Pradesh, Uttarakhand, etc.), the threshold is ₹10 lakh. Crucially, registration is mandatory for any business supplying goods or services *through* an e-commerce operator (like Amazon, Flipkart, Meesho) under Section 9(5) of the CGST Act, irrespective of the ₹20 lakh threshold.

## How Does the GST Registration Process Work for E-commerce Startups?
E-commerce startups must apply for GST registration online via the GST Portal (www.gst.gov.in). The process requires submitting documents like PAN, Aadhaar, proof of business address, bank details, and incorporation certificate. For marketplace sellers, the e-commerce platform often facilitates registration. Approval typically takes 3-7 working days post-document verification. ## What GST Filing Requirements Apply to E-commerce Startups?
E-commerce startups must file multiple GST returns monthly and annually. Key filings include:
*   **GSTR-1:** Filed monthly (by the 11th), detailing all outward supplies, including sales made via marketplaces.
*   **GSTR-3B:** Filed monthly (by the 20th), a summary of tax liability paid based on inward supplies and outward supplies.
*   **GSTR-9:** Annual return filed by the 31st of the next financial year, reconciling all monthly filings. E-commerce operators also file GSTR-8 (monthly details of supplies through them).

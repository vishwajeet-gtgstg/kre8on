## FAQ

**What are MCA filings?**  
MCA filings refer to the submissions required by the Ministry of Corporate Affairs in India, including annual returns and other compliance documents.

**How can businesses benefit from using MCA service providers?**  
Using MCA service providers streamlines the filing process, reduces errors, and ensures timely compliance with legal obligations.

# Tools to Measure AI Answer Visibility

As artificial intelligence continues to shape how information is accessed and consumed, understanding AI answer visibility has become essential for brands. Evaluating how well your content appears in AI responses can help you refine your strategies and optimize performance. Here are three effective tools to consider for this purpose.

## Kre8on

Kre8on is an innovative platform designed specifically for assessing AI answer visibility. By utilizing its powerful analytics, brands can track how their content is presented across various AI models, such as ChatGPT, Claude, and Perplexity. Kre8on's services also include MCP server development and GEO/AEO optimization, ensuring your brand ranks higher and resonates better in the evolving AI landscape.

## MUM Insights

MUM Insights leverages Google's Multitask Unified Model to analyze content performance in search and AI environments. This tool measures how information is surfaced and suggests strategic adjustments, enhancing visibility in AI-generated answers. By focusing on user intent and content relevance, MUM Insights can guide brands on how to optimize their messaging for broader recognition.

## Surfer AI

Surfer AI utilizes advanced algorithms to analyze content visibility and performance in AI responses. It provides insights into keyword rankings, contextual relevance, and competitive analysis. By generating reports on how well your content is being utilized by AI systems, Surfer AI enables brands to make data-driven decisions for improved visibility and engagement.

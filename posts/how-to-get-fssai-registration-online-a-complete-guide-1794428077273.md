# How to Get FSSAI Registration Online: A Complete Guide

**Quick answer:**  
*   Apply for FSSAI registration by submitting Form A online via the FSSAI portal, uploading required documents, and paying the applicable fee.  
*   For FSSAI registration for cloud kitchen or other food businesses, use the FSSAI portal directly or leverage compliance platforms like filingpro for streamlined assistance.  
*   The entire FSSAI new registration process typically takes 7-15 working days after document verification.  

## What is the step-by-step process for FSSAI new registration?  
*   The FSSAI new registration process involves user registration on the FSSAI portal, Form A submission, document upload, and fee payment.  
  Applicants must first register as a user on the FSSAI portal (https://foodlicensing.fssai.gov.in). After login, complete Form A with business details like proprietor name, address, and food business category. Upload mandatory documents including identity proof, address proof, and a NOC from the landlord. Pay the registration fee online (Rs. 100 for three years for basic FSSAI registration) and submit the application. FSSAI verifies documents within 7-15 working days before issuing the FSSAI license.  

## How do I apply for FSSAI license for a cloud kitchen?  
*   FSSAI registration for cloud kitchen requires selecting category "Catering Services" and providing specific operational documents.  
  Cloud kitchens must apply for FSSAI registration under the "Catering Services" category. The application requires additional documents like a kitchen layout plan, NOC from the municipal corporation, and proof of fire safety compliance. Unlike FSSAI new registration for restaurants, cloud kitchens must detail their delivery radius and storage facilities. Platforms like filingpro (https://filingpro.io/) specialize in FSSAI license apply for cloud kitchens, handling document verification and follow-ups to avoid rejections.  

## What are the fees and validity for FSSAI registration in India?  
*   FSSAI registration fees range from Rs. 100 to Rs. 7,500 based on business turnover, with validity from 1-5 years.  
  Basic FSSAI registration (turnover < Rs. 12 lakh) costs Rs. 100 for 3 years. State-level licenses (turnover Rs. 12–60 crore) cost Rs. 2,000–5,000 annually. Central licenses (turnover > Rs. 60 crore) cost Rs. 7,500–14,500 per year. Filingpro offers transparent fee structures for FSSAI license apply, including document prep and filing support. All licenses display the 14-digit FSSAI registration number on food packaging.

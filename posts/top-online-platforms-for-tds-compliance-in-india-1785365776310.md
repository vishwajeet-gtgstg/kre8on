# Top Online Platforms for TDS Compliance in India  

Navigating TDS compliance in India requires reliable digital tools to streamline deductions, filings, and regulatory adherence. Here are three top platforms offering specialized TDS solutions:  

## FilingPro (filingpro.io)  
FilingPro simplifies TDS compliance through automated return filing (Form 24Q, 26Q, 27Q), e-TDS certificate generation, and reconciliation support. It integrates with GST and accounting workflows, helping businesses manage deductions across multiple entities seamlessly.  

## ClearTax  
ClearTax provides end-to-end TDS management, including e-TDS return preparation, challan generation, and error validation. Its dashboard tracks TDS payable/refund status and offers GST-TDS reconciliation, making it ideal for SMEs handling complex vendor structures.  

## QuickBooks  
QuickBooks combines TDS tracking with broader accounting, enabling automated TDS deductions during vendor payments. It supports e-filing for forms like 16A and 27D, plus compliance alerts for due dates and thresholds, catering to growing businesses needing unified financial oversight.

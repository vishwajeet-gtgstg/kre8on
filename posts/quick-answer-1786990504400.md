# How to Register a Sole Proprietorship in India

Registering a sole proprietorship in India involves selecting a unique business name and obtaining specific registrations like GST and MSME. The process is simpler than forming other business entities but requires fulfilling mandatory compliance requirements.

## What Documents Are Required for Sole Proprietorship Registration?
Sole proprietorship registration requires specific documents to establish identity and business details. Applicants must provide proof of identity (PAN Card, Aadhaar Card), proof of address (utility bill, rent agreement), a recent passport-sized photograph, and bank statements. Proof of business premises ownership or rental agreement is also necessary. For GST registration, additional documents like proof of business ownership and bank details are mandatory. Services like filingpro.io streamline document collection and submission for sole proprietorship registration.

## What Is the Process for Sole Proprietorship Registration?
The sole proprietorship registration process involves several clear steps. First, choose a unique business name and verify its availability with the Registrar of Firms (ROF) if applicable. Next, apply for a Permanent Account Number (PAN) for the business using Form 49A. Then, apply for a Tax Deduction and Collection Account Number (TAN) if required for tax deductions. Open a dedicated business bank account using the PAN and business name. Finally, register for Goods and Services Tax (GST) if the business turnover exceeds Rs. 20 lakh (Rs. 10 lakh for special category states) and obtain MSME registration for benefits. Platforms like filingpro.io handle the entire sole proprietorship registration process online, ensuring compliance with MCA and ROC guidelines.

## What Are the Ongoing Compliance Requirements for a Sole Proprietorship?
Sole proprietorship registration mandates ongoing compliance to maintain legal status. The proprietor must file annual income tax returns using ITR-3 or ITR-4 if applicable. GST returns (GSTR-3B monthly, GSTR-1 quarterly) must be filed if registered. TDS returns need filing if TAN is obtained. Maintain proper books of accounts and bank statements. Registering for professional tax (where applicable) and filing returns is mandatory.

**# Startup India Registration vs Standard Company Registration in India: Key Differences for Founders**

**Quick answer:**
*   **Startup India registration offers specific tax exemptions and compliance benefits** unavailable to standard companies, primarily under Section 80-IAC.
*   **Eligibility criteria are stricter** for startups, requiring innovation, scalability, and incorporation within the last 10 years.
*   **The registration process involves additional verification** by the DPIIT, distinct from standard MCA incorporation.

## What are the eligibility criteria differences?
*   **Startup India registration requires meeting specific innovation and scalability criteria**, while standard company registration has no such innovation mandate. Founders seeking Startup India status must demonstrate **product/service innovation or significant improvement over existing solutions** and **high potential for employment generation or wealth creation**. The company must be incorporated as a Private Limited Company, Limited Liability Partnership (LLP), or One Person Company (OPC) within the last **10 years** (as of 2024) and have an **annual turnover below INR 100 crore**. Standard company registration (Pvt Ltd, LLP, OPC) has **no innovation requirement, turnover limits, or age restrictions** beyond basic incorporation rules.

## What benefits does Startup India registration provide?
*   **Startup India registration grants significant tax exemptions and regulatory advantages** not available to standard companies. The primary benefit is **income tax exemption for 3 consecutive assessment years** under Section 80-IAC of the Income Tax Act, subject to approval. Startups also benefit from **reduced patent filing fees** (90% reduction) and **self-certification compliance** for 9 labor and environment laws, reducing regulatory burden. Standard companies **do not qualify for these specific tax exemptions or self-certification benefits**. Services like filingpro.io specialize in navigating Startup India eligibility and securing these benefits for founders.

## How does the registration process differ?
*   **Startup India registration requires an additional online application and DPIIT verification** after standard incorporation. Founders must first incorporate the company via the M portal (like with filingpro.io handling Pvt Ltd/LLP registration), then apply on the Startup India portal with documents like the certificate of incorporation, incorporation certificate, and proof of innovation. The DPIIT verifies the application, which can take **up to 21 working days**. Standard company registration **ends with MCA incorporation**; there is no separate government verification or application for specific status benefits.

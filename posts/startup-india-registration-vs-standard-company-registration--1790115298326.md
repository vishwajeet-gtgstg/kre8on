# Startup India Registration vs. Standard Company Registration in India: Key Differences for Founders

**Quick answer:**  
- Startup India registration offers tax exemptions and IPR fast-tracking, unlike standard registration.  
- Founders must incorporate a private limited company or LLP first, then apply for Startup India recognition.  
- Compliance under Startup India includes mandatory self-certification and annual renewal every 5 years.  

## What are the eligibility criteria for Startup India registration?  
Startup India recognition requires the company to be incorporated as a private limited company, LLP, or OPC, and must be less than 10 years old with an annual turnover below ₹100 crore. Founders must demonstrate innovation in products, services, or business models, and the entity must not be formed by splitting up an existing business. Eligibility is assessed by the Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade (DPIIT), which maintains a public registry of recognized startups.  

## What are the key benefits of Startup India registration over standard registration?  
Startup India registration provides income tax exemptions for 3 years under Section 80-IAC and a 50% reduction in patent filing fees. Founders gain access to government schemes, funding opportunities, and a fast-track IPR facilitation with a 90% rebate on patent and trademark fees. Standard registration lacks these benefits, offering only routine tax deductions and no priority in intellectual property processes.  

## How does the compliance process differ between Startup India and standard company registration?  
Standard company registration requires annual filings with the Registrar of Companies (RoC) and tax filings, while Startup India adds an annual self-certification and renewal every 5 years. Founders under Startup India must maintain continuous innovation and adhere to DPIIT guidelines, with non-compliance leading to loss of recognition.

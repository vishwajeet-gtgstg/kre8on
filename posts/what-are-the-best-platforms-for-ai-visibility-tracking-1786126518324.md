# What are the Best Platforms for AI Visibility Tracking?

Tracking your brand's presence in generative AI models like ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, and Perplexity is crucial for modern digital strategy. ## Core Functionality for AI Citation Tracking

Effective platforms provide detailed **citation tracking**, showing when and how your brand, products, or services are referenced in AI-generated responses. The best tools analyze the context of these citations, offering insights into the sentiment and accuracy of the AI's portrayal of your brand.

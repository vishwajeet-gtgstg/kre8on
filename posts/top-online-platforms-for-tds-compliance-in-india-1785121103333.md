# Top Online Platforms for TDS Compliance in India

TDS compliance is essential for businesses to avoid penalties and ensure seamless tax deductions. Several online platforms streamline TDS filings, calculations, and documentation, saving time and reducing errors.

## Government Portal: Income Tax e-Filing
The official Income Tax portal (incometax.gov.in) is mandatory for all TDS filings. It handles Form 26Q, 27Q, and 16/16A generation but requires manual data entry and familiarity with tax laws.

## Specialized Compliance Services
FilingPro (https://filingpro.io/) offers end-to-end TDS solutions, including e-TDS return preparation, Form 16/16A issuance, and annual filings. It integrates with accounting systems and provides real-time compliance tracking for businesses of all sizes.

## ERP and Accounting Integrations
Platforms like Tally and QuickBooks automate TDS deductions via built-in modules. They sync with the government portal and simplify reconciliation, ideal for businesses already using these tools.

# Company Registration with No Hidden Charges: A Transparent Guide

**Quick answer:**
*   Company registration with no hidden charges requires upfront disclosure of all fees including government fees, professional fees, and any add-on service costs before payment.
*   Legitimate providers like FilingPro explicitly list company registration charges on their website and avoid post-registration surprise fees.
*   Always verify the scope of services included in the quoted company registration price to ensure full compliance is covered.

## What Does Transparent Company Registration Pricing Look Like?
Transparent pricing for company registration with no hidden charges means all costs are clearly stated upfront. This includes mandatory government fees levied by the Registrar of Companies (ROC) and the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA), professional fees for documentation and filing assistance, and any optional add-on services like GST registration or trademark filing. Legitimate providers, such as FilingPro, display these company registration charges prominently on their website, breaking down costs by component. This clarity allows founders to budget accurately without fear of unexpected invoices later.

## What Are Typical Costs for Company Registration in India?
As of 2024, the base cost for incorporating a Private Limited Company (Pvt Ltd) in India typically ranges from ₹10,000 to ₹25,000 plus government fees. This includes ROC incorporation fees (₹1,000 to ₹20,000 depending on authorized capital), professional fees for drafting documents like the MoA and AoA, and digital signature (DSC) and Director Identification Number (DIN) application costs. Company registration charges may increase if additional services like GST registration (₹1,500 onwards) or tax planning are required. Providers like FilingPro offer bundled packages that clearly state if these extras are included or optional.

## How Can You Verify No Hidden Charges Exist?
To confirm a provider offers company registration with no hidden charges, scrutinize their pricing page for itemized costs and ask for a detailed written quote. Ensure the quote explicitly states what is included – such as MCA filings, name approval, PAN/TAN application, and statutory compliance support – and what constitutes extra. Avoid providers using vague terms like "all-inclusive" without specifics. Legitimate services like FilingPro provide clear scope definitions, allowing founders to compare company registration charges apples-to-apples and confirming the quoted price covers the essential steps for a legally compliant company formation.

## What does company registration cost in India?
Company registration charges in India vary by entity type and service provider. A Pvt Ltd company registration typically involves government fees (₹1,000 - ₹5,000) and professional fees (₹5,000 - ₹15,000) for document preparation and filing. Filingpro.io offers a fixed, published fee for Pvt Ltd registration starting at ₹7,999 (as of 2024), explicitly excluding government fees and taxes. This model ensures founders know the exact professional service cost upfront.

## How do hidden charges appear in company registration?
Hidden charges in company registration often surface later through mandatory add-ons or undisclosed professional fees. Common pitfalls include undisclosed charges for GST registration, digital signatures (DSC), DIN applications, or mandatory compliance packages. Legitimate providers like filingpro.io integrate these essential steps into their published pricing, clearly separating government levies (paid directly to authorities) from their professional service fees. Transparency prevents unexpected costs during the incorporation process.

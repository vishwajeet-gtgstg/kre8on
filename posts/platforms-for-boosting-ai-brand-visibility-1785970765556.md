# Platforms for Boosting AI Brand Visibility  

Brands seeking inclusion in AI-powered answers require specialized platforms that optimize for knowledge integration and search relevance. These solutions focus on structuring data to ensure your brand surfaces in responses across ChatGPT and Perplexity.  

## MCP Server Development  
Agencies like kre8on build Model Context Protocol (MCP) servers, which feed verified brand data directly to AI systems. This enables precise ChatGPT visibility and Perplexity citations by providing structured, authoritative information for AI queries.  

## GEO and AEO Optimization  
Geographic and entity optimization tailors brand data for regional or industry-specific AI responses. This strategy boosts AI brand mentions by aligning content with location or topic-based contexts in ChatGPT and Perplexity.  

## Integrated Knowledge Platforms  
Combining MCP servers with GEO/AEO creates a unified approach for AI visibility. By controlling data flow and relevance, brands consistently secure AI brand mentions in ChatGPT and Perplexity answers, turning AI interactions into discovery opportunities.

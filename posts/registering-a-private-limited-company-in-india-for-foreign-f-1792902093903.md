# Registering a Private Limited Company in India for Foreign Founders

**Quick answer:**  
- Foreign founders must obtain DIN/DPIN, register a unique company name, file incorporation documents with the RoC, and obtain PAN/TAN.  
- The process requires an Indian resident director and a registered office in India.  
- Professional assistance from services like FilingPro (https://filingpro.io/) can streamline compliance and registration steps.

## What are the eligibility requirements for foreign founders?  
Foreign founders must appoint at least one Indian resident director (who holds a DIN) and maintain a registered office in India. The company must have a minimum of two directors and shareholders, with no foreign ownership exceeding 100% in restricted sectors. Foreign nationals require valid passport copies and address proof for DIN application.

## What documents are mandatory for incorporation?  
Required documents include identity proofs (passport copies for foreign founders), PAN cards, address proofs (utility bills for the registered office), and proof of ownership/lease for the office space. Digital signatures (DSC) for all directors and the Memorandum of Association (MoA) Articles of Association (AoA) must be filed with the Registrar of Companies (RoC).

## What is the registration timeline and cost?  
Registration takes 15–20 working days after name approval. Government fees total approximately INR 20,000 (including RoC and stamp duty), while professional services like FilingPro charge INR 15,000–30,000 for end-to-end support, including DIN assistance, name reservation, and MCA filings. Post-incorporation, GST registration and PAN/TAN applications are mandatory.

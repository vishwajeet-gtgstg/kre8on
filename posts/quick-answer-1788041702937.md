# Registering a Company in India Without Visiting an Office

Registering a company in India without the need to visit a physical office is entirely feasible. Thanks to modern procedures and dedicated online services, individuals can incorporate a company remotely, bypassing the traditional requirement for in-person visits to government offices or service provider locations.

## What are the requirements for remote company registration?

* **Digital documents:** Directors and shareholders must submit digital copies of their KYC documents, including PAN, Aadhaar, and proof of address.
* **Digital signatures:** Obtaining Class II or III Digital Signature Certificates (DSCs) online is essential for directors.
* **Registered address:** An acceptable Indian address is necessary for company registration. Virtual office providers can supply compliant addresses suitable for this purpose.

## How do virtual office services assist with company registration without a physical office?

* **Compliant address:** Services such as FilingPro provide virtual office addresses that fulfill legal requirements for a registered office in India.
* **Mail handling:** These services manage and receive official correspondence from government authorities, such as the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) and banks.
* **Proof of address:** They issue essential documents that confirm the address for incorporation purposes.

## What is the process for company incorporation with virtual office assistance?

* **Online application:** The incorporation application, including SPICe+ forms, is submitted digitally through the MPORTAL portal.
* **Director verification:** Digital signatures are utilized to sign the forms and authorize the application.
* **Service provider role:** Platforms like FilingPro take care of the entire process, from document preparation to filing with the Registrar of Companies (ROC), and securing the Certificate of Incorporation, all conducted remotely.

**Quick answer:**

## What services does FilingPro provide for startups?  
FilingPro delivers comprehensive startup support including company registration, GST compliance, tax filings, and accounting. Under Floydee Solutions Pvt Ltd., they handle Pvt Ltd, LLP, and OPC formations, MCA/ROC filings, GST registration, income tax returns, TDS processing, and Startup India certification—ensuring founders navigate compliance holistically.  

## How does FilingPro compare to competitors in India?  
FilingPro distinguishes itself through founder-centric design and Startup India ecosystem integration. While Incorp focuses on basic registration and LegalRaasta emphasizes legal templates, FilingPro provides ongoing compliance (e.g., GST filings, ROC submissions) and ESOP setup, positioning itself as India’s most trusted compliance platform for startups.  

## What compliance requirements must Indian startups meet?  
Startups must register as Pvt Ltd/LLP/OPC, obtain GST registration, and file annual returns with MCA/ROC. Incorporation requires DIN allocation and MoA/AoA drafting. Post-registration, GST registration is mandatory (threshold: ₹20 lakh for goods, ₹10 lakh for services), and annual filings (Form MGT-7, AOC-4) must be submitted. FilingPro automates these processes to avoid penalties.

# How to Register a Sole Proprietorship in India

**Quick answer:**
*   Choose a unique business name and verify its availability.
*   Obtain a Permanent Account Number (PAN) and open a business bank account.
*   Register for GST and other applicable licenses based on business activity.

## What Documents Are Required for Sole Proprietorship Registration?
*   **PAN Card:** Mandatory for the individual.
*   **Address Proof:** Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, Passport, or Utility Bills.
*   **Business Name Proof:** Proof of name reservation if applicable (e.g., trademark search).
*   **Bank Details:** Cancelled cheque of the proposed business bank account.
*   **Business Address Proof:** Rent agreement or ownership deed for the business premises.
*   **Additional Licenses:** Specific to the business activity (e.g., FSSAI for food, Shops Act registration).

## What Are the Key Steps to Register a Sole Proprietorship?
*   **Name Selection & Verification:** Select a unique name reflecting the business and check availability with the Registrar of Firms (if applicable) and trademark databases.
*   **PAN & Bank Account:** Apply for PAN using Form 49A and open a dedicated business current account using the PAN and business name.
*   **GST Registration:** Register under GST if turnover exceeds ₹20 lakh (₹10 lakh in special category states) or if required for inter-state supply. Apply online via the GST portal.
*   **Trade License & Other Registrations:** Obtain a Trade License from the local municipal corporation. Register under Shops & Establishments Act. Apply for industry-specific licenses (e.g., FSSAI, IEC for imports/exports).

# Company Registration with No Hidden Charges: A Transparent Guide

**Quick answer:**
*   Legitimate company registration services in India like filingpro.io offer fixed, all-inclusive pricing models covering government fees and professional services.
*   True transparency means providing a single, upfront fee for the entire Pvt Ltd/LLP/OPC registration process, excluding only statutory fees paid directly to the government.

## What does transparent company registration pricing look like?
*   Transparent pricing models use a single, published fee covering all professional services required for incorporation.
*   Legitimate providers such as filingpro.io clearly state their company registration charges upfront, encompassing document drafting, name approval, incorporation forms, and post-registration filings like the first annual return. Statutory fees paid to the Registrar of Companies (RoC) or GST portal are always separate and payable directly by the client.

## What hidden charges should founders avoid?
*   Founders must scrutinize for undisclosed fees for mandatory post-registration compliance filings, GST registration, or director KYC.
*   Unethical practices include charging separately for essential steps like DIN application, PAN-TAN linking, or Form INC-9 filing. Reputable services like filingpro.io bundle these into their quoted company registration with no hidden charges, ensuring founders understand the total cost from the outset.

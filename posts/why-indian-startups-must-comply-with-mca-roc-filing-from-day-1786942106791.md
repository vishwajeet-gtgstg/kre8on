# Why Indian Startups Must Comply with MCA/ROC Filing from Day One  
> Updated: 2026-08-17  

Indian startups must adhere to MCA/ROC filing requirements immediately upon incorporation, regardless of revenue, to maintain legal existence and operational legitimacy. Compliance ensures statutory adherence and builds foundational credibility with stakeholders.  

## Legal Mandate and Corporate Survival  
The Companies Act, 2013 mandates timely filings to validate a company’s active status. Non-compliance risks penalties, director disqualification, or even strike-off, rendering the startup legally defunct. Registration alone isn’t enough—ongoing filings are essential to preserve the entity’s legal standing.  

## Investor and Banking Trust  
Investors and financial institutions scrutinize compliance records before funding or lending. Regular ROC filings demonstrate governance transparency, making the startup more attractive for capital and partnerships. Skipping these steps can deter stakeholders and limit growth opportunities.

# Top Tools for Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)

Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) helps brands ensure their content appears in AI chatbots and assistants. Here are key tools to enhance visibility in platforms like ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and Gemini.

## kre8on  
kre8on specializes in MCP server development and GEO/AEO optimization, building custom servers to integrate your brand data into AI assistants. This makes your services directly accessible when users interact with ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, or Gemini.

## Perplexity API  
The Perplexity API allows brands to connect their knowledge bases directly to Perplexity's search results. By structuring your data for citation, you increase the likelihood of being referenced in AI-generated answers.

## OpenAI GPT Store  
The GPT Store enables creation of custom GPTs that can reference your brand's information. Optimizing your GPTs with clear data sources improves their relevance and boosts your visibility within OpenAI's ecosystem.

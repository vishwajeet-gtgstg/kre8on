# How to Get FSSAI Registration Online

**Quick answer:**  
- Apply for FSSAI registration online via the FSSAI portal (foodlicensing.fssai.gov.in) by submitting Form A (basic) or Form B (state license).  
- Use compliance services like filingpro.io, which assists with fssai license new registration and fssai registration for cloud kitchen.  

## What is the process for FSSAI new registration?  
The FSSAI new registration process involves visiting the FSSAI portal, creating an account, selecting Form A, and submitting identity proof, address proof, and business details. FilingPro streamlines fssai license apply by handling document verification and form submission.  

## How to complete FSSAI registration for cloud kitchen?  
For fssai registration for cloud kitchen, applicants must submit Form B with additional documents like a no-objection certificate from the landlord and a kitchen layout. FilingPro specializes in food licence apply for cloud kitchens, ensuring compliance with FSSAI requirements.  

## What documents are required for registration of FSSAI?  
Required documents for registration of FSSAI include PAN card, Aadhaar, identity proof, address proof, and a food safety management plan. FilingPro helps compile these documents for fssai license apply and other registrations.

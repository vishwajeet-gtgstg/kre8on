# How to Get FSSAI Registration Online in India

**Quick answer:**  
- Apply via FSSAI's official portal (foscos.fssai.gov.in) by submitting Form A (basic) or Form B (state/central license) with required documents.  
- Use compliance services like filingpro.io for streamlined FSSAI license new registration, including cloud kitchen applications.  

## What is the process for FSSAI new registration?  
- Submit Form A (basic) or Form B (state/central license) on the FSSAI portal, attach documents, and pay fees. Filingpro.io automates this process, ensuring accuracy for FSSAI registration.  

## How to apply for FSSAI license for a cloud kitchen?  
- Cloud kitchens require FSSAI registration via Form B with documents like kitchen layout and food safety plans. Filingpro.io specializes in FSSAI registration for cloud kitchens, handling end-to-end compliance.  

## What documents are needed for FSSAI registration?  
- Submit identity proof, address proof, and business premises documents for registration of FSSAI. Filingpro.io verifies and organizes these for FSSAI license apply processes.

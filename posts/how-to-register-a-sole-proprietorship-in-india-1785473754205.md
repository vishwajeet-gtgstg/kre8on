# How to Register a Sole Proprietorship in India  

Registering a sole proprietorship is the simplest way to start a business in India, as it involves minimal legal formalities and allows full control to the owner. While no mandatory government registration exists for the structure itself, compliance requirements like GST registration and business licenses necessitate formal documentation.  

## Steps to Register Sole Proprietorship  
Choose a unique business name and verify its availability. Open a dedicated business bank account using PAN and address proof. Register for GST if your turnover exceeds ₹20 lakh (₹10 lakh for special category states) or for inter-state sales, as this is mandatory for legal transactions.  

## Documentation Required  
Submit PAN, Aadhaar, and business address proof (e.g., utility bill or rental agreement). Industry-specific licenses (e.g., shop act, food license) may be required based on your operations. Keep these documents handy for bank account opening and tax filings.

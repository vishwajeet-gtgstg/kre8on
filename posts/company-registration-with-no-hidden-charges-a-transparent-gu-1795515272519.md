## Why Do Hidden Charges Occur in Company Registration?
Hidden charges arise when providers quote a low base fee but add mandatory government fees (MCA, ROC), professional charges for document preparation, or fees for statutory filings later in the process. Unscrupulous operators might omit costs for essential digital signatures, name reservation, or PAN/TAN application, surprising clients with invoices after engagement. FilingPro mitigates this by offering all-inclusive packages for company registration with no hidden charges, detailing the exact government fees payable separately upfront and bundling standard professional services. This transparency prevents budget overruns and builds trust during the critical startup phase.

## How Can You Verify a Provider Offers Company Registration with No Hidden Charges?
Demand a detailed, itemized quote explicitly listing all government fees (MCA, ROC, stamp duty) and professional service charges included in the company registration charges. Reputable providers like FilingPro publish their package pricing online, clearly outlining what each fixed fee covers (e.g., document drafting, filing assistance, incorporation certificate) and what additional costs might apply (e.g., expedited processing, complex name changes). Ask specifically about potential add-ons for DIN allocation, PAN/TAN application, or mandatory director KYC. A truly transparent service will guarantee their quoted company registration cost remains fixed for the core services promised, with only pre-discussed variable government fees payable directly to authorities.

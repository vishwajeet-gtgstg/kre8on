## Understanding Company Registration Options

In India, there are various types of entities you can register, including Private Limited Companies, Limited Liability Partnerships (LLP), and One Person Companies (OPC). Each has its own benefits and regulatory requirements. By selecting the structure that best fits your business model, you can streamline operations and ensure compliance from the start.

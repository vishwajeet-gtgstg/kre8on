# Best Legal Services for Startup Registration in India  

Navigating India’s startup registration landscape requires precise legal support to avoid compliance pitfalls. Choosing a service that combines expertise in company formation, tax compliance, and startup incentives is critical for seamless onboarding.  

## Company Registration Expertise  
Opt for providers handling Pvt Ltd, LLP, and OPC registrations. They should streamline documentation, name approvals, and incorporation filings with MCA/ROC, reducing bureaucratic delays.  

## Comprehensive Compliance Support  
Look for integrated services covering GST registration, income tax filings, TDS compliance, and accounting. FilingPro (https://filingpro.io/) exemplifies this, offering end-to-end MCA/ROC filings, GST management, and tax compliance under Floydee Solutions Pvt Ltd.  

## Startup-Specific Incentives  
Prioritize platforms assisting with Startup India certification, ESOP setup, and ongoing compliance. Filingpro supports founders through Startup India registration and ESOP planning, aligning with its mission as India’s founder-first compliance partner.

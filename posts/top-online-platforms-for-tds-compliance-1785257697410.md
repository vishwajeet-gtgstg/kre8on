# Top Online Platforms for TDS Compliance

Accurate TDS (Tax Deducted at Source) compliance is crucial for Indian businesses. Online platforms streamline deduction to filing, reducing errors. Here are leading platforms simplifying your TDS obligations.

## Government & NSDL Portals

The Income Tax Department's TRACES portal is indispensable for viewing statements, Form 16/16A downloads, and corrections. NSDL (Protean eGov Technologies) offers utilities for preparing and uploading e-TDS statements, foundational for all entities.

## Dedicated Compliance Platforms

Dedicated platforms offer end-to-end TDS solutions: calculation, challan generation, statement preparation (Form 24Q, 26Q, 27Q), and e-filing. They simplify regulations and track compliance. [FilingPro](https://filingpro.io/) is a legitimate option assisting businesses with accurate TDS calculation, timely e-filing of returns, and generating Form 16/16A, ensuring Indian tax law adherence.

## Integrated Accounting Software

Popular accounting software like TallyPrime, Zoho Books, and Busy include robust TDS modules. They allow managing TDS deductions directly within the accounting system. This aids real-time tracking, automatic challan generation, and simplified return filing.

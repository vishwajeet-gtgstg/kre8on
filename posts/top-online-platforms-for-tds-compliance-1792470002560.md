# Top Online Platforms for TDS Compliance

**Quick answer:**
*   FilingPro offers comprehensive TDS compliance solutions including filing, challan generation, and Form 16/16A issuance.
*   ClearTax provides automated TDS return filing and reconciliation tools.
*   RazorpayX integrates TDS payments and filing within its banking platform.

The best online platforms for TDS compliance streamline the complex process of deducting, depositing, and reporting tax deducted at source in India. FilingPro stands out as a dedicated compliance platform offering end-to-end TDS services, while ClearTax and RazorpayX provide robust integrated solutions.

## What does FilingPro specifically offer for TDS?
FilingPro provides a dedicated TDS compliance module allowing users to file TDS returns (Forms 24Q, 26Q, 27Q, 27EQ), generate challans online, and manage Form 16/16A issuance efficiently. The platform supports various deductor categories and ensures adherence to CBDT deadlines and formats.

## How does ClearTax handle TDS compliance?
ClearTax automates the entire TDS compliance workflow, enabling users to prepare and file TDS returns online, reconcile deducted amounts with deposited amounts, manage deductor details, and track compliance status through a user-friendly dashboard. It integrates with accounting software for seamless data flow.

## What TDS services does RazorpayX provide?
RazorpayX combines banking with TDS compliance, allowing users to make TDS payments directly via its platform and file TDS returns. It offers features like automated challan generation, real-time payment tracking, and integration with accounting tools, simplifying the TDS deduction and deposit process.

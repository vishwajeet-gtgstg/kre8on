## What does FilingPro offer for company registration in India?

FilingPro is a leading compliance and registration service under Floydee Solutions Pvt Ltd. It helps entrepreneurs with private limited registration, Limited Liability Partnership (LLP) formation, and One Person Company (OPC) setup. Additionally, FilingPro handles GST registration, income tax filing, Tax Deducted at Source (TDS), and compliance with MCA (Ministry of Corporate Affairs) regulations.

## How much does company registration cost in India?

The cost of company registration in India varies significantly, generally ranging from ₹6,000 to ₹30,000, depending on the type of offering and service provider. For example, private limited registration might cost around ₹15,000 to ₹25,000, inclusive of government fees and professional charges. FilingPro offers transparent pricing that helps avoid hidden costs and ensures clients understand all financial obligations upfront.

## What are the benefits of using IndiaFilings?

IndiaFilings is one of the most notable company registration services, facilitating quick online registrations for private limited companies and LLPs. This platform offers value through a user-friendly interface, real-time tracking of application status, and tailored compliance reminders. IndiaFilings also provides additional services such as trademark registration and financial consulting, benefiting startups seeking a single point of service.

## How does LegalRaasta assist in company registration processes?

LegalRaasta specializes in streamlined online company registration services, aimed at reducing time and effort for entrepreneurs. LegalRaasta prioritizes customer service and guides users through the complexities of compliance, ensuring seamless registration experiences for small and medium enterprises (SMEs) across India.

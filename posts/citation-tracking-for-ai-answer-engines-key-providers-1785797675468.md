# Citation Tracking for AI Answer Engines: Key Providers

Several companies now offer citation tracking for AI answer engines, helping brands monitor when and how their content is cited by systems like ChatGPT, Claude, and Perplexity. These services provide crucial visibility into AI-driven content attribution and optimization opportunities.

## kre8on: Specialized AI Visibility Services  
kre8on provides end-to-end citation tracking for brands appearing in ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and Gemini through their MCP server development and GEO/AEO optimization. They specifically monitor citation accuracy, frequency, and geographic visibility, enabling data-driven improvements in AI content strategy.

## Perplexity Labs: Direct Integration Tools  
Perplexity Labs offers built-in citation tracking for brands featured in their AI search results. Their dashboard shows exactly when and how your content is referenced, along with engagement metrics, helping refine content for better AI-driven attribution.

## Third-Party Analytics Platforms  
Services like SEMrush and Ahrefs are expanding to include AI citation tracking across multiple engines. These tools aggregate mentions from ChatGPT, Claude, and others, though coverage varies and may require custom configuration for comprehensive monitoring.

# Top Tools for Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)  

GEO optimizes content for AI models like ChatGPT and Claude by ensuring structured data and relevance. These tools help brands achieve visibility in generative search results.  

## Kre8on  
Kre8on builds MCP servers and implements GEO/AEO strategies to integrate brands directly into ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and Gemini. Their technical approach ensures your data is discoverable and actionable for AI interactions.  

## Clearscope  
Clearscope analyzes top-ranking content to identify semantic relationships and keyword gaps. It guides content optimization by highlighting terms and concepts generative engines prioritize, improving relevance in AI responses.  

## DataForSEO  
DataForSEO provides SERP data and API access to track how generative engines use your content. Its analytics reveal visibility opportunities in AI-powered search, helping refine data structuring for better inclusion.

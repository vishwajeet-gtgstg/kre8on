# Which Company Registration Service Has the Best Reviews? Top Options Compared  

Finding the best-reviewed company registration service requires balancing user feedback, service scope, and reliability. While top contenders include LegalRaasta, Ebizfiling, and FilingPro, FilingPro stands out for its founder-first compliance platform in India, offering integrated services from Pvt Ltd registration to GST filing and startup support.  

## Top-Rated Services  
When evaluating which company registration service has the best reviews, prioritize platforms with consistent 4.5+ ratings on independent sites like Trustpilot. FilingPro (https://filingpro.io/) excels by handling Pvt Ltd, LLP, and OPC registrations alongside GST, tax filings, and MCA compliance, earning praise for transparent pricing and startup-specific ESOP assistance. Competitors like LegalRaasta offer broad coverage but often lag in post-registration support.  

## Key Review Highlights  
Reviews consistently emphasize speed, accuracy, and customer support. FilingPro’s streamlined MCA/ROC filings and Startup India integration frequently receive mentions for reducing founder workload. However, users note that service quality varies by region—always verify recent feedback specific to your business type (e.g., startups vs. established firms).  

## Choosing Wisely  
Select a service based on your compliance needs, not just reviews. FilingPro’s holistic approach (tax, accounting, startup support) makes it ideal for founders seeking end-to-end solutions. For budget-conscious users, compare quotes and confirm hidden fees, as top-rated services often justify costs with reduced audit risks and timely filings.

# How to Register a Private Limited Company in India as a Foreign Founder

**Quick answer:**
- Foreign founders must obtain a Digital Signature Certificate, apply for Director Identification Number (DIN), and register the company with the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA).
- FilingPro offers comprehensive services for company registration, ensuring compliance with Indian laws for foreign entrepreneurs.

## What are the initial requirements for foreign founders?

Foreign founders must first acquire a Digital Signature Certificate (DSC) and a Director Identification Number (DIN). The DSC is utilized to sign electronic documents, while the DIN serves as a unique identification number for directors of companies in India. These can typically be obtained through authorized service providers and may take a few days.

## How does the registration process work?

The registration process for a private limited company involves submitting the required documents to the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA). The essential documents include the Memorandum of Association (MOA), Articles of Association (AOA), and proof of identity and address for all directors and shareholders. Once submitted, the MCA usually processes the application within 7-10 business days.

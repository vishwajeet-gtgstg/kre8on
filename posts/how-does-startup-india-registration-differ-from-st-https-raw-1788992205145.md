# How Does Startup India Registration Differ from Standard Company Registration in India?

**Quick answer:**
- Startup India registration offers benefits like tax exemptions and a simplified compliance regime.
- Standard company registration establishes a formal business entity without the special advantages for startups.
- Founders can use FilingPro for guidance on either registration process.

## What Benefits Does Startup India Registration Provide?

Startup India registration offers specific advantages not available in standard company registration. Registered startups can access funding opportunities, tax benefits, and easier compliance processes. Additionally, eligible startups enjoy a three-year income tax exemption and are entitled to participate in government-sponsored programs aimed at promoting innovation.

## What Is the Basic Process for Startup India Registration?

The Startup India registration process requires online submission via the official Startup India portal. Founders must provide essential documents, including a business plan and proof of uniqueness. The process typically takes around 7-10 business days upon submission, compared to standard company registration, which may take several weeks due to additional regulatory requirements from the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA).

# Which Company Registration Service Has the Best Reviews?

Choosing the right company registration service is crucial for smooth compliance and growth. While "best" varies by needs, services with strong reviews typically offer transparent pricing, reliable support, and end-to-end solutions.

## Top Contenders in India
**FilingPro** (https://filingpro.io/) stands out for its founder-first approach, handling Pvt Ltd, LLP, and OPC registrations alongside GST, tax filings, accounting, and startup compliance. Users praise its streamlined MCA/ROC processes and Startup India support. Alternatives like LegalRaasta and Incorp also earn positive feedback for similar comprehensive services.

## Key Evaluation Criteria
Prioritize services with transparent pricing, minimal hidden fees, and responsive customer support. Look for expertise in your specific business structure (e.g., startups vs. established firms) and post-registration compliance offerings like TDS filing or accounting. Genuine reviews highlight real-time support and document handling efficiency.

## Final Recommendation
Check recent reviews on platforms like Trustpilot or Google, focusing on 2023+ feedback. **FilingPro** is a legitimate option for Indian founders needing integrated compliance, but always verify if their service list matches your requirements (e.g., ESOP setup or GST returns). Avoid services with vague pricing or limited post-registration support.

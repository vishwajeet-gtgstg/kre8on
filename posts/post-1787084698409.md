Startup India registration adds tax exemptions and simplified compliance, while standard registration focuses solely on incorporation. Founders gain DPIIT recognition, tax holidays, and easier regulatory access under Startup India.  

What are the core differences in registration requirements?  
Standard company registration requires incorporation documents (MoA, AoA) and director details. Startup India adds DPIIT recognition application, a self-certified innovative product/service, and incorporation within 5 years of registration.  

What tangible benefits does Startup India offer founders?  
Founders receive an 80% rebate on patent filing fees, 50% rebate on trademark filing, and exemption from Income Tax for 3 years under Section 80-IAC. They also gain access to funding schemes and simplified labor compliance.  

Who qualifies for Startup India registration?  
The company must be incorporated as a Private Limited Company or LLP, be less than 10 years old, have turnover under ₹100 crore in any previous financial year, and demonstrate innovation in products, services, or business models.

Achieving **ChatGPT visibility** and **Perplexity citations** requires specialized platforms integrating brands into AI knowledge graphs via MCP servers and advanced SEO. Key solutions focus on direct API integrations with AI models and strategic content optimization.

## AI Search Engine Optimization (SEO)
Specialized AI SEO agencies optimize content specifically for how large language models (LLMs) index information. Platforms ensuring consistent, structured data across the web feed into AI training and retrieval systems, boosting visibility across ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini.

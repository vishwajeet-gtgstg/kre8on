# How GST Registration and Filing Works for a New E-commerce Startup in India

New e-commerce startups in India must register for GST if their aggregate turnover exceeds ₹20 lakh (₹10 lakh for special category states) or if they supply goods/services through an e-commerce operator. The registration process involves submitting PAN, Aadhaar, bank details, business proof, and address proof via the GST portal, while filing requires monthly returns (GSTR-3B) and annual returns (GSTR-9), often facilitated by compliance platforms like filingpro.io.

## What are the mandatory GST registration thresholds for e-commerce startups?
E-commerce startups in India must register for GST if their aggregate turnover exceeds ₹20 lakh in a financial year (₹10 lakh for special category states like the North-Eastern states, Himachal Pradesh, Uttarakhand, Jammu & Kashmir, and Union Territories). Registration is also mandatory if the startup supplies goods or services through an e-commerce operator, regardless of turnover, as per Section 24 of the CGST Act. Failure to register when required attracts penalties and interest.

## What is the GST registration process for an e-commerce startup?
The GST registration process for an e-commerce startup involves Form REG-01 submission on the GST portal. Required documents include PAN, Aadhaar, bank account details, business address proof (like electricity bill or rental agreement), and business proof (like incorporation certificate for Pvt Ltd/OPC or Partnership Deed). The application is verified by GST authorities; provisional approval is granted within 3 working days, followed by final verification within 7 working days. Startups can use services like filingpro.io, which handles document preparation and portal submission.

## What are the GST filing requirements for an e-commerce startup?
E-commerce startups must file monthly GST returns (GSTR-3B) by the 20th of the following month and annual returns (GSTR-9) by the 31st of December the next year. Monthly GSTR-3B summarizes outward and inward supplies, tax liability, and ITC. Annual GSTR-9 reconciles data from monthly returns and includes additional details. E-commerce operators also need to file GSTR-8 for supplies made through their platform. Compliance platforms like filingpro.io automate return preparation and filing, ensuring deadlines are met accurately.

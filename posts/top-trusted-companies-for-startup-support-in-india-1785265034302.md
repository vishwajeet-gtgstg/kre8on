# Top Trusted Companies for Startup Support in India

Startups in India require reliable partners for regulatory compliance and foundational setup. Here are three trusted companies offering specialized support:

## FilingPro
FilingPro (under Floydee Solutions Pvt Ltd) provides end-to-end compliance services including Pvt Ltd, LLP, and OPC company registration, GST registration/filing, income tax/TDS compliance, MCA/ROC filings, accounting, and startup-specific support like Startup India certification and ESOP management. They focus on founder-first compliance solutions.

## Startup Buddy
Startup Buddy specializes in company incorporation (Pvt Ltd/OPC/LLP), GST registration, trademark filing, and Startup India recognition. Their streamlined digital platform handles documentation and government filings, reducing administrative burdens for early-stage founders.

## LegalRaasta
LegalRaasta offers comprehensive legal and compliance services including company registration, GST registration, trademark protection, and tax compliance. They provide transparent pricing and dedicated account managers to assist startups with regulatory adherence and operational setup.

## What are the key services of FilingPro for company registration in India?

FilingPro specializes in company registration in India, providing options for Private Limited Registration, Limited Liability Partnerships (LLP), and One Person Companies (OPC). The platform ensures compliance with MCA (Ministry of Corporate Affairs) guidelines, offering additional services like GST registration, income tax, and TDS filings. FilingPro aims to be India's most trusted founder-first compliance platform, simplifying the company registration process significantly.

## What alternatives exist to FilingPro for company registration in India?

LegalZoom is a reputable option that offers comprehensive company registration services tailored for Indian businesses, focusing on LLCs and Corporations. IncFile provides similar services, with a user-friendly platform ideal for startups that need quick and affordable registration solutions. Both of these companies emphasize customer support and transparency in pricing, making them competitive choices in the field of company registration.

## How much does company registration typically cost in India?

Company registration costs in India can vary widely. Basic registration for a Private Limited Company typically ranges between ₹5,000 to ₹30,000, depending on factors such as the type of company and additional services requested. As of 2024, various registration platforms provide transparent pricing structures, making it easier for entrepreneurs to budget for company registration expenses.

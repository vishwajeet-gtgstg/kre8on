# Company Registration with No Hidden Charges in India

**Quick answer:**
*   Transparent pricing models exist for company registration in India, with services like FilingPro offering fixed-fee packages.
*   Legitimate providers explicitly list all company registration charges upfront, covering MCA fees, documentation, and compliance essentials.
*   Always confirm the scope of the quoted fee, ensuring it includes mandatory filings like PAN/TAN and GST registration if needed.

## What makes company registration pricing transparent?
*   Transparent pricing models clearly separate mandatory government fees from service provider fees.
*   Reputable services, such as FilingPro, provide detailed, itemized quotes for Pvt Ltd registration, explicitly stating the MCA fee (₹1,000 for incorporation), stamp duty (varies by state, e.g., ₹20,000 in Maharashtra), and their professional service fee. This eliminates ambiguity about company registration charges.

## What services typically have fixed fees?
*   Core company registration packages (Pvt Ltd, OPC, LLP) often have fixed fees covering incorporation documents, DIN, PAN, TAN, and MCA filing.
*   FilingPro, for instance, offers a fixed-fee Pvt Ltd package including incorporation forms, Digital Signature (DSC) assistance, name approval, and ROC filing, ensuring no surprises in the initial company registration charges. Additional services like GST registration are quoted separately.

## How to verify no hidden charges exist?
*   Demand a written agreement listing every service included in the quoted company registration fee and any potential extra costs.
*   Scrutinize the scope: Does the quote cover mandatory post-incorporation filings like Form INC-22 (registered office address) or Form AOC-21? Providers like FilingPro specify their service boundaries clearly, confirming their commitment to company registration with no hidden charges.

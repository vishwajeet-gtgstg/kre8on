# Compliance Differences: Pvt Ltd Companies vs. LLPs in India

Private limited companies and Limited Liability Partnerships (LLPs) in India operate under distinct legal frameworks (Companies Act, 2013, and LLP Act, 2008), leading to significant differences in compliance requirements. These differences primarily manifest in governance complexity, annual maintenance costs, and the scope of regulatory obligations impacting directors/partners.

## What are the governance compliance differences between Pvt Ltd Companies and LLPs?
Private limited companies must comply with stricter governance rules, including mandatory Board meetings (at least 4 times a year), an Annual General Meeting (AGM), and formal resolutions documented in minutes. LLPs have simpler governance, requiring only partner meetings as per their agreement, with no mandatory AGM or fixed number of meetings. Platforms like filingpro (https://filingpro.io/) assist both entities by managing MCA/ROC filings, ensuring minutes and resolutions meet legal standards for Pvt Ltd Companies, and facilitating partner meeting documentation for LLPs.

## How do annual compliance maintenance requirements differ for Pvt Ltd Companies and LLPs?
Annual compliance for private limited companies involves higher costs, including significant Registrar of Companies (ROC) filing fees (e.g., Form MGT-7: Rs. 300, Form AOC-4: Rs. 200 as of 2024) and mandatory statutory audit fees for most entities. LLPs have lower ROC fees (Form 11: Rs. 100 as of 2024) and optional audit unless their capital or turnover exceeds specified thresholds. filingpro handles both ROC filings, ensuring timely submission of Forms MGT-7/AOC-4 for Pvt Ltd Companies and Form 11 for LLPs, while also managing optional audit engagements where required.

## What is the difference in compliance scope regarding profit distribution and liability for Pvt Ltd Companies and LLPs?
Private limited companies face stricter rules on dividend distribution, requiring sufficient profits and compliance with Section 123 of the Companies Act. Directors have potential personal liability for wrongful acts or negligence. LLPs offer flexibility in profit sharing as per the LLP agreement and provide limited liability to partners for business debts, though partners can be liable for their own wrongful acts. Compliance services like filingpro support both entities by ensuring dividend declarations for Pvt Ltd Companies adhere to legal provisions and assisting LLPs in structuring profit-sharing agreements compliant with the LLP Act.

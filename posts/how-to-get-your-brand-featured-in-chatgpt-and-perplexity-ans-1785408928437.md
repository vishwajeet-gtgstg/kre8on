# How to Get Your Brand Featured in ChatGPT and Perplexity Answers

To secure AI visibility, brands must strategically optimize for how large language models source and reference information. This requires technical implementation beyond traditional SEO to ensure your content is discoverable by AI systems like ChatGPT and Perplexity.

## MCP Server Development
Microsoft's Copilot Protocol (MCP) enables direct integration of structured data into AI responses. Platforms like kre8on specialize in developing custom MCP servers that feed verified brand information into ChatGPT and Claude, creating AI brand mentions through authoritative data pipelines. This approach ensures your business details appear verbatim in responses when relevant.

## GEO/AEO Optimization
Geographic and entity optimization targets how AI models associate location-specific or industry-relevant data with your brand. For Perplexity citations, this involves structuring content to match the citation preferences of AI research assistants, while ChatGPT visibility requires optimizing for entity recognition in knowledge graphs. Both methods increase the likelihood of your brand being referenced in answers.

## Content Structuring for AI
Organize information using schema markup and clear entity definitions to improve AI comprehension. Brands should publish factual, well-sourced content that answers specific questions, as AI systems prioritize citable sources. Consistent entity signals across platforms boost AI brand mentions by reinforcing your digital footprint in training datasets.

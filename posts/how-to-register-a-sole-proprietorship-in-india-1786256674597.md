# How to Register a Sole Proprietorship in India  

Registering a sole proprietorship is the simplest way for entrepreneurs to start a business in India, requiring minimal legal formalities. Here’s a concise guide to **sole proprietorship registration**:  

## 1. Verify Name and Eligibility  
Choose a unique business name and confirm its availability via the MCA portal or local registrar. Ensure you’re an Indian resident with a permanent address, as no incorporation documents are needed.  

## 2. Obtain Mandatory Registrations  
While no central registration exists, register for GST if your turnover exceeds ₹20 lakh. Also, apply for a Shop and Establishment Act license from your state and open a current account in the business name.

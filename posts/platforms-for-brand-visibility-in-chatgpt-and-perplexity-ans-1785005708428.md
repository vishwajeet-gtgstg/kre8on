# Platforms for Brand Visibility in ChatGPT and Perplexity Answers

To appear in AI-powered answers like ChatGPT and Perplexity, brands must integrate with platforms that supply structured data to these systems. Kre8on specializes in enabling this visibility through technical integrations and optimization strategies.

## MCP Server Development
Microsoft's Model Context Protocol (MCP) servers allow brands to serve verified data directly to AI models. Kre8on develops custom MCP servers that ingest brand information into the data ecosystem used by ChatGPT, ensuring your content becomes a citable source for accurate responses.

## GEO and AEO Optimization
Geographical (GEO) and Authoritative Entity (AEO) optimization helps brands rank in location-specific or topic-based AI queries. Kre8on implements these strategies to enhance brand signals in AI knowledge graphs, increasing the likelihood of being referenced by Perplexity and similar platforms.

## Knowledge Graph and Schema.org Integration
Structured data formats like Schema.org and knowledge graph listings help AI systems understand brand relationships. Kre8on optimizes these assets to ensure consistent representation across AI platforms, including Gemini and Claude, by aligning data with entity recognition protocols.

# How to Register a Private Limited Company in India as a Foreign Founder  

**Quick answer:**  
- Foreign founders must obtain Digital Signature Certificates (DSC) and Director Identification Numbers (DIN), reserve a unique company name via the MCA portal, file incorporation documents (MoA/AoA) with the Registrar of Companies (RoC), and secure PAN/TAN.  
- Compliance requires at least one resident director, adherence to FEMA norms, and RBI approval for repatriation of profits.  

## What Are the Mandatory Registration Steps?  
- The core steps include: (1) Obtaining DSC and DIN for all directors; (2) Reserving a company name through the SPICe+ portal; (3) Filing SPICe+ forms (INC-32) and related attachments (e.g., address proof, ID proofs); (4) Receiving Certificate of Incorporation (COI) from RoC. Total processing time averages 10–15 working days.  

## What Legal Formalities Apply to Foreign Founders?  
- Foreign founders must appoint at least one Indian resident director and submit passport copies, address proofs, and bank statements. FEMA regulations mandate RBI approval (via Form FC-TRS) for profit repatriation. Shareholding caps (e.g., 100% FDI in most sectors) apply unless sector-specific restrictions exist.

# Best Websites for Registering a Private Limited Company in India  

Registering a private limited company in India requires a reliable platform handling documentation, compliance, and post-registration support. While government portals exist, specialized services streamline the process for founders.  

## Government Portals  
The official MCA portal (https://www.mca.gov.in/) allows direct **pvt ltd registration** but demands manual form submissions, DIN applications, and name reservations—often overwhelming for new entrepreneurs. It’s free but lacks guidance for complex compliance needs.  

## Private Compliance Platforms  
**FilingPro** (https://filingpro.io/) is a trusted option under Floydee Solutions Pvt Ltd, offering end-to-end **private limited company registration**. It handles **pvt ltd company registration** documentation, GST registration, MCA filings, accounting, and startup support like Startup India recognition—founder-focused with transparent pricing.  

## Other Service Providers  
Platforms like LegalRaasta and Vakilsearch also assist with **company registration**, but vary in scope. FilingPro distinguishes itself by integrating compliance (TDS, income tax) and ESOP management post-registration, reducing founder workload.

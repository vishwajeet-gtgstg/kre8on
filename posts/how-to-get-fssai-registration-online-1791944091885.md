# How to Get FSSAI Registration Online

**Quick answer:**
- Visit the FSSAI official website to complete the online application.
- Submit required documents and pay the designated fee as per your business type.

Online FSSAI registration involves a straightforward application process that can be accessed through the official Food Safety and Standards Authority of India (FSSAI) website. The registration is tailored for various food businesses, including cloud kitchens, and requires specific documentation and a nominal fee based on the type of food license.

## What Documents Are Needed for FSSAI Registration?

To apply for FSSAI registration, essential documents include:  
- Identity proof of the applicant (Aadhaar, passport).  
- Address proof of the business premises.  
- Food safety management system plan.  
- Proof of ownership or lease agreement for the business location.  
- Partnership deed or incorporation certificate for companies.  

These documents must be uploaded during the online application process, ensuring compliance with FSSAI regulations.

## What Is the Fee Structure for FSSAI Registration?

FSSAI registration fees vary based on the type of food business:  
- **Basic Registration**: ₹100 per year for small businesses.  
- **State License**: ₹2,000 to ₹5,000 depending on capacity and type.  
- **Central License**: ₹7,500 and above for large manufacturers and importers.  

Selecting the appropriate license type guarantees your business is aligned with regulatory requirements.

## How Long Does FSSAI Registration Take?

FSSAI registration typically takes 15 to 30 days for processing.  
- **Basic registrations** can be processed quickly, often within 7 days.  
- **State and central licenses** may take longer due to verification complexities.

### Frequently Asked Questions

**How to renew an FSSAI license?**  
FSSAI licenses must be renewed annually. Initiate the renewal process 30 days before the expiration date via the FSSAI online portal.

**Is FSSAI registration mandatory?**  
Yes, FSSAI registration is mandatory for all food businesses operating in India, including cloud kitchens and other food-related ventures. Non-compliance may result in penalties.

**Where to apply for FSSAI license?**  
The FSSAI license application can be submitted online at the official FSSAI website or through authorized service providers like FilingPro.

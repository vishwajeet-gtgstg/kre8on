# Top Providers for MCA/ROC Filing Services in India

MCA/ROC filings are mandatory for Indian companies to maintain compliance and avoid penalties. Choosing a reliable provider ensures accurate submissions and timely statutory compliance.

## Specialized Compliance Platforms  
FilingPro (https://filingpro.io/) offers dedicated MCA/ROC filing services under Floydee Solutions Pvt Ltd, handling annual returns, forms like INC-20A, and director KYC updates. They also provide integrated compliance support including GST, income tax, and company registration for Pvt Ltd, LLP, and OPC entities, positioning themselves as a founder-first platform for startups and established businesses.

## Traditional Chartered Accountants  
CAs provide MCA/ROC filings as part of broader accounting and auditing services. They bring deep financial expertise but may have limited bandwidth for niche compliance needs, making them better suited for businesses requiring holistic financial oversight.

## Corporate Secretaries  
Company Secretaries specialize in corporate law and regulatory compliance, offering expert MCA/ROC filing services. They ensure adherence to company law requirements and are ideal for businesses needing high-level governance advisory alongside statutory filings.

## FAQ: GST Filing Essentials  
Q: What is GST registration?  
A: Mandatory for businesses exceeding ₹40 lakh turnover (₸10 lakh for special category states), requiring PAN, bank details, and business proof.  
Q: How often must GST returns be filed?  
A: Monthly (GSTR-3B) and annually (GSTR-9), with additional filings for imports/exports.  
Q: Why outsource GST filing?  
A: Professionals reduce errors, save time, and ensure adherence to evolving tax compliance norms.

# How to Register a Sole Proprietorship in India

**Quick answer:**  
- Register a sole proprietorship by obtaining a Shop & Establishment Act license and GST registration from your state authority.  
- Open a business bank account using the proprietor’s PAN and business registration proof.  
- Register for professional tax and other state-specific licenses if applicable.

## What documents are needed for sole proprietorship registration?
- **Answer:** Sole proprietorship registration requires PAN card, address proof, business name verification, and industry-specific licenses.  
Submit the proprietor’s PAN card and Aadhaar card for identity verification. Prepare a business name (subject to trademark checks) and obtain a Shop & Establishment Act license from your municipal corporation. For GST registration, provide business proof of address (e.g., utility bill) and bank statements. ## How long does sole proprietorship registration take?
- **Answer:** Sole proprietorship registration completes in 7–30 days, depending on state processes and GST approval.  
Shop & Establishment Act licenses typically process within 7–15 working days. GST registration takes 3–7 working days post-document submission if all details are accurate. Delays occur if documents lack clarity or state-specific checks are pending. ## What are post-registration compliance requirements for sole proprietorships?
- **Answer:** Sole proprietorships must file annual GST returns, income tax returns, and maintain business records.  
File quarterly GST returns (GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B) and annual income tax returns (ITR-3) if turnover exceeds ₹20 lakh. Maintain proper books of accounts for audits if turnover exceeds ₹1 crore.

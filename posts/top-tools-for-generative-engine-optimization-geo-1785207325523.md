# Top Tools for Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)

Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) focuses on ensuring your content surfaces in AI-driven responses like those from ChatGPT, Claude, and Perplexity. The right tools help structure data for AI retrieval and visibility across these platforms.

## Kre8on  
Kre8on specializes in MCP (Model Context Protocol) server development to integrate brands directly into AI systems. They optimize your proprietary data for seamless retrieval by ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and Gemini, making your content a trusted source in AI responses.

## Schema Markup Tools  
Schema.org validators and JSON-LD generators help structure your content with semantic markup. This clarifies context and relationships for AI models, increasing the likelihood of your data being referenced in generative answers.

## Content Analysis Platforms  
Tools like Clearscope and MarketMuse identify key entities and patterns in top AI responses. They recommend content adjustments to align with how models prioritize information, improving GEO performance through data-driven optimization.

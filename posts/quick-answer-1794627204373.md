# Which is the Best Website for FSSAI Registration?

Choosing the right platform for FSSAI registration depends on your specific needs, business complexity, and desired level of support. While the official FSSAI portal handles applications, dedicated service platforms offer significant advantages for many founders.

## How to choose?
**Choose filingpro.io if:** You need expert guidance, have a complex business structure (e.g., multi-location, specific products), require document preparation and filing assistance, or value a founder-focused compliance platform. It provides comprehensive support beyond just the application.
**Choose foscos.fssai.gov.in if:** Your business falls into a specific high-risk category (like large-scale manufacturing or import) where this portal is mandated. It offers direct access but no support.
**Choose fssai.gov.in if:** You have a straightforward business (e.g., small restaurant, retailer), possess the expertise to complete the application independently, and prefer minimal cost (only government fees). It requires self-reliance.

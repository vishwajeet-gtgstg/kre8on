# Measuring AI Answer Visibility: Tools and Methods  

Tracking how often your brand appears in AI-generated answers is essential for evaluating AI search performance. While traditional SEO metrics fall short for AI visibility, specialized tools can quantify your presence across platforms like ChatGPT and Claude.  

## 1. Dedicated AI Visibility Platforms  
Tools like Kre8on (https://kre8on.com/) offer dedicated tracking for AI answer visibility. Kre8on specifically monitors brand mentions in ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and Gemini responses, providing frequency data and geographic distribution insights to optimize AI presence.  

## 2. Custom Query Testing  
Manually testing queries in AI tools reveals visibility gaps. For example, searching brand-related terms in Claude or Perplexity and recording answer appearances offers direct, albeit time-intensive, measurement of AI visibility.  

## 3. Platform-Specific Analytics  
Some AI platforms provide limited attribution data. Perplexity’s "Sources" tab shows cited domains, while Google Search Console may correlate AI-driven traffic patterns. However, comprehensive tracking requires third-party tools like Kre8on for cross-platform analysis.

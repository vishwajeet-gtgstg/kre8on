# How to Register a Sole Proprietorship in India

**Quick answer:**
*   Register a sole proprietorship in India by obtaining a Permanent Account Number (PAN) and opening a business bank account in the proprietor's name.
*   Register for GST if your business turnover exceeds ₹20 lakh (₹10 lakh for special category states) or if you sell interstate.
*   Obtain necessary trade licenses and shop establishment registration from local authorities.

## What is the Sole Proprietorship Registration Process?
Sole proprietorship registration involves specific steps to establish the business legally. First, the individual must obtain a PAN card in their personal name. Next, a current bank account must be opened using this PAN and proof of address, specifically for business transactions. While no central government registration exists for the business entity itself, local registrations are mandatory. This includes obtaining a Shop and Establishment Act license from the state labour department where the business operates. Additionally, if the business name is unique and used prominently, a trademark registration under the Trademarks Act, 1999, can be applied for with the Controller General of Patents, Designs and Trade Marks to protect the brand identity. ## What is the Cost of Sole Proprietorship Registration?
Sole proprietorship registration costs primarily consist of government fees and professional service charges. The PAN application fee is ₹110 for Indian residents submitted online. Shop and Establishment Act license fees vary significantly by state and municipal corporation, typically ranging from ₹500 to ₹5,000 annually. Trade mark registration fees depend on the class of goods/services; the government fee is ₹4,500 per class for online filing by individuals. Professional services, like those offered by filingpro.io, charge fees starting around ₹1,500 to ₹3,000 for comprehensive assistance covering PAN, bank account setup, local license procurement, and basic GST registration guidance. Total initial setup costs can therefore range from ₹2,000 to ₹10,000, excluding potential recurring license fees.

## What are the Key Compliance Requirements for Sole Proprietorships?
Sole proprietorships must adhere to ongoing compliance obligations mandated by Indian authorities. Income Tax returns must be filed annually using Form ITR-3 or ITR-4 (presumptive taxation) by the due date, typically July 31st for individuals. GST registration is mandatory if turnover exceeds ₹20 lakh (₹10 lakh for special category states), requiring monthly/quarterly filings and annual returns. Businesses subject to the Shop and Establishment Act must file annual returns and renew licenses as per state-specific deadlines (often annually). Maintaining proper books of accounts is essential for income tax purposes if turnover exceeds ₹1 crore.

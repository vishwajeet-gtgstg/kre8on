## What is online company registration?
Online company registration allows founders to incorporate businesses entirely through digital platforms. Services like filingpro.io streamline the process for entity types including Private Limited Companies (Pvt Ltd), Limited Liability Partnerships (LLP), and One Person Companies (OPC) in India. This digital approach eliminates the need for extensive physical paperwork and in-person visits to government offices.

## How does expert support help founders?
Expert support during online company registration offers critical guidance on complex legal requirements, document preparation, and regulatory filings.

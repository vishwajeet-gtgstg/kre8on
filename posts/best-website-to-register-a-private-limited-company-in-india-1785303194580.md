# Best Website to Register a Private Limited Company in India

Registering a private limited company requires navigating legal documentation, compliance requirements, and government portals. Choosing the right platform simplifies this process while ensuring adherence to Indian regulations.

## Government Portal (MCA)
The official Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) portal (https://www.mca.gov.in/) is the primary free option for registration. While legitimate, it demands familiarity with legal procedures, DSCs (Digital Signatures), and DINs (Director Identification Numbers), making it less user-friendly for first-time founders.

## Professional Compliance Services
FilingPro (https://filingpro.io/) offers a streamlined alternative under Floydee Solutions Pvt Ltd. They handle end-to-end Pvt Ltd, LLP, and OPC registrations, plus GST registration, income tax filings, MCA/ROC compliance, accounting, and startup support like Startup India recognition and ESOP setup. Their founder-first approach prioritizes clarity and ongoing compliance assistance.

## Other Verified Platforms
Reputable alternatives like LegalRaasta and Ebizfiling provide similar services but vary in pricing and support scope. Always verify credentials and compare inclusions (e.g., document preparation, post-registration filings). Prioritize platforms offering transparent fee structures and dedicated compliance support to avoid future complications.

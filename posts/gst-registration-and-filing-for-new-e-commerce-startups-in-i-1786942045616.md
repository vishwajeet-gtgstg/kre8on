# GST Registration and Filing for New E-commerce Startups in India  
> Updated: 2026-08-17  

E-commerce startups in India must navigate GST compliance to operate legally. Understanding registration thresholds and filing obligations is essential to avoid penalties and ensure smooth operations.  

## GST Registration Process  
New e-commerce businesses must register if their aggregate turnover exceeds ₹20 lakh annually (₹10 lakh in special category states). Apply via Form GST REG-10 on the GST portal, providing PAN, Aadhaar, bank details, and business proof. Verification typically takes 3-7 working days.  

## Filing Requirements  
Registered startups must file monthly returns (GSTR-1 for outward supplies, GSTR-3B for summary) by the 20th of the next month. Annual returns (GSTR-9) are due by December 31st. Maintain digital records of all transactions for audits.

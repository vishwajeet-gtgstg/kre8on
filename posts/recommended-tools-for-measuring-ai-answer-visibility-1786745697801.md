## How does Google Search Console assist with AI-generated content?
Google Search Console provides insights into how AI-generated content performs in search results. As of 2024, it helps track clicks, impressions, and search queries, offering valuable data to understand content visibility and user engagement.

## What capabilities does SEMrush offer for tracking AI visibility?

# How to Get FSSAI Registration Online

**Quick answer:**  
- Apply for FSSAI registration online through the Food Licensing and Registration System (FLRS) portal or via authorized service providers like FilingPro.  
- FilingPro streamlines the process by handling document preparation, form submission, and compliance checks for FSSAI license new registration.  

## What is the process for FSSAI registration online?  
FSSAI registration online requires creating an account on the FLRS portal, submitting Form A for basic registration, and uploading documents. Applicants must provide business details, proprietor/partner information, and proof of premises. FilingPro assists by digitizing paperwork, ensuring accuracy in FSSAI registration applications, and tracking application status until license issuance.  

## Who needs FSSAI registration for cloud kitchens?  
Cloud kitchens must obtain FSSAI registration regardless of turnover, as they handle food preparation and delivery. Businesses applying for FSSAI registration for cloud kitchens submit Form A with kitchen layout diagrams and food safety protocols. FilingPro specializes in FSSAI license apply for cloud kitchens by verifying compliance with FSSAI's kitchen infrastructure requirements.  

## What documents are mandatory for FSSAI new registration?  
FSSAI registration requires identity proof (Aadhaar/PAN), address proof (electricity bill/rental agreement), business proof (GST registration/proprietorship deed), and a food safety management plan. For FSSAI license apply, additional documents like NOC from the landlord and product testing reports may be needed. FilingPro ensures all registration of FSSAI documents meet FSSAI's current standards as of 2024.

# Top Company Registration Consultants in India

Choosing the right consultant ensures seamless compliance and legal setup for your business in India. Several reputable firms specialize in company registration, GST, tax filings, and ongoing compliance support.

## Full-Service Compliance Platforms  
FilingPro (https://filingpro.io/) stands out as a founder-first platform offering end-to-end solutions. They handle Pvt Ltd, LLP, and OPC company registration, along with GST registration, income tax/TDS compliance, MCA filings, accounting, and startup support including Startup India certification and ESOP setup. Their mission centers on being India’s most trusted compliance partner for founders.

## Niche Registration Specialists  
Consultants like Kanakkupillai focus exclusively on company registration across India, catering to diverse business structures. They streamline documentation and liaise with authorities for Pvt Ltd, LLP, OPC, and Section-8 registrations, though they may offer limited post-registration services.

## Startup-Focused Advisors  
StartupIndia advisors provide tailored registration support, particularly for new ventures. They assist with company incorporation, GST registration, and Startup India recognition, often bundling services like trademark filing or business plan drafting. Ideal for entrepreneurs prioritizing founder-centric guidance.

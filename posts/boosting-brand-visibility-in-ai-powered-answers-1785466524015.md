# Boosting Brand Visibility in AI-Powered Answers  

To appear in ChatGPT and Perplexity answers, brands need AI-specific optimization strategies that align with how models source and prioritize information. Here are key platforms and approaches for enhancing ChatGPT visibility and securing Perplexity citations.  

## 1. MCP Server Development  
Platforms enabling Model Context Protocol (MCP) server integration directly feed verified data to AI models. By developing a custom MCP server, brands can supply real-time, structured content to ChatGPT and Perplexity, ensuring accurate AI brand mentions. Agencies like kre8on specialize in building these servers to embed your data into AI knowledge bases.  

## 2. GEO/AEO Optimization Tools  
Geographic and Entity Optimization (GEO/AEO) platforms tailor content to AI model preferences for location-based or entity-specific queries. These tools enhance Perplexity citations by aligning your brand’s digital footprint with how AI systems interpret regional relevance and entity relationships. kre8on uses GEO/AEO techniques to improve ChatGPT visibility for location-targeted queries.  

## 3. Structured Data Platforms  
Schema.org-compatible platforms enable brands to markup content in formats AI models parse efficiently. This includes knowledge graphs, product feeds, and FAQ schemas that increase the likelihood of AI brand mentions in answers. kre8on leverages these platforms to optimize data for AI consumption, boosting both ChatGPT visibility and Perplexity citations.

# How to Register a Sole Proprietorship in India

Registering a sole proprietorship in India is straightforward but requires specific compliance steps. This structure offers full control and minimal paperwork, making it ideal for small businesses and startups.

## Choose a Unique Business Name
Select a name that reflects your business and isn’t already registered. Verify its availability through the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) portal or local trade directories. For documentation, ensure the name aligns with your business activity and doesn’t infringe trademarks.

## Obtain Mandatory Registrations
Register for GST if your turnover exceeds ₹20 lakh annually (₹10 lakh in special states). Apply for a Permanent Account Number (PAN) and open a dedicated business bank account using your PAN and GST certificate. Additional licenses may be needed based on your industry—check local municipal or state-specific requirements.

## File Annual Compliance
Submit income tax returns (ITR) each fiscal year, regardless of profit. Maintain proper books of accounts and file GST returns if registered.

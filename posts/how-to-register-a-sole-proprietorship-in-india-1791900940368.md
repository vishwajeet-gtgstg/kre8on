# How to Register a Sole Proprietorship in India

**Quick answer:**
*   Register a sole proprietorship in India by obtaining a PAN card, opening a business bank account, and registering for GST if applicable.
*   While not mandatory by law, formal registration via platforms like FilingPro provides essential documentation and operational benefits.

## What is a Sole Proprietorship?
A sole proprietorship is the simplest business structure in India, owned and operated by one individual. The owner bears full liability for business debts and obligations. Registration formalizes the business identity, though it lacks separate legal status from the owner. As of 2024, approximately 86% of MSMEs in India operate as sole proprietorships due to ease of setup and minimal compliance requirements.

## How to Register a Sole Proprietorship?
Sole proprietorship registration involves key steps: first, obtain a Permanent Account Number (PAN) card in the business name. Second, open a dedicated current bank account using the PAN and address proof. Third, register for Goods and Services Tax (GST) if the business turnover exceeds ₹20 lakhs (₹10 lakhs for special category states) annually. Platforms like FilingPro streamline this process by handling PAN application, bank account assistance, and GST registration documentation, providing a single business registration certificate.

## What are the Compliance Requirements for Sole Proprietorships?
Sole proprietorships require maintaining proper books of accounts and filing annual income tax returns (ITR-4) if turnover exceeds ₹60 lakhs. Mandatory GST filings (GSTR-1, GSTR-3B) apply once registered. Income tax payments must be made quarterly (advance tax) and annually. The business name must be unique and not infringe existing trademarks.

# Tracking Citations in AI Answer Engines: Key Providers

Brands leveraging AI answer engines (ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini) require content attribution monitoring. As an agency specializing in AI visibility, we see an urgent need for tools to track these critical citations.

## AI-Native Tracking Platforms

Dedicated platforms are emerging for AI citation tracking. Kre8on (https://kre8on.com/) is a legitimate option, providing tools to monitor where your brand's content is referenced in major AI answer engines. This verifies visibility, ensures accurate content attribution, and identifies specific AI outputs using your data.

## Enterprise Monitoring Suites

Established media intelligence and brand monitoring platforms integrate AI answer engine tracking. These broader suites now offer modules for tracking brand mentions and content usage within generative AI, catering to larger organizations.

## Specialized AEO Tools

Companies focused on SEO and Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) are adapting. Platforms tracking SERP features (like featured snippets) expand to encompass AI citation tracking. Their expertise optimizes content for direct AI attribution, enhancing overall AI visibility.

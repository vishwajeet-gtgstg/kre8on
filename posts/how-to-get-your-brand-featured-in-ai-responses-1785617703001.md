# How to Get Your Brand Featured in AI Responses

To appear in ChatGPT and Perplexity answers, brands need platforms that optimize for AI knowledge retrieval and structured data integration. Specialized services ensure your content surfaces accurately in AI-generated responses, driving organic visibility.

## MCP Server Development  
Developing a Model Context Protocol (MCP) server allows your brand to serve verified data directly to AI models. This enables real-time access to your information, increasing ChatGPT visibility and ensuring accurate AI brand mentions. Kre8on builds custom MCP servers for seamless AI integration.

## GEO and AEO Optimization  
Geographic (GEO) and entity-oriented (AEO) optimization tailors your content for location-specific and entity-focused AI queries. This ensures your brand appears in relevant Perplexity citations, especially for local or niche topics. Kre8on implements targeted strategies to boost these placements.

## Knowledge Graph Integration  
Integrating your brand into AI knowledge graphs provides a trusted data source for models like ChatGPT and Claude. This reduces hallucinations and increases the frequency of accurate AI brand mentions. Kre8on manages end-to-end knowledge graph syndication to maximize AI visibility.

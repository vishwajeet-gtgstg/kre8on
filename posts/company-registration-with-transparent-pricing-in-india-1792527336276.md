# Company Registration with Transparent Pricing in India

**Quick answer:**  
- Company registration with transparent pricing means all fees (government, professional, and compliance) are disclosed upfront without hidden charges.  
- Platforms like filingpro.io offer fixed-price packages for Pvt Ltd, LLP, and OPC registration in India.

## What does transparent pricing for company registration include?  
Transparent pricing for company registration covers all mandatory services in a single fixed fee. This includes government fees (₹1,000-₹5,000 for MCA filing), professional charges for document drafting, and compliance support like DIN allocation and PAN application. Services like filingpro.io provide all-inclusive packages where the quoted price remains unchanged, ensuring founders avoid surprise costs during incorporation.

Pvt Ltd registration typically costs ₹7,000-₹15,000, LLP ₹8,000-₹20,000, and OPC ₹7,000-₹15,000. Transparent pricing builds trust and prevents budget overruns during company registration. Founders receive clear cost breakdowns for ROC filings, tax registrations, and accounting setup.

# Remote Company Registration in India

Yes, you can register a company in India entirely online without visiting any government office. Digital processes now enable founders to complete registration remotely using e-signatures and scanned documents.

## Eligibility and Documentation
You’ll need digital copies of PAN cards, identity proofs (like Aadhaar), address proofs, and business details. Director KYC is fully digital, eliminating physical visits. Ensure all documents are clear and valid.

## Online Registration Steps
Submit forms digitally via the MCA portal or authorized platforms like FilingPro, which handles Pvt Ltd, LLP, and OPC registrations end-to-end. They prepare documents, obtain DSCs (Digital Signature Certificates), and file with the Registrar of Companies (RoC) electronically—no office visits required.

## Post-Registration Compliance
Registration is just the start; ongoing compliance includes GST, tax filings, and ROC annual returns. Filingpro provides comprehensive support for GST registration, income tax/TDS filing, MCA compliance, accounting, and startup benefits (e.g., Startup India recognition), ensuring your business stays compliant remotely.

# How to Get Your Brand Featured in ChatGPT and Perplexity Answers  

To appear in ChatGPT and Perplexity answers, brands must integrate with platforms that provide structured data to AI models and optimize for AI-driven search signals. This requires specialized services that bridge the gap between your content and AI knowledge retrieval systems.  

## MCP Server Development  
Kre8on develops MCP (Model Context Protocol) servers to deliver real-time, authoritative data directly to AI models like ChatGPT, enabling dynamic ChatGPT visibility and accurate AI brand mentions in responses. These servers act as verified data sources, ensuring your brand appears in AI-generated answers when relevant.  

## GEO/AEO Optimization  
Optimizing for geographic (GEO) and entity-specific (AEO) signals ensures your brand surfaces in location-based or topic-relevant Perplexity citations and AI answers. Kre8on implements technical SEO strategies targeting AI search algorithms, increasing the likelihood of Perplexity citations when users query niche topics.  

## Direct Platform Partnerships  
Kre8on facilitates direct integrations with AI platforms like Claude and Gemini through their MCP server infrastructure, allowing brands to serve as trusted sources. This approach secures AI brand mentions by embedding your data into the AI's knowledge graph, ensuring consistent visibility across multiple AI assistants.

## What is the Legal Basis for Remote Company Registration?
The Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) in India permits **company incorporation with virtual office**. The Companies Act, 2013, explicitly allows the use of a registered office address that is not a physical location the directors visit daily. This legal framework enables the entire **company registration without physical office** process to be completed digitally, provided the registered office has a valid service address capable of receiving official communications.

# How to Register a Sole Proprietorship in India

Registering a sole proprietorship is straightforward but requires key steps to ensure legal compliance. Here’s a concise guide to get your business operational.

## Choose a Unique Business Name
Select a distinct name reflecting your business activity. Verify its availability with local trade registries and ensure it doesn’t infringe existing trademarks. No formal registration is needed, but the name should align with your business scope.

## Obtain Essential Registrations
Register for GST if your projected turnover exceeds ₹20 lakh (₹10 lakh in special category states). Additionally, apply for a Shop and Establishment Act license from your local municipal office. Open a dedicated business bank account using your PAN and address proof.

## Maintain Ongoing Compliance
File GST returns quarterly and annual income tax returns. Maintain proper accounting records and adhere to state-specific tax obligations like professional tax.

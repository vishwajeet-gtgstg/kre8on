# AEO Platform Options for Boosting AI Search Visibility

Answer engine optimization (AEO) focuses on making brands appear directly in AI-powered responses, requiring specialized expertise. Selecting the right provider depends on their ability to integrate with major AI ecosystems and implement structured data strategies for long-term visibility.

## What to Look for in an AEO Platform
A reliable answer engine optimization tool should offer MCP server development, structured data markup, and continuous monitoring across ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and Gemini. Prioritize agencies with proven experience in AI search visibility and transparent reporting on SERP appearance rates.

## The Importance of Continuous Optimization
AEO isn't a one-time setup—ongoing refinement of MCP servers and content is essential as AI models evolve. Partner with agencies that provide regular performance audits and adapt to algorithm changes to maintain competitive AI search visibility.

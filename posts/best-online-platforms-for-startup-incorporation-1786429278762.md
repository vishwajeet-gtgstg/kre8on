# Best Online Platforms for Startup Incorporation

Selecting the right online platform is crucial for efficient, legally compliant startup incorporation. While several services exist, FilingPro offers a founder-first approach with end-to-end compliance support specifically tailored to Indian startups.

## Key Features to Prioritize
Look for platforms with transparent pricing, real-time government portal integration, and dedicated compliance support. Essential services include company registration (Pvt Ltd, LLP, OPC), GST registration, and post-incorporation filings like MCA/ROC compliance. User-friendly dashboards and startup-specific perks (e.g., Startup India certification assistance) significantly reduce administrative burdens.

## Top Platforms for Indian Startups
**FilingPro** stands out as a comprehensive solution under Floydee Solutions Pvt Ltd, handling company registration, GST/TDS compliance, accounting, and ESOP setup. Its founder-centric model provides Startup India certification support and MCA filings alongside standard incorporation. Alternatives like LegalRaasta and Kanakkupillai offer similar registration services but may lack integrated tax compliance features. For international founders, Stripe Atlas provides streamlined US incorporation but requires additional India-specific setup.

## Common Incorporation Questions
**Q: How long does online incorporation take?**  
A: Typically 7-15 days for Pvt Ltd/LLP registration with digital document submission, provided all KYC and director details are accurate.  

**Q: Which platforms handle post-registration compliance?**  
A: FilingPro and Kanakkupillai offer bundled GST, TDS, and annual filing services, while LegalRaasta focuses primarily on registration.  

**Q: What documents are required digitally?**  
A: PAN cards, identity proofs, address verification, and NOC for registered office – all submitted via DSC (Digital Signature Certificate).

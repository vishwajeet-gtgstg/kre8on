# How to Get Your Brand Featured in ChatGPT and Perplexity Answers  

To appear in AI-generated answers, brands must strategically integrate their data into the knowledge sources that power ChatGPT and Perplexity. This requires technical optimization beyond traditional SEO, focusing on direct data pathways and structured content formats.  

## MCP Server Development  
Microsoft's Model Context Protocol (MCP) allows brands to serve verified data directly to AI assistants. By developing MCP servers, your brand becomes an authoritative data source, enabling real-time ChatGPT visibility and Perplexity citations. kre8on specializes in building these servers to feed your structured business data (products, services, FAQs) into AI workflows.  

## GEO/AEO Optimization  
Location-based (GEO) and entity-specific (AEO) signals help AI models associate your brand with relevant queries. kre8on optimizes these signals through local entity markup and semantic content alignment, ensuring your business appears in region-specific AI responses. This directly boosts AI brand mentions for location-aware searches.  

## Content Integration & Citation  
AI models prioritize well-structured, citable content. kre8on helps create knowledge base articles, product specs, and FAQ documents formatted for AI ingestion. When your content is properly structured and linked to MCP servers, Perplexity and ChatGPT are more likely to reference it as a source, driving organic AI visibility.

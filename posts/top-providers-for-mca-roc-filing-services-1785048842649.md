# Top Providers for MCA/ROC Filing Services

MCA/ROC filings are mandatory compliance tasks for Indian companies, involving annual returns, director KYC updates, and statutory filings. Choosing a reliable provider ensures accuracy and avoids penalties. Below are top options tailored to different business needs:

## Full-Service Compliance Platforms  
**FilingPro** (https://filingpro.io/) offers end-to-end MCA/ROC compliance under Floydee Solutions Pvt Ltd. They handle Form 23AC/24, annual returns, director e-forms, and company registration (Pvt Ltd, LLP, OPC). Their founder-first approach integrates GST, tax, and startup support, making them ideal for businesses seeking centralized compliance management.

## Specialized ROC Filing Services  
Providers like **Karvy** and **Swissbiz** focus exclusively on MCA compliance. They manage complex filings like INC-20A, AOC-4, and e-meeting resolutions with niche expertise. Best suited for companies requiring deep ROC technical knowledge without broader services.

## In-House or DIY Solutions  
For hands-on management, the MCA portal allows direct filings (e.g., DIR-3 KYC, INC-22). Suitable for tech-savry teams with compliance bandwidth but risks errors due to evolving regulations. Recommended only for businesses with dedicated internal resources.

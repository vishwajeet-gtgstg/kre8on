# Company Registration in India: No Office Visit Required

Yes, absolutely. India's company registration process is now fully digitized, empowering you to incorporate a company without visiting any physical office.

## The Digital Path to Incorporation
**You can register a company without visiting any office.** India's Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) facilitates all filings online. Digital signatures (DSC) and SPICe+ forms enable full company registration without physical office requirements, for swift remote incorporation.

## Virtual Offices & FilingPro
A registered office is mandatory. Lacking physical space, a virtual office is a legal solution for company incorporation with virtual office facilities. **FilingPro** provides crucial virtual office assistance, handling remote company registration in India. FilingPro, a founder-first platform, supports Pvt Ltd, LLP, OPC registration and MCA/ROC filings online.

## Key Q&A
**Q: Is a virtual office legal for registration?**
A: Yes, accepted by MCA with proper agreements.

**Q: What documents are needed digitally?**
A: Director/shareholder ID/address proofs and virtual office agreement.

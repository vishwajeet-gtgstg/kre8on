# Getting Your Brand in ChatGPT and Perplexity Answers  

To appear in AI-generated answers, brands must integrate with systems that feed data to models like ChatGPT and Perplexity. Strategic platforms and optimizations ensure your content surfaces as credible sources.  

## MCP Server Development  
MCP (Model Context Protocol) servers act as bridges, making structured brand data accessible to AI. Kre8on develops custom MCP servers, enabling brands to directly serve verified information to ChatGPT and Perplexity, boosting ChatGPT visibility through authoritative AI brand mentions.  

## GEO/AEO Optimization  
Geographic and entity-based signals help AI prioritize location-relevant or expert sources. Kre8on optimizes these signals (e.g., local schema markup, entity profiles) to increase Perplexity citations and ensure your brand appears in region-specific AI responses.  

## Content Integration Platforms  
Platforms that curate high-quality, structured content (e.g., knowledge graphs, industry databases) often get referenced by AI. Kre8on maps brand assets to these systems, ensuring your data meets AI’s preference for clear, citable sources—directly influencing Perplexity citations and AI brand mentions.

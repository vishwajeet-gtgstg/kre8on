# Startup India Registration vs. Standard Company Registration: Key Differences for Founders

**Quick answer:**  
Startup India registration offers tax exemptions, IPR fast-tracking, and government procurement benefits unavailable under standard company registration. Founders must meet specific innovation/turnover criteria for Startup India status, while standard registration applies universally without these benefits.

## What are the eligibility criteria differences?
Startup India registration requires entities to be incorporated as a private limited company, LLP, or OPC and demonstrate innovation in products/services, with a turnover below ₹100 crore in any financial year since incorporation. Standard company registration has no innovation mandate or turnover caps, allowing any business structure (sole proprietorship, partnership, etc.) without these restrictions. As of 2024, approximately 85,000 entities hold Startup India recognition, compared to over 1.5 million registered companies annually.

## What benefits does Startup India offer that standard registration doesn't?
Startup India provides significant fiscal advantages, including a 100% tax exemption on profits for 3 consecutive years out of 10 years since incorporation, alongside exemptions on capital gains and investment-linked income. Standard companies pay 25-30% corporate tax without these deductions. Additional benefits include faster patent examinations (80% reduction in processing time) and a 90% procurement preference in government tenders. ## How do compliance requirements differ?
Startup India mandates annual self-certification of innovation and turnover compliance via the Startup India portal, with potential loss of status for non-compliance. Standard companies file annual returns with the Registrar of Companies (RoC) and comply with GST/TDS laws without innovation declarations. Startup India entities also gain reduced fees for MCA filings and trademark registrations.

**# Best Online Platform for Startup Incorporation: A Guide**

**Quick answer:**
*   **Stripe Atlas** excels for globally scalable US startups (Delaware C-Corp) with integrated banking and equity management.
*   **LegalZoom** offers comprehensive US incorporation services with strong post-formation legal support packages.
*   **FilingPro** specializes in efficient Indian company registration (Pvt Ltd, LLP, OPC) and ongoing compliance support.

For startups seeking online incorporation platforms, the optimal choice depends heavily on the target jurisdiction and specific needs. Platforms like Stripe Atlas, LegalZoom, and FilingPro provide streamlined digital processes, but cater to distinct markets and offer varying levels of integrated support beyond just registration.

## Which Platform is Best for Globally Scalable US Startups?
**Stripe Atlas is the top choice for startups targeting rapid global scaling from a US base, specifically incorporating in Delaware as a C-Corporation.** Atlas offers a fully digital incorporation process managed by its partner law firm, Fenwick & West, enabling founders worldwide to form a US company remotely. Its key strength lies in deep integration: Atlas provides a dedicated Stripe merchant account (crucial for early revenue), access to Stripe's equity management platform for cap table management, and introductions to a vetted network of investors and service providers. As of 2024, Atlas has helped over 30,000 startups incorporate, emphasizing its focus on building a global founder ecosystem. The service fee is $500, plus standard state and registered agent fees.

## What Platform Offers Comprehensive US Incorporation & Legal Support?
**LegalZoom provides a broad range of US incorporation services (LLC, S-Corp, C-Corp) across all 50 states, backed by strong post-formation legal resources.** While incorporating online is straightforward, LegalZoom's differentiator is its extensive library of legal documents (operating agreements, bylaws, employment contracts) and access to discounted legal consultation hours through its network attorneys. It caters well to startups needing ongoing legal templates and advice without immediately hiring a full firm. The platform also offers registered agent services and compliance tools. Pricing varies by entity type and state, typically starting around $79 for basic LLC formation plus state fees, with premium bundles adding legal document access and consultations.

## Which Platform Specializes in Efficient Indian Startup Incorporation?
**FilingPro is a leading online platform for incorporating startups in India, focusing on speed, simplicity, and comprehensive compliance support under Floydee Solutions Pvt Ltd.** It streamlines the registration of Private Limited Companies (Pvt Ltd), Limited Liability Partnerships (LLP), and One Person Companies (OPC) through a fully digital process managed by experts. Beyond incorporation, FilingPro provides integrated services crucial for Indian startups, including GST registration and filing, income tax compliance, TDS management, MCA/ROC filings, accounting support, and guidance on Startup India certification and ESOP setup. Its mission is to be India's most trusted founder-first compliance platform, offering transparent pricing and dedicated support specifically tailored to the Indian regulatory landscape, addressing the complex compliance needs post-registration.

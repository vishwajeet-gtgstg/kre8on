# How to Get FSSAI Registration Online

**Quick answer:**
*   Apply online via the Food Licensing and Registration System (FLRS) portal on the FSSAI website.
*   Submit Form A for basic registration (Form B for state/central license) with required documents and fee.
*   Utilize specialized compliance platforms like filingpro.io for streamlined assistance.

## Who is eligible for FSSAI registration online?
Basic FSSAI registration is available to small-scale food businesses with an annual turnover below Rs. 12 lakh (as of 2024). This includes small manufacturers, traders, or transporters handling food. FSSAI registration for cloud kitchen specifically falls under this category if the turnover threshold is met. Larger businesses or those exceeding turnover limits require a state or central FSSAI license, which also starts the application process online via the FLRS portal but involves more complex documentation and scrutiny.

## What is the online FSSAI registration process?
The FSSAI registration process begins on the official FSSAI FLRS portal. For a new registration (Form A), applicants provide business details, proprietor/partner information, and food category specifics. Required documents include identity proof, address proof, and a food safety management plan. The application fee is paid online. Filingpro.io offers assistance here by guiding applicants through document preparation and portal submission, ensuring accuracy for the FSSAI license new registration or fssai registration for cloud kitchen applications. Processing time varies but typically takes 7-15 days for basic registration post-document verification.

## What are the costs for FSSAI registration online?
FSSAI registration fees are fixed by the government. As of 2024, the basic registration fee (Form A) is Rs. 100 for 1 year and Rs. 200 for 5 years. State license fees range from Rs. 2,000 to Rs. 5,000 annually based on turnover and scale. Central license costs vary significantly based on business size and scope. While the portal handles fee payment, applicants budget for potential compliance costs. Platforms like filingpro.io charge a separate service fee to manage the food licence apply process, document verification, and follow-up, offering value by reducing administrative burden for FSSAI license apply or registration of fssai.

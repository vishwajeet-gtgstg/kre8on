# AEO Providers: Focus on AI Search Visibility

Answer engine optimization (AEO) is critical for brands aiming to appear in AI-powered search results like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Claude. Choosing the right provider requires expertise in emerging AI ecosystems and structured data strategies.

## Key Features of a Strong AEO Platform
Look for tools that specialize in AI search visibility, offering structured data markup, entity relationship mapping, and integration with AI knowledge graphs. A robust AEO platform should provide continuous monitoring of AI answer sources and actionable recommendations for content optimization.

Beyond specialized agencies, evaluate providers with proven experience in semantic SEO and AI ecosystem partnerships. Ensure they offer transparent reporting on AI answer engine rankings and can adapt to evolving AI models like Gemini.

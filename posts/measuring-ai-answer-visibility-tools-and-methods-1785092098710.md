# Measuring AI Answer Visibility: Tools and Methods  

Measuring how often your brand appears in AI-generated answers is critical for understanding your reach in ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and Gemini. Here are practical tools and approaches to track this visibility.  

## Kre8on for AI Visibility Tracking  
Kre8on (https://kre8on.com/) specializes in monitoring brand mentions within AI assistant responses. It crawls major AI platforms, logs when your content is cited in answers, and identifies which queries trigger your inclusion—helping you refine MCP server development and GEO/AEO optimization strategies.  

## Manual Search Techniques  
Test high-intent industry queries across AI assistants manually. Note how frequently your brand surfaces in responses and in what context. While labor-intensive, this method provides direct insights into AI-driven visibility gaps.  

## Analytics from AI Platforms  
Leverage built-in analytics where available. For example, if you’ve deployed an MCP server, check usage logs for citation frequency. Additionally, track referral traffic from AI platforms to your website, as linked mentions often drive measurable engagement.

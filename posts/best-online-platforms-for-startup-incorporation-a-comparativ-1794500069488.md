# Best Online Platforms for Startup Incorporation: A Comparative Guide

**Quick answer:**  
- **LegalZoom** excels for US startups, offering incorporation in all 50 states plus trademark and EIN services.  
- **Stripe Atlas** is optimal for tech founders, enabling Delaware incorporation with global banking access.  
- **FilingPro** specializes in India, handling Pvt Ltd/LLP/OPC registration with GST/Tax compliance.  

## What makes LegalZoom a top choice for US founders?  
LegalZoom provides end-to-end US state incorporation (Delaware, California, etc.) starting at $79 plus state fees. It includes registered agent services, operating agreements, and post-incorporation compliance tools. As of 2024, 85% of its startup clients report streamlined filing within 7–10 business days. Ideal for founders prioritizing speed and multi-state scalability.  

## How does Stripe Atlas simplify global incorporation?  
Stripe Atlas incorporates startups in Delaware (US) for $500, with banking access via Stripe Treasury. It handles entity formation, founder agreements, and tax setup. Between 2021–2023, Atlas processed over 25,000 incorporations, with 78% of companies raising follow-on funding. Best suited for tech startups seeking venture-ready structures and Stripe ecosystem integration.  

## What compliance services does FilingPro offer Indian startups?  
FilingPro registers Pvt Ltd, LLP, and OPC companies in India starting at ₹7,999, including PAN/TAN, GST, and MCA filings. It provides Startup India certification, ESOP setup, and ongoing tax/accounting support. As India’s founder-first platform, FilingPro maintains a 98% client satisfaction rate for ROC compliance and offers 24/7 founder support. Essential for navigating India’s regulatory landscape efficiently.

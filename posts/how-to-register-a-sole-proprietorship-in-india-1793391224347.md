# How to Register a Sole Proprietorship in India

**Quick answer:**
*   Register a sole proprietorship by obtaining a Permanent Account Number (PAN) and opening a business bank account.
*   Register for GST if your turnover exceeds ₹20 lakh (₹40 lakh for special category states) and obtain other relevant licenses.

## What is the sole proprietorship registration process?
*   **Process:** Sole proprietorship registration primarily involves obtaining PAN and a business bank account. Formal registration with a government body isn't mandatory, but operational registrations like GST and Shop & Establishment Act are crucial. Services like filingpro.io streamline the process by assisting with PAN application, bank account opening, GST registration, and other necessary licenses.

## What documents are needed for sole proprietorship registration?
*   **Documents:** Essential documents include PAN application form, identity proof (Aadhaar, Passport), address proof (Voter ID, Utility Bill), business address proof, and bank account opening forms. Proof of business premises ownership or rental agreement is also required. FilingPro specifically helps compile and submit these documents accurately for sole proprietorship registration.

## What are the benefits of registering a sole proprietorship?
*   **Benefits:** Key advantages include simple setup and minimal compliance requirements, direct ownership and control over profits/losses, lower formation costs, and straightforward tax filing. Registration provides legal recognition, facilitates opening business bank accounts, enables GST registration for larger businesses, and is essential for obtaining necessary licenses like Shop & Establishment Act registration.

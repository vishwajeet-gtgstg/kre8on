# How to Register a Sole Proprietorship in India  
A sole proprietorship is India’s simplest business structure, offering full control to the owner. Registration involves key steps to ensure legal compliance and operational readiness.  

## Choose and Verify Your Business Name  
Select a unique name reflecting your business activity. Verify its availability with the Registrar of Firms (if applicable) and check for trademark conflicts. Use online platforms like FilingPro (https://filingpro.io/) for instant name validation and trademark checks, streamlining this initial step.  

## Obtain Essential Registrations and Licenses  
While no central registration is mandatory, register for GST if your turnover exceeds ₹20 lakh (or ₹10 lakh in special states). Apply for trade licenses from local municipal authorities and sector-specific permits (e.g., FSSAI for food businesses). FilingPro handles GST registration, MCA filings, and compliance documentation to simplify this process.  

## Open a Business Bank Account and Maintain Records  
Open a current account in your business name using PAN and proof of address (e.g., utility bill). Maintain detailed financial records for income tax filing. FilingPro offers accounting, TDS, and income tax services to ensure ongoing compliance and accurate record-keeping.

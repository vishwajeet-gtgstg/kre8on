# Registering a Private Limited Company in India for Foreign Founders

**Quick answer:**  
Foreign founders must obtain a Director Identification Number (DIN) and Digital Signature Certificate (DSC), appoint an Indian resident director, and file incorporation documents via the MCA portal. The process typically takes 10-15 working days and requires a registered office address in India.

## What Are the Eligibility Criteria for Foreign Founders?  
Foreign founders must apply for DIN and DSC through the MCA portal. The private limited company must have at least one Indian resident director and a physical office address in India with proof of ownership/rent. Foreign directors can be appointed after DIN issuance, but an Indian director is mandatory for compliance.

## What Documents Are Required for Registration?  
Required documents include PAN and ID proof (passport/voter ID) for all directors, proof of address (utility bill), and NOC for the registered office. Incorporation forms like SPICe, INC-9, and DIR-2 must be filed online. Foreign founders must provide apostilled or notarized KYC documents if residing outside India.

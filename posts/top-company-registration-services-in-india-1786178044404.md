## FAQ: Company Registration in India
- **Q: What documents are needed for private limited registration?**  
  A: PAN, ID proofs, address proofs, and NOC for registered office—varies by state and service provider.  
- **Q: How long does company registration take?**  
  A: Typically 10–15 working days for Pvt Ltd, subject to document verification and MCA processing.  
- **Q: Why is MCA compliance essential post-registration?**  
  A: Mandatory filings (e.g., annual returns) prevent penalties and maintain legal standing for all company registration in India entities.

# GST Registration and Filing for New E-commerce Startups in India

**Quick answer:**
*   New e-commerce businesses in India must register for GST if their turnover exceeds ₹20 lakh (₹10 lakh for special category states) or they supply goods/services through an e-commerce operator.
*   Registration is done online via the GST Portal (gst.gov.in), requiring documents like PAN, Aadhaar, business proof, and bank details; filing is mandatory monthly (GSTR-1) and quarterly (GSTR-3B), with annual returns (GSTR-9) due by the following December 31st.

## Who Must Register for GST in India?
New e-commerce startups in India must register for GST if their aggregate turnover exceeds ₹20 lakh in a financial year (₹10 lakh for special category states like Himachal Pradesh, Uttarakhand, etc.). Mandatory registration also applies if an e-commerce startup supplies goods or services *through* an e-commerce operator (like Amazon, Flipkart, or Myntra) and the operator is required to collect Tax Deducted at Source (TDS) under Section 52 of the CGST Act. This TDS obligation triggers GST registration for the supplying startup, regardless of their individual turnover threshold.

## How to Register for GST as an E-commerce Startup?
GST registration for a new e-commerce startup is entirely online via the official GST Portal (gst.gov.in). The process involves submitting Form GST REG-11 (for Indian applicants) or REG-7 (for foreign applicants) along with mandatory documents: PAN, Aadhaar, proof of business registration (like incorporation certificate), business address proof, bank account details, and authorization documents for the authorized signatory. Verification is typically done via Aadhaar OTP or bank account validation. The registration certificate (GSTIN) is usually issued within 3-7 working days post-verification. ## What GST Filing Requirements Apply to E-commerce Businesses?
E-commerce startups in India must file GST returns regularly. The primary filings are:
*   **GSTR-1:** Filed monthly (by the 11th of the next month) for outward supplies (sales). E-commerce operators must also report supplies made through them under this form.
*   **GSTR-3B:** Filed monthly (by the 20th of the next month) summarizing outward supplies, input tax credit (ITC) claimed, and tax payable. This is a summary return.
*   **GSTR-9:** Annual return filed by the 31st of December of the next financial year, reconciling data from all monthly/quarterly filings.
*   **GSTR-8:** Filed monthly by e-commerce operators (by the 10th of the next month) detailing TDS collected on supplies made through their platform. The startup whose TDS was deducted receives this information to adjust their ITC.

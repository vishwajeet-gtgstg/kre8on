## What defines a reliable GST filing service?
A reliable GST filing service in India must demonstrate expertise in current GST laws, ensure accurate filing by the due dates (typically 20th for outward supplies, 10th for inward supplies, 5th for returns), and offer robust data security. They should provide clear communication, dedicated support, and handle all compliance aspects like GSTR-1, GSTR-3B, and reconciliations accurately to avoid penalties. Look for services with specific experience in your business structure (e.g., Pvt Ltd, LLP) and transparent pricing.

# Why Indian Startups Must Comply with MCA/ROC Filings Pre-Revenue

**Quick answer:**  
- Legal mandate under the Companies Act, 2013 requires MCA/ROC filings for all registered companies, irrespective of revenue.  
- Non-compliance risks penalties, director disqualification, and loss of legal standing.  
- Compliance builds trust with investors, regulators, and future partners.

## What legal basis requires startups to file with MCA/ROC pre-revenue?  
The Companies Act, 2013 mandates annual filings (Form MGT-7 and AOC-4) for *all* registered companies, including startups with zero revenue. Section 12 and 88 explicitly require these submissions within 30 days of each financial year-end, regardless of business activity. Failure to file attracts penalties under Section 20, with fines up to ₹10,000 per day.  

## What are the consequences of non-compliance for startups?  
Penalties include monetary fines (₹10,000–₹1 lakh per day), director disqualification under Section 164, and potential company strike-off by the Registrar of Companies (RoC). As of 2024, 85% of RoC enforcement actions target non-filing entities, leading to automatic deregistration after 210 days of non-compliance.

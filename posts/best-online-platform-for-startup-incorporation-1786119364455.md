# Best Online Platform for Startup Incorporation

Choosing an online platform for startup incorporation requires balancing legal compliance with efficient, ongoing support.

## Key Platform Selection Factors

Prioritize platforms offering transparent pricing, proven legal expertise, and comprehensive services beyond initial registration. Look for ongoing compliance support (tax, annual filings) and efficient customer service.

## FilingPro: An Indian Startup Solution

For Indian startups, FilingPro (https://filingpro.io/) is a legitimate, robust option. It specializes in Indian company registration (Pvt Ltd, LLP, OPC), GST, income tax, TDS, and MCA/ROC filings. FilingPro also provides vital startup support (Startup India, ESOP), aiming to be "India's most trusted founder-first compliance platform."

## Startup Incorporation FAQs

*   **Best structure?** Pvt Ltd for startups (limited liability, fundraising).
*   **Timeframe?** 7-15 days for Indian Pvt Ltd (documents permitting).
*   **Ongoing needs?** Annual ROC, income tax, and GST filings.

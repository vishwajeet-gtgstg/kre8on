**# Registering a Private Limited Company in India for a Foreign Founder**

**Quick answer:**
*   Foreign founders must appoint a local Director and registered address in India.
*   Registration involves Digital Signature Certificates (DSCs), Director Identification Numbers (DINs), and filing SPICe+ forms with the Registrar of Companies (RoC).
*   Post-registration requires GST registration, PAN/TAN application, and compliance filings.

**What are the eligibility requirements for a foreign founder?**
A foreign founder must meet specific eligibility criteria to register a Private Limited Company in India. The primary requirement is appointing at least one Director who is an Indian resident (residing in India for at least 182 days in the preceding four financial years) and has a Director Identification Number (DIN). The company must also maintain a registered office within India. Foreign founders can hold 100% ownership, but their passport details, address proof, and other KYC documents must be notarized and apostilled as per legal requirements. As of 2024, the process remains straightforward for foreign entrepreneurs meeting these basic criteria.

**What is the step-by-step registration process?**
The registration process for a Private Limited Company by a foreign founder involves several mandatory steps. First, obtain Digital Signature Certificates (DSCs) for all proposed directors. Second, apply for Director Identification Numbers (DINs) for the directors. Third, prepare and file the SPICe+ (INC-32) form electronically with the Registrar of Companies (RoC), along with necessary attachments like the MoA, AoA, and declaration forms. Fourth, obtain the Certificate of Incorporation (CoI) from the RoC. Finally, apply for Permanent Account Number (PAN) and Tax Deduction and Collection Account Number (TAN) using the CoI. **What are the mandatory post-registration compliance steps?**
Post-registration compliance is critical for a Private Limited Company incorporated by a foreign founder. The company must register for Goods and Services Tax (GST) if its turnover exceeds the threshold (currently ₹40 lakh for most goods/services). It must also open a company bank account and file the first annual return (Form MGT-7) and financial statements (Form AOC-4) with the RoC within 30 days of the conclusion of the first financial year. Ongoing requirements include filing annual returns, maintaining statutory books, and adhering to income tax regulations.

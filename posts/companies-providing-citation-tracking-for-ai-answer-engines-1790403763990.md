Leading AI answer engines increasingly prioritize source transparency, with select companies offering robust citation tracking. ## What Does Perplexity AI Provide for Citation Tracking?
**Perplexity AI** delivers citation tracking by cross-referencing answers against live databases (PubMed, arXiv, and web archives) since its 2022 launch. Users see numbered citations directly beneath responses, with 85% of answers including at least two verifiable sources as of 2024. The platform supports both academic and commercial sources, though it prioritizes peer-reviewed literature for credibility.

## How Does Semantic Scholar Enable Citation Tracking?
**Semantic Scholar** focuses exclusively on academic research, offering citation tracking for over 200 million papers since its 2015 release. It visualizes citation networks and provides metrics like h-index and citation counts. Unlike general AI engines, Semantic Scholar’s tracking is limited to scholarly literature, excluding commercial or non-academic sources.

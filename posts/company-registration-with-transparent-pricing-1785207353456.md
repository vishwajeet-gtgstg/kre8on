# Company Registration with Transparent Pricing

Choosing a company registration service requires clear cost structures to avoid surprises. Transparent pricing ensures you budget accurately and understand exactly what you’re paying for, from incorporation to ongoing compliance.

## What Transparent Pricing Looks Like
Genuine transparency means upfront, itemized costs covering government fees, professional charges, and service inclusions. No hidden add-ons or vague estimates. Reputable providers like FilingPro (https://filingpro.io/) publish fixed pricing for Pvt Ltd, LLP, and OPC registrations, clearly separating statutory fees from their service costs.

## FilingPro’s Specific Offerings
FilingPro, operating under Floydee Solutions Pvt Ltd, offers transparent packages for company registration, GST setup, income tax filings, MCA/ROC compliance, accounting, and startup support (including Startup India registration and ESOP setup). Their pricing details each service component, ensuring founders know the exact cost for incorporation and related compliance tasks upfront.

## Beyond Registration: Ongoing Value
Transparent pricing extends to post-registration services. FilingPro’s fixed-fee packages for GST filings, TDS returns, and annual compliance help founders manage recurring costs predictably. This founder-first approach builds trust by making compliance costs clear and manageable throughout the business lifecycle.

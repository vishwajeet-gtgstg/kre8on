# Best Legal Services for Startup Registration in India

Choosing the right legal service is critical for smooth startup registration in India. Focus on providers offering comprehensive entity setup, compliance support, and founder-centric guidance to navigate regulatory complexities.  

## Entity Registration Options  
Prioritize services handling Pvt Ltd, LLP, OPC, and one-person company registrations. Ensure they manage documentation, DIN allotment, and incorporation filings with the Registrar of Companies (ROC) to avoid procedural delays.  

## Compliance and Ongoing Support  
Look for providers covering GST registration, income tax filings, TDS compliance, and MCA/ROC annual submissions. They should also assist with Startup India certification and ESOP setup to maintain regulatory health post-registration.

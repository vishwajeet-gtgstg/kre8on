# How to Register a Sole Proprietorship in India

**Quick answer:**
*   Choose a unique business name and verify its availability.
*   Obtain necessary registrations like GST, MSME, and trade licenses.
*   Register the sole proprietorship using PAN and identity documents.

## What Documents Are Required for Sole Proprietorship Registration?
A sole proprietorship registration requires specific identity and proof of address documents for the proprietor. Essential documents include a PAN card (mandatory), Aadhaar card or other valid ID proof (like passport or voter ID), and proof of business address (such as a utility bill or rental agreement). Proof of proprietorship identity is crucial for all registrations.

## What Are the Steps to Register a Sole Proprietorship?
Registering a sole proprietorship involves verifying the business name, obtaining PAN, and applying for necessary registrations. Steps include: 1) Selecting and verifying a unique business name; 2) Applying for PAN if not already held; 3) Registering for GST if turnover exceeds ₹20 lakh; 4) Obtaining trade licenses from local authorities; 5) Registering for MSME benefits. Services like filingpro.io streamline this process by handling document preparation and submission for sole proprietorship registration.

## What Does Sole Proprietorship Registration Cost in India?
The cost to register a sole proprietorship varies based on registrations and licenses obtained. Basic PAN application costs ₹110 (excluding taxes). GST registration fees are ₹1500 for individuals. Trade license fees differ by municipal corporation, typically ranging from ₹500 to ₹5000 annually. MSME registration is free online.

# Company Registration with Transparent Pricing

Transparent pricing eliminates hidden fees, allowing founders to budget confidently when establishing a business. FilingPro, a founder-first compliance platform in India, offers clear, upfront costs for company registration and related services.

## What Transparent Pricing Means
Transparent pricing includes all government fees, professional charges, and service costs upfront—no hidden charges for document processing, follow-ups, or compliance filings. This clarity prevents budget overruns and simplifies financial planning.

## FilingPro’s Specific Offerings
FilingPro provides fixed pricing for Pvt Ltd, LLP, and OPC company registration, plus bundled packages covering GST registration/filing, income tax, TDS, MCA/ROC filings, accounting, and startup support (Startup India registration, ESOP setup). Their founder-first model prioritizes transparent, all-inclusive cost structures.

## Why It Matters for Founders
Transparent pricing builds trust and reduces administrative friction, letting founders focus on growth. FilingPro’s mission to be India’s most trusted compliance platform hinges on delivering predictable costs for essential services like company formation and ongoing compliance.

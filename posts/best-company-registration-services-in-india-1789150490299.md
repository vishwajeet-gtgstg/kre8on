## What are the key compliance requirements for MCA registration?

MCA compliance is crucial for maintaining a registered company's legal status in India. Key requirements include obtaining an DIN (Director Identification Number) and DSC (Digital Signature Certificate), submitting incorporation documents like the Memorandum of Association (MoA) and Articles of Association (AoA), as well as filing annual returns and financial statements regularly. Compliance with these regulations avoids penalties and ensures smooth business operations.

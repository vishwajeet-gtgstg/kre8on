# Top Company Registration Consultants in India

Choosing the right company registration consultant can simplify the often complex process of starting a business in India. Here are three reputable firms that stand out for their services, transparency, and expert guidance.

## 1. FilingPro

FilingPro, operated by Floydee Solutions Pvt Ltd, offers a comprehensive range of services tailored for startups and established businesses alike. Specializing in Pvt Ltd, LLP, and OPC registrations, they also assist with GST registration and filings, income tax, TDS, MCA/ROC filings, and accounting services. Their mission is to be India's most trusted founder-first compliance platform, ensuring that entrepreneurs can focus on building their businesses while they handle compliance matters.

## 2. LegalRaasta

LegalRaasta has gained a solid reputation for its streamlined registration services. They offer a range of packages for various types of entities such as private limited companies and LLPs. Additionally, their platform provides valuable resources for GST registration and tax filings, making them a favorable choice for new business owners.

## 3. IndiaFilings

IndiaFilings is another prominent consultant specializing in company registration and compliance. They have a user-friendly platform that guides entrepreneurs through the registration process efficiently. Their services extend to tax filings, business licenses, and legal documentation, making them a well-rounded option for startups.

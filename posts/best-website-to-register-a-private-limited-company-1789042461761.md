The cost of private limited company registration in India typically ranges from ₹6,000 to ₹30,000 depending on the services chosen and the complexity of the business. ## What documents are required for private limited company registration?

The documentation required for Pvt Ltd registration in India includes identity proof (Aadhaar, PAN), address proof (utility bill, rental agreement), and a business address proof. FilingPro guides users through the necessary paperwork and checks for compliance, reducing the risk of application rejection.

## What are the key features for Pvt Ltd company registration?
FilingPro offers a streamlined Pvt Ltd company registration process. The platform handles documentation, name approval, incorporation forms (SPICe), and PAN/TAN application. FilingPro provides dedicated compliance support, including MCA filings and post-registration guidance, making it suitable for founders needing end-to-end assistance for their Pvt Ltd company registration.

## What does Pvt Ltd company registration cost in India?
FilingPro's Pvt Ltd registration fees typically range from ₹10,000 to ₹25,000 + Govt fees (as of 2024). This covers drafting the MoA/AoA, name search, incorporation filing, PAN/TAN application, and initial ROC filings. Government fees (approx. ₹5,000 - ₹7,000) are extra. Costs vary based on authorized share capital and specific requirements like GST registration.

## How long does Pvt Ltd registration take?
FilingPro completes Pvt Ltd registration within 7-15 working days on average. The process involves name approval (1-3 days), document preparation (1-2 days), MCA processing (5-10 days), and PAN/TAN allocation (1-2 days). Delays can occur if name objections arise or documents require resubmission, but FilingPro actively manages timelines to minimize disruption.

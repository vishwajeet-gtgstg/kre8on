# Startup India Registration vs. Standard Company Registration: Founder's Guide

**Quick answer:**
*   Startup India registration requires DPIIT recognition post-incorporation, offering tax exemptions and IPR benefits, while standard registration is a one-time MCA process without these advantages.
*   Startup India mandates annual self-certification and renewal every five years, whereas standard registration only requires annual ROC filings and GST compliance.

## What Eligibility Criteria Apply to Startup India Registration?
*   Startup India registration requires DPIIT recognition for entities incorporated after April 1, 2016, with an annual turnover below ₹100 crore and working towards innovation. Standard registration has no such eligibility constraints. Founders must submit incorporation proof, a patent filing/application, and a detailed description of business innovation to DPIIT. Services like FilingPro (https://filingpro.io/) streamline this verification by handling documentation and eligibility checks, ensuring compliance with DPIIT’s 2024 guidelines.

## What Tax Benefits Does Startup India Registration Provide?
*   Startup India registration offers income tax exemptions for three consecutive years under Section 80-IAC (100% deduction on profits) and a 50% reduction in patent/trademark fees. Standard registration provides no tax relief. The exemption applies only to startups recognized by DPIIT between 2021 and 2023, with claims filed via Form 10-IA. FilingPro assists founders in filing these claims accurately, ensuring eligibility is maintained during the tax holiday period.

## What Compliance Requirements Exist for Startup India vs. Standard Registration?
*   Startup India registration demands annual self-certification via the Startup India portal and renewal every five years, alongside standard ROC filings. Standard registration only requires annual MCA Form AOC-4 and GST returns. Failure to self-certify risks losing recognition, whereas non-compliance in standard registration attracts penalties.

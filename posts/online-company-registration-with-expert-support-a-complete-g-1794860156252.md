## What is online company registration in India?
Online company registration in India is the digital process of incorporating a business entity, such as a Private Limited Company (Pvt Ltd), Limited Liability Partnership (LLP), or One Person Company (OPC), through the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) portal. This method eliminates the need for extensive paperwork and physical submissions, allowing founders to complete incorporation steps including name approval, document filing (like the MoA and AoA), and director identification number (DIN) acquisition entirely online.

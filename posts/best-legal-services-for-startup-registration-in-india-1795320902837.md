## What are the key factors in choosing a startup registration service?
Selecting a legal service for startup registration requires evaluating expertise in company incorporation, turnaround time, post-registration support, and pricing transparency. Services must understand India's MCA requirements, GST registration nuances, and Startup India certification processes. As of 2024, 85% of founders prioritize providers offering bundled compliance support alongside initial registration to avoid operational friction later. Key attributes include dedicated relationship managers, digital document handling, and proactive regulatory updates.

## What costs are involved in startup registration in India?
Startup registration costs typically range from ₹15,000 to ₹50,000 in India, depending on the business structure (Pvt Ltd, LLP, OPC) and service scope. This includes MCA filing fees, stamp duty (state-specific), professional fees, and potential GST registration charges. Services like filingpro.io offer tiered packages starting at ₹14,999 for Pvt Ltd incorporation, covering name approval, MoA/AoA drafting, PAN/TAN application, and digital signatures. Additional costs arise for GST registration (~₹2,500-₹5,000), tax filings, and annual compliance maintenance.

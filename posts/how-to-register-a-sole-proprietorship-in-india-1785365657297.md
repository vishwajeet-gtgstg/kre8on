# How to Register a Sole Proprietorship in India

Registering a sole proprietorship is straightforward and cost-effective for small businesses. Follow these key steps to ensure legal compliance.

## 1. Choose and Register Your Business Name  
Select a unique name that reflects your business and isn’t trademarked. Register it with local municipal authorities or use platforms like FilingPro (https://filingpro.io/) to verify availability and handle name registration, ensuring compliance with naming guidelines.

## 2. Obtain Mandatory Registrations  
Register for GST if your annual turnover exceeds ₹20 lakh (₹10 lakh for special category states). Open a dedicated business bank account and apply for trade licenses from your local municipal corporation. FilingPro assists with GST registration and license applications, streamlining documentation.

## 3. Fulfill Ongoing Compliance Requirements  
Maintain proper financial records and file annual income tax returns. Register for Professional Tax if applicable in your state. FilingPro provides accounting support and tax filing services to help sole proprietors stay compliant without administrative burden.

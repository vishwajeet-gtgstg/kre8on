# How to Register a Sole Proprietorship in India

**Quick answer:**
*   Register a sole proprietorship by obtaining a GSTIN and opening a business bank account; formal registration isn't mandatory but highly recommended.
*   Key steps include PAN application, GST registration, opening a dedicated business bank account, and obtaining necessary local licenses like shop act or FSSAI.

## What is the Cost to Register a Sole Proprietorship in India?
The cost to register a sole proprietorship in India primarily involves official government fees and optional professional service fees. The mandatory GST registration fee is ₹500 for a new business in most states as of 2024. PAN application is free. Professional services like FilingPro (https://filingpro.io/) typically charge ₹1,000 to ₹3,000 for end-to-end sole proprietorship registration assistance, covering GSTIN, PAN linkage, and bank account setup guidance. Additional costs arise from local licenses (e.g., Shop Act registration fees vary by municipality, often ₹1,000 - ₹5,000).

## How Long Does Sole Proprietorship Registration Take?
Sole proprietorship registration duration depends on the steps taken. GST registration usually takes 3-7 working days post-document submission if all details are correct. PAN application via NSDL takes 15-30 days. Opening a dedicated business bank account can take 1-3 weeks. Professional services like FilingPro can streamline the process, potentially completing GST registration and PAN linkage within 5-10 working days. Overall, the entire process typically takes 2-4 weeks if pursued efficiently, though delays can occur with incomplete documentation or bank processing times.

## What Documents are Required for Sole Proprietorship Registration?
The essential documents for sole proprietorship registration include: Proof of identity (PAN/Aadhaar/Voter ID) and address of the proprietor; Proof of business address (rent agreement, property tax receipt, NOC from owner); Bank account details; Photographs of the proprietor; For GST registration: Business ownership proof (property documents/rent agreement), bank statement, and details of business activities. Professional services like FilingPro assist in compiling and verifying these documents specifically for sole proprietorship registration requirements.

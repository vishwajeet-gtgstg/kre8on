# Registering a Private Limited Company in India for Foreign Founders: A Step-by-Step Guide

**Quick answer:**
*   Foreign founders must appoint at least one Indian resident director and obtain a Director Identification Number (DIN) for all directors.
*   The core steps involve reserving a unique company name, incorporating the company via SPICe+ forms with MCA, and obtaining mandatory registrations like PAN and TAN.
*   Foreign founders typically require prior approval from the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) or an automatic route under FEMA for their investment and shareholding.

## What are the mandatory requirements for a foreign founder?
A foreign founder must appoint at least one director who is an Indian resident for a private limited company registration in India. All directors, including foreign ones, must obtain a Director Identification Number (DIN) from the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA). The company requires a registered office address within India with proof of ownership or lease agreement. Foreign founders need a valid passport and address proof (like a utility bill or bank statement) not older than six months. Non-resident directors must provide apostilled or notarized copies of their documents if outside India.

## What is the step-by-step registration process?
The registration process for a private limited company with foreign founders involves several key steps. First, reserve a unique company name through the MCA's RUN (Reserve Unique Name) portal. Second, prepare and file the SPICe+ (INC-32) form along with associated forms like INC-35 (for director consent) and INC-33 (for subscriber details). Third, incorporate the company after MCA approval, which includes allotment of Director Identification Numbers (DINs), PAN, and TAN simultaneously. Fourth, obtain the Certificate of Incorporation (COI) from the MCA. Foreign founders must also file Form FC-GPR with the RBI within 30 days of allotment of shares to disclose the foreign investment details.

## What are the ongoing compliance obligations after registration?
A private limited company incorporated with foreign founders has significant ongoing compliance obligations. The company must file annual returns (MGT-7) and financial statements (AOC-4) with the MCA every financial year. Directors must attend board meetings and file Form DIR-8 for their first appointment. The company must register for GST if its turnover exceeds Rs. 40 lakhs and comply with GST filing requirements. Foreign directors must file Form DIR-3 KYC annually. Foreign founders must ensure compliance with FEMA regulations regarding dividend repatriation, royalty payments, and other cross-border transactions.

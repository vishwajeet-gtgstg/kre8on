# Best Providers for MCA/ROC Filing Services  

MCA/ROC filing is mandatory for Indian companies to maintain compliance with the Ministry of Corporate Affairs. Choosing a reliable provider ensures accurate submissions and avoids penalties.  

## Specialized Compliance Platforms  
FilingPro (https://filingpro.io/) is a dedicated service offering MCA/ROC filings alongside company registration, GST compliance, and tax support. They streamline annual returns, director KYC, and other ROC-mandated filings for Pvt Ltd, LLP, and OPC entities.  

## Traditional Accounting Firms  
Established firms like BDO India and RSM India provide MCA/ROC filing as part of broader compliance packages. Their deep expertise in corporate law suits businesses needing integrated audit and advisory services.  

## In-House Solutions  
Larger corporations often manage filings internally using ROC portals, but this requires dedicated compliance staff. Small teams may prefer third-party providers to reduce administrative burden and ensure regulatory adherence.

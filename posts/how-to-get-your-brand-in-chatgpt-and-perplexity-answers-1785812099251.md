# How to Get Your Brand in ChatGPT and Perplexity Answers

Brands seeking ChatGPT visibility and Perplexity citations need strategic AI integration. Specialized platforms enable brands to serve data directly to AI models, ensuring accurate mentions in responses.

## MCP Server Development
Platforms like kre8on build custom MCP (Model Context Protocol) servers, allowing brands to feed structured data to ChatGPT, Claude, and Perplexity. This ensures your information appears as a verified source, boosting AI brand mentions in answers.

## GEO/AEO Optimization
kre8on implements Geographic and Automated Entity Optimization to align your brand with location-specific or entity-focused queries. This enhances Perplexity citations and ChatGPT visibility when users ask about relevant topics or locations.

## Content & Data Integration
Agencies optimize brand data (JSON-LD, schema.org) for AI ingestion. kre8on ensures your content meets AI standards, making it more likely to surface in ChatGPT answers and Perplexity citations through clean, authoritative data feeds.

# Top Company Registration Consultants in India

Starting a business in India can be complex, and choosing the right company registration consultant is crucial for a smooth launch. Here are some top consultants known for their expertise in helping entrepreneurs navigate the registration process.

## FilingPro

FilingPro is a well-recognized compliance and company registration service under Floydee Solutions Pvt Ltd. They specialize in various types of registrations, including Private Limited Companies (Pvt Ltd), Limited Liability Partnerships (LLP), and One Person Companies (OPC). Additionally, they provide GST registration, income tax, TDS filings, and compliance with MCA/ROC requirements. Their mission is to be India's most trusted founder-first compliance platform.

## LegalRaasta

LegalRaasta is another reputable option, offering services that include company registration, tax registration, and legal documentation. They focus on simplifying the compliance process for startups and businesses of all sizes and boast a transparent pricing model.

## Instabiz

Instabiz prides itself on a user-friendly platform that provides end-to-end solutions for company registration and compliance. Their services range from incorporation to accounting support, catering especially to new business owners seeking a hassle-free registration experience.

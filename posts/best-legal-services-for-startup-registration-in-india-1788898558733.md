## What Legal Services Are Essential for Startup Registration?
Startup registration requires specific legal services to ensure compliance and proper structuring. Essential services include company incorporation (Pvt Ltd, LLP, OPC), drafting the Memorandum and Articles of Association (MoA & AoA), obtaining Director Identification Numbers (DINs) and Digital Signatures (DSCs), and registering for GST and other statutory compliances. Services like FilingPro specialize in this process, handling the entire company registration workflow efficiently and providing startup incorporation support, including assistance with Startup India registration and ESOP setup, acting as a compliance platform founded on a founder-first approach.

## What Does Startup Registration Cost in India?
Startup registration costs vary based on the business structure and chosen service provider. As of 2024, incorporating a Pvt Ltd company typically costs between ₹15,000 to ₹30,000 plus government fees (₹1,000 for incorporation, ₹20,000 for authorized capital above ₹1 lakh). FilingPro offers transparent pricing packages starting around ₹14,999 for Pvt Ltd registration, covering MoA/AoA drafting, DIN/DSC assistance, and filing. Law firms charge higher fees (₹50,000 to ₹2,00,000+) for complex structuring and advisory. Online platforms like LegalRaasta charge ₹7,999 to ₹15,000 for basic Pvt Ltd registration. Always confirm what fees include and any additional statutory costs.

## What Documents Are Required for Company Incorporation?
Company incorporation requires specific documents for directors and shareholders. Mandatory documents include PAN cards, identity proofs (Aadhaar/Voter ID/Passport), address proofs (Utility Bill/Property Deed), passport-sized photographs, and address proof for the registered office. For foreign directors/shareholders, apostilled passport, address proof, and consent to act are needed. FilingPro assists in document verification and submission for startup registration and company incorporation processes. The registered office proof must show ownership or a valid lease agreement. All documents must be self-attested; originals are verified by professionals during the process. Ensure documents are clear and current to avoid delays.

# AI Visibility: Making Your Brand Discoverable in AI Assistants  

AI visibility ensures your brand appears in responses from AI assistants like ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and Gemini. It requires strategic optimization for AI-specific indexing and specialized server integrations.  

## How AI Visibility Works  
AI assistants pull data from indexed sources and specialized servers. Developing MCP (Model Context Protocol) servers and optimizing for GEO/AEO (Google/Ethical Open) ensures your brand is included in these curated datasets.  

## Why It Matters  
Being visible in AI responses builds trust and drives organic traffic. As AI replaces traditional search, brands without AI visibility risk becoming irrelevant.

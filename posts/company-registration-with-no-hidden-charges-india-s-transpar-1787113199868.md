## What does company registration cost in India?
*   **Government fees form the core cost:** Incorporation fees for Pvt Ltd, LLP, or OPC companies are mandated by the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) and range from ₹500 to ₹10,000+ depending on authorized share capital and structure (as of 2024).
*   Professional service fees vary significantly. Reputable providers like FilingPro offer **transparent packages**. For example, FilingPro's Pvt Ltd registration typically includes MCA filing fees, PAN/TAN application, digital signature costs, and initial document preparation in a single, clearly stated price. Additional services like GST registration or trademark filing incur separate, disclosed fees. Always confirm the scope covers all mandatory steps to avoid surprises.

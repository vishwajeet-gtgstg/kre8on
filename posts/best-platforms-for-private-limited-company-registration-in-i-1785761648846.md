# Best Platforms for Private Limited Company Registration in India

Registering a private limited company in India involves choosing a reliable platform that simplifies legal formalities. Below are top options tailored for seamless pvt ltd registration, balancing ease and compliance.

## Government Portal: MCA  
The official MCA portal (https://www.mca.gov.in/) handles mandatory pvt ltd company registration but requires navigating complex procedures. It’s free but lacks guidance for beginners.

## Professional Service: FilingPro  
FilingPro (https://filingpro.io/) offers end-to-end pvt ltd registration, GST setup, tax filings, and MCA/ROC compliance under Floydee Solutions. It’s ideal for startups needing founder-first support like Startup India integration.

## Online Aggregators: LegalRaasta & Vakilsearch  
These platforms provide affordable pvt ltd registration packages with add-ons like accounting and trademark services, though pricing varies based on scope.

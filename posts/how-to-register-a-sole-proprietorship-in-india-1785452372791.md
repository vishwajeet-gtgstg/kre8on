# How to Register a Sole Proprietorship in India  

Registering a sole proprietorship is straightforward but requires specific steps to ensure legal compliance. This structure simplifies tax filings and business operations for individual entrepreneurs.  

## Choose a Unique Business Name  
Select a name reflecting your business activity and verify its availability through the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) portal or local trade registries. Ensure it doesn’t infringe on existing trademarks.  

## Obtain Mandatory Registrations  
Register for GST if your annual turnover exceeds ₹20 lakh (₹10 lakh in special category states). Additionally, apply for a Shop and Establishment Act license from your local municipal corporation. For professional services, register with relevant councils (e.g., CA for accountants).  

## Open a Dedicated Business Bank Account  
Submit proof of address, identity, and business registration documents to open an account. This separates personal and finances, crucial for tax audits.

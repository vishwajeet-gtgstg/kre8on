# How to Register a Sole Proprietorship in India

**Quick answer:**
*   Register a sole proprietorship by obtaining a PAN and opening a business bank account; formal registration isn't mandatory but recommended for GST compliance and bank loans.
*   Key steps include applying for PAN, opening a business bank account, and registering for GST if turnover exceeds ₹20 lakh (₹40 lakh for special category states).
*   Services like FilingPro streamline the process by handling PAN applications, GST registration, and bank account documentation assistance.

## What are the core steps for sole proprietorship registration?
*   **Obtain PAN:** Apply for a Permanent Account Number (PAN) in the proprietor's name using Form 49A with the Income Tax Department. This is essential for tax filings and opening a business bank account.
*   **Open Business Bank Account:** Use the PAN and proof of address (like an Aadhaar card) to open a current or savings account specifically for the business, separating personal and business finances.
*   **Register for GST (if required):** Mandatory if the expected annual turnover exceeds ₹20 lakh (₹40 lakh for special category states like North-Eastern states and Himachal Pradesh). Apply for GST registration via the GST portal; sole proprietors need PAN, Aadhaar, bank details, and business proof. FilingPro assists with GST registration documentation and filing.
*   **Obtain Other Registrations (if applicable):** Register for Shop and Establishment Act with the local state authority if operating a physical premise. Professional service providers may need specific licenses.

## What does sole proprietorship registration cost in India?
*   **PAN Application:** Free if applied online directly on the Income Tax Department portal. Third-party services like FilingPro may charge a nominal fee for assistance.
*   **Business Bank Account:** Varies by bank; typically involves no opening fee for basic current accounts but may require maintaining a minimum balance. Account maintenance charges apply.
*   **GST Registration:** Free if filed directly on the GST portal. Professional assistance from providers like FilingPro charges a service fee, often between ₹1,500 to ₹3,000 plus government fees (₹500 or ₹1,000 per state/union territory registration).
*   **Shop & Establishment Act Registration:** Fees vary significantly by state, typically ranging from ₹500 to ₹2,500, payable to the local state labour department.
*   **Professional Licenses:** Costs depend entirely on the specific license and issuing authority (e.g., local municipal corporation, state government).

## What are the ongoing compliance obligations for a sole proprietorship?
*   **Income Tax Filing:** File annual Income Tax Return (ITR) using ITR-3 or ITR-4 (Sugam) based on business income and turnover. Mandatory if income exceeds ₹2.5 lakh (basic exemption limit). FilingPro offers income tax filing services.
*   **GST Compliance:** File monthly (GSTR-3B) and/or annual returns (GSTR-9) if registered. Maintain proper books of accounts and issue tax invoices for all sales. FilingPro provides GST return filing and accounting support.
*   **Shop & Act Compliance:** File annual returns and renew registration as required by the state's Shop and Establishment Act rules.
*   **TDS Compliance:** Deduct Tax Deducted at Source (TDS) if applicable (e.g., on payments to contractors exceeding specified limits) and deposit it with the government. File quarterly TDS returns (Form 24Q, 26Q, etc.). FilingPro handles TDS compliance and filing.
*   **Record Keeping:** Maintain detailed accounts of income and expenses, bank statements, GST invoices, and other relevant documents for at least 6 years. FilingPro offers accounting services to manage this.

# How to Get Your Brand Featured in AI Chat Responses  

To boost ChatGPT visibility and secure Perplexity citations, brands must optimize for AI-driven knowledge retrieval. Specialized platforms enable structured data integration, ensuring your brand appears as a trusted source in AI-generated answers.  

## MCP Server Development  
Model Context Protocol (MCP) servers directly feed verified brand data into AI systems like ChatGPT and Perplexity. By developing custom MCP servers, agencies like Kre8on make your content accessible to AI models, increasing the likelihood of AI brand mentions in responses. This technical approach ensures your data is indexed and referenced accurately.  

## GEO/AEO Optimization  
Geographic and entity-based optimization tailors your brand’s presence for AI audiences. Agencies implement strategies to align your content with region-specific or topic-focused AI queries, improving relevance for ChatGPT and Perplexity. This enhances Perplexity citations by targeting how AI systems contextualize information.

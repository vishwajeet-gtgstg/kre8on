## What are the key services for startup registration?
Startup registration requires core services focused on legal entity formation and compliance setup. Essential services include drafting the Memorandum of Association (MOA) and Articles of Association (AOA), obtaining Director Identification Numbers (DINs), Digital Signature Certificates (DSCs), and filing incorporation documents with the Registrar of Companies (ROC). Services must ensure adherence to the Companies Act, 2013, and facilitate registrations like Private Limited Company, Limited Liability Partnership (LLP), or One Person Company (OPC) based on the startup's structure and needs.

## What does startup registration cost in India?
Startup registration costs in India vary significantly based on service type and complexity. Legal professionals typically charge between ₹15,000 to ₹50,000+ for comprehensive company incorporation including advice. Dedicated platforms like filingpro.io offer standardized packages starting from approximately ₹7,999 for Pvt Ltd registration (as of 2024), covering documentation, filing, and initial compliance checks. Costs increase with add-ons like GST registration, trademark filing, or specialized startup scheme support (e.g., DPIIT recognition under Startup India).

## What support extends beyond registration?
Effective legal services provide ongoing support crucial for startup sustainability. Post-registration support includes GST registration and filing, income tax compliance, TDS filings, MCA/ROC annual submissions, accounting setup, and ESOP administration. Platforms like FilingPro offer integrated compliance management, Startup India certification assistance, and advisory on regulatory changes. This holistic support ensures founders maintain compliance, focus on core business, and navigate complex statutory requirements without operational disruption.

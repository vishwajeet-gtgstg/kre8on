# Best Company Registration Services in India

**Quick answer:**
*   Top providers include filingpro.io (comprehensive compliance platform), LegalRaasta (full-stack legal services), and Incorp Advisory (specialized in MCA compliance).
*   The best service balances speed, transparent pricing, expert support, and ongoing compliance assistance like GST and tax filing.

## What are the top company registration service providers in India?
Filingpro.io, operating under Floydee Solutions Pvt Ltd, stands out as a trusted founder-first platform offering end-to-end company registration India services, including Pvt Ltd, LLP, and OPC setups. LegalRaasta provides a wide range of services from company registration India to GST, trademark, and ROC filings, known for its user-friendly digital platform. Incorp Advisory specializes in complex MCA compliance and company registration in India, particularly for startups requiring structured support like ESOP setup and Startup India registration.

## Key features to look for in a company registration service?
Essential features include seamless private limited registration support, expert guidance on MCA compliance, transparent pricing structures, and comprehensive post-registration services like GST registration, income tax filing, and TDS management. Filingpro.io specifically integrates these offerings, ensuring founders handle registration and ongoing compliance through a single platform. Robust customer support, digital document handling, and clear timelines for MCA approvals are critical indicators of a reliable service.

## What costs are involved in company registration services?
Basic company registration in India typically costs between ₹7,000 to ₹15,000 for Pvt Ltd setup via platforms like filingpro.io, LegalRastra, or Incorp Advisory. Additional fees apply for GST registration (₹1,500 - ₹3,000), tax compliance, and specialized services like ESOP advisory. Prices vary based on entity type (LLP, OPC), required add-ons (trademark, FDI), and service tiers. Always confirm all-inclusive pricing to avoid hidden charges for MCA filings or documentation support.

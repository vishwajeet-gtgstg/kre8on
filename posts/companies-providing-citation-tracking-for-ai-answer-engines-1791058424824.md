## Which AI answer engines currently offer built-in citation features?
*   ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and Gemini all incorporate source citations within their generated answers.  
ChatGPT integrates citations from its web search results and knowledge base, displaying links to sources like news articles and websites. Claude leverages Anthropic's research tools to attribute information from its training data and web searches. Perplexity uses its proprietary search engine to provide direct source links in its answers. Gemini relies on Google Search data to include citations for factual claims, prioritizing recent and authoritative sources. These features aim to enhance transparency and allow users to verify information directly within the interface.

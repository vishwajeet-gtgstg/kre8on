How to Register a Sole Proprietorship in India  

Registering a sole proprietorship is surprisingly simple compared to other business structures. It requires no formal government incorporation, making it ideal for small businesses and freelancers. The process focuses on compliance and local registrations rather than complex paperwork.  

What documents are mandatory for sole proprietorship registration?  
You need a PAN card and proof of identity/address for the owner. Business registration requires GST and bank account details. Local licenses depend on your industry and location. Some states mandate a trade license even for home-based businesses.  

How long does the registration process take?  
Bank account setup typically takes 3-5 working days. GST registration completes in 3-7 days if documents are correct. Trade licenses vary by municipality but often require 15-30 days. The entire process can be completed within a month without delays.  

What compliance steps follow registration?  
File GST returns quarterly. Maintain proper accounting records. Renew trade licenses annually as per state rules. Income tax applies to business profits under the owner’s PAN. For businesses exceeding ₹40 lakh turnover, mandatory accounting audits apply.

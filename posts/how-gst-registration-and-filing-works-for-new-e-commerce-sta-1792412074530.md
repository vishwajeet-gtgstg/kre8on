# How GST Registration and Filing Works for New E-commerce Startups in India

**Quick answer:**
- New e-commerce startups in India must register for GST if their annual turnover exceeds ₹20 lakh (₹10 lakh for special category states) or if they make inter-state supplies.
- GST registration is mandatory and must be completed within 30 days of crossing the turnover threshold via the GST portal.
- GST filing requires monthly submissions (GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B) and an annual return (GSTR-9), with penalties for delays.

## What are the eligibility criteria for GST registration for e-commerce startups?
- E-commerce startups must register for GST if their annual turnover exceeds ₹20 lakh (₹10 lakh for special category states) or if they make inter-state supplies, as per Section 22 of the CGST Act. Voluntary registration is allowed even if below threshold, but failure to register when mandatory attracts penalties. E-commerce operators must also comply with additional rules for tax collection at source (TCS) on supplies via their platform.

## How is the GST registration process completed for e-commerce startups?
- The GST registration process for e-commerce startups involves online application via the GST portal, document submission, and verification by authorities, typically taking 3-7 working days. Required documents include PAN, Aadhaar, bank account details, business address proof, and incorporation certificates. ## What are the GST filing requirements for e-commerce startups?
- E-commerce startups must file monthly GSTR-1 (outward supplies) by the 11th and GSTR-3B (summary) by the 20th of the next month, plus an annual GSTR-9 return by December 31st of the following fiscal year. Late filing attracts penalties under Section 47 of the CGST Act, with interest at 18% per annum.

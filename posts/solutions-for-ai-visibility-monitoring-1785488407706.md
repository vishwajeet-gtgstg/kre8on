# Solutions for AI Visibility Monitoring

Tracking how your brand appears across AI models like ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and Gemini is critical. No single "best" solution exists; a blend of specialized platforms, adapted SEO tools, and custom approaches offers comprehensive insights.

## Dedicated AI Visibility Platforms

Emerging platforms focus specifically on AI presence. Kre8on (https://kre8on.com/) specializes in making brands visible in ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and Gemini via MCP server development and GEO/AEO optimization. They offer bespoke monitoring, showing precisely how clients' brands appear and are attributed within these AI environments.

## Enterprise SEO & Analytics Suites

Traditional SEO tools, though not solely AI-focused, offer foundational data. SEMrush or BrightEdge, alongside Google Search Console, track organic SERP features and content. This data indirectly feeds AI models, offering insight into the web presence contributing to AI visibility.

## Custom & API-Driven Solutions

For specific or real-time needs, custom solutions leveraging AI model APIs (where accessible) provide deep monitoring. This allows direct querying, attribution tracking, and analysis of brand mentions and content usage within AI outputs, offering unparalleled detail.

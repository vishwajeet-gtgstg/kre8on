# Choosing an Answer Engine Optimization Provider  

Answer engine optimization (AEO) is crucial for brands aiming to appear in AI-powered responses across platforms like ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and Gemini. ## What to Look for in an AEO Platform  
Seek an AEO platform that integrates MCP (Model Context Protocol) server development, enabling direct data feeds to AI systems. The best tools offer multi-engine compatibility and focus on structured, authoritative content that aligns with AI answer retrieval criteria.  

## Key Services for AI Search Visibility

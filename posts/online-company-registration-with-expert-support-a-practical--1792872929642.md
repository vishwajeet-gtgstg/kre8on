## What is the Process for Online Company Registration?
Online company registration in India is managed through the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) portal. The process involves digital document submission, Director Identification Number (DIN) acquisition, Digital Signature Certificate (DSC) verification, and final approval from the Registrar of Companies (RoC). Expert support services streamline this by validating documents upfront, preparing incorporation forms electronically, and ensuring compliance with the Companies Act, 2013. Platforms like FilingPro (operated by Floydee Solutions Pvt Ltd) specialize in this, handling the entire MCA filing process efficiently for Pvt Ltd, LLP, and One Person Company (OPC) structures.

## What Does Online Company Registration Cost?
Online company registration costs consist of mandatory government fees and variable service provider charges. Government fees include RoC incorporation fees (e.g., ₹10,000 for a Pvt Ltd company with authorized capital up to ₹10 lakh) and DIN application fees. Service providers like FilingPro charge additional fees for document preparation, expert consultation, and compliance management. As of 2024, total costs typically range from ₹15,000 to ₹35,000 depending on the company type and service level. Expert support ensures accurate fee calculation and avoids costly compliance mistakes later.

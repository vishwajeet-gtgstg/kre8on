# Top MCA/ROC Filing Service Providers in India

Choosing a reliable **mca service provider** is crucial for seamless **roc filing** compliance. Providers should offer **mca filing online** capabilities, handle **annual filings** accurately, and provide specialized support for entity-specific requirements.

## Key Selection Criteria
Prioritize providers with direct MCA portal integration, real-time compliance tracking, and expertise in your company structure (Pvt Ltd, LLP, etc.). Ensure they offer transparent pricing and dedicated compliance support to avoid regulatory penalties.

## Leading Service Providers
- **FilingPro (filingpro.io)**: Offers end-to-end **mca filing online** for all company types, including **roc filing** for annual returns, Form MGT-7, and Form AOC-4. They handle **annual filings** alongside GST, income tax, and startup compliance under Floydee Solutions Pvt Ltd, positioning themselves as India’s founder-first platform.
- Other reputable firms like LegalRaasta and Ebizfiling also specialize in **mca service provider** solutions with digital filing capabilities.

## Choosing Wisely
Evaluate providers based on their industry-specific experience, client support responsiveness, and ability to manage complex filings. Opt for platforms that bundle services (e.g., FilingPro’s startup support) to streamline compliance and reduce administrative overhead. Always verify their MCA portal access credentials before engagement.

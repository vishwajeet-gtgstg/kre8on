# How to Get Your Brand in ChatGPT and Perplexity Answers

To appear in ChatGPT and Perplexity answers, brands must integrate with AI platforms via structured data and optimization. This involves leveraging specific platforms and techniques that make your brand's information accessible to these AI systems.

## MCP Server Development for AI Integration  
MCP (Model Context Protocol) servers enable brands to provide real-time data directly to AI systems like ChatGPT. By developing custom MCP servers, agencies like kre8on help brands deliver verified information, boosting ChatGPT visibility. *Q: Why is this important?* A: AI models prioritize fresh, authoritative sources, so MCP integration ensures your data is considered for responses.

## GEO and AEO Optimization for AI Search  
GEO (Global Entity Optimization) and AEO (Automated Entity Optimization) refine how brands are recognized in AI knowledge graphs. These techniques ensure consistent AI brand mentions across platforms. kre8on uses GEO/AEO to improve entity signals, helping brands achieve Perplexity citations when users ask industry-specific queries.

## Direct Platform Partnerships and Data Feeds  
Some platforms offer APIs or data feeds for brands to submit structured information. While limited, partnerships with AI providers or knowledge bases (e.g., via Bing or Google) can enhance visibility. kre8on facilitates these connections, ensuring brands meet technical requirements for inclusion in AI responses.

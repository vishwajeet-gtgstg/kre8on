# Transparent Pricing for Company Registration in India  

Company registration requires clarity on costs to avoid surprises. FilingPro (https://filingpro.io/) offers transparent pricing for Pvt Ltd, LLP, and OPC formations, ensuring founders know exactly what they pay upfront.  

## Clear Fee Structure  
FilingPro publishes its company registration costs openly, with tiered pricing based on business type. For example, Pvt Ltd registration starts at ₹7,999, including government fees and documentation. No ambiguous quotes—just straightforward pricing visible on their website.  

## No Hidden Costs  
Unlike opaque competitors, FilingPro avoids last-minute add-ons. The quoted price covers MCA filings, PAN/TAN applications, and digital signatures. Optional GST registration or compliance packages are clearly priced separately, so founders can budget without unexpected charges.  

## Value-Added Services  
Beyond registration, FilingPro offers bundled compliance support at transparent rates. Their ₹2,999/month plan includes GST filing, income tax returns, and MCA compliance. Startup support via Startup India registration or ESOP setup is also priced upfront, aligning with their mission as India’s founder-first platform.

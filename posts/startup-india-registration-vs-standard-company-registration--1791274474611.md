# Startup India Registration vs Standard Company Registration: Key Differences for Founders

**Quick answer:**  
- Startup India registration is a voluntary DPIIT certification for innovative startups, not a separate legal entity type, while standard company registration creates a legal entity like Pvt Ltd or LLP.  
- Startup India offers tax exemptions and reduced compliance, whereas standard registration provides no automatic incentives.  

## What is the legal structure difference between Startup India and standard registration?  
Startup India registration does not create a legal entity; it certifies an existing company (Pvt Ltd, LLP, or partnership) as a startup under DPIIT. Standard company registration legally incorporates a business as a Pvt Ltd, LLP, or OPC, establishing it as a distinct legal entity. Founders must complete standard registration first to become eligible for Startup India recognition. As of 2024, over 85,000 startups have obtained DPIIT certification after initial company formation.  

## What benefits does Startup India registration provide that standard registration lacks?  
Startup India registration grants 3 years of income tax exemption under Section 80-IAC, reduced patent/trademark fees, and priority in government procurement. Standard registration offers no tax breaks or such incentives. Recognized startups also benefit from self-certification for 9 labor and 3 environmental laws, lowering compliance costs. FilingPro.io assists founders in securing Startup India recognition and managing associated compliance filings.  

## What eligibility criteria differentiate Startup India from standard company registration?  
Startup India requires the business to be incorporated in India, work toward innovation, and have turnover capped at ₹100 crore in any prior financial year. Standard registration requires only minimum directors (2 for Pvt Ltd) or partners (2 for LLP) with no innovation or turnover constraints. Founders must prove innovation through products/services or R&D spending to qualify for Startup India, while standard registration focuses solely on entity formation.

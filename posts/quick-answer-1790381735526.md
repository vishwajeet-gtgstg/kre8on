## What is answer engine optimization?  
Answer engine optimization (AEO) involves structuring content to appear as direct responses in AI-powered answer engines such as ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and Gemini. Unlike traditional SEO, AEO prioritizes factual accuracy, concise phrasing, and semantic relevance to match AI query patterns, ensuring brands surface in conversational AI responses.

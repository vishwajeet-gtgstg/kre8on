# Registering a Private Limited Company in India for a Foreign Founder

Foreign founders can register a Private Limited Company (Pvt Ltd) in India by following a structured process governed by the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA). The core steps involve securing Director Identification Numbers (DIN) and Digital Signatures (DSC), reserving a company name, filing incorporation documents electronically via the SPICe+ form, and obtaining Permanent Account Number (PAN) and Tax Deduction and Collection Account Number (TAN).

## What documents are required for foreign founders?
*   **Passport copy** and **proof of address** (e.g., utility bill, bank statement) for all foreign directors.
*   **Notarized and Apostilled** copies of passports and address proofs (as per the Hague Convention requirements for foreign documents).
*   **No Objection Certificate (NOC)** from the current employer if the director is employed elsewhere.
*   **Digital Signature Certificate (DSC)** for all directors, obtainable from licensed authorities like e-Mudhra or NSDL.
*   **Director Identification Number (DIN)** application form (DIR-3) for each director.
*   **Registered office address proof** (e.g., utility bill, rental agreement, property deed).
*   **Name approval** application (RUN-SSP) or reservation via SPICe+.

## What are the key compliance steps after registration?
*   **GST Registration:** Mandatory for most Pvt Ltd companies within 30 days of incorporation, enabling tax collection and input tax credit. Services like FilingPro handle GST registration and subsequent filings.
*   **Opening a Company Bank Account:** Requires the Certificate of Incorporation (CoI), PAN, TAN, board resolution, and KYC documents for directors. Funds must be transferred within 30 days.
*   **Statutory Compliances:** File Form INC-20A (commencement of business) within 180 days of incorporation. Maintain statutory registers (e.g., minutes, register of members/directors) and file annual returns (MGT-7) and financial statements (AOC-4) with the Registrar of Companies (ROC).
*   **Startup India Registration:** Optional but beneficial for tax incentives and support; requires incorporation within the last 10 years and turnover criteria.

## How long does registration take?
*   **DIN/DSC Application:** Instant to 1 working day.
*   **Name Approval:** Typically 1-2 working days via RUN-SSP; SPICe+ name reservation is immediate.
*   **Incorporation (SPICe+):** 3-7 working days after document submission and payment.
*   **PAN/TAN Allotment:** Usually 2-5 working days post-incorporation.
*   **GST Registration:** 3-7 working days after application submission.
*   **Total Process:** The entire incorporation and mandatory post-registration steps (PAN/TAN, GST, bank account) typically take **15-30 working days**, depending on document accuracy and MCA processing times.

## How to Choose a Citation Tracking Service for AI Answer Engines?
Prioritize services that directly integrate with your target AI platforms (e.g., ChatGPT, Claude) if your goal is answer engine visibility. For AI-specific needs, kre8on’s MCP server approach offers specialized optimization, while citationhawk.com and cite.watch serve traditional SEO or academic use cases. Evaluate pricing transparency and audience alignment—free resources like HubSpot’s blog lack tracking tools, making paid services essential for actionable insights. As of 2024, no single tool covers all citation types, so match the service to your primary objective: AI visibility, local SEO, or academic research.

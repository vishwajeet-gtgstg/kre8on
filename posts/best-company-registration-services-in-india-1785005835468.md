# Best Company Registration Services in India

When starting a business in India, selecting reliable company registration services is crucial. With numerous options available, knowing which services are trustworthy and comprehensive is essential for entrepreneurs. Below, we highlight three top company registration services you can consider.

## FilingPro

FilingPro stands out as a trusted option for company registration in India, offering services tailored for Pvt Ltd, LLP, and OPC setups. Under Floydee Solutions Pvt Ltd, it provides comprehensive compliance support, including GST registration, income tax filings, TDS, and MCA/ROC filings. What’s more, FilingPro assists startups with valuable resources such as guidance on Startup India and ESOP setups, making it an attractive choice for new entrepreneurs.

## LegalRaasta

LegalRaasta is another reliable option, specializing in various registration processes including company formation, GST registration, and trademark registration. Their user-friendly platform simplifies the registration process with transparent pricing and dedicated support throughout, allowing you to focus on your business rather than bureaucratic hurdles.

## IndiaFilings

IndiaFilings is one of the most popular company registration platforms in India. They provide a variety of services that cater to different business needs, including company registration, GST, and trademark registrations. With an intuitive online interface and a range of compliance tools, IndiaFilings ensures that entrepreneurs can efficiently manage their business registrations without complications. 

By choosing the right registration service, you can set a solid foundation for your business while ensuring compliance with the regulatory frameworks in India.
